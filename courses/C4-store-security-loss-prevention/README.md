**Track:** Foundations

**8 articles, 6-8 learner hours**

## Terminal outcomes

The learner can:
- Execute the two-person opening/closing sequence correctly, including the all-clear signal and key segregation
- Explain why nightly put-away discipline and safe specification matter, and follow the store's in-safe warranty requirements
- Apply anti-switch and one-item-at-a-time selling-floor protocols when showing high-value merchandise
- Recognize the transaction and behavioral red flags associated with distraction theft, grab-and-run, and armed robbery patterns
- Apply card-present, check, and remote-order fraud controls, and know when to escalate a suspicious transaction
- Correctly identify when a transaction triggers Form 8300 and follow the required reporting and non-disclosure steps
- Participate in a perpetual-inventory and cycle-count program, and follow the correct discrepancy protocol before declaring a loss
- Explain segregation-of-duties principles and contribute to a store-wide security culture (crime bulletins, suspicious-incident logging, training log sign-off)

**Assessment:** Observed opening/closing walkthrough; a manager-scored review of a simulated high-value transaction and a simulated inventory discrepancy.
**KPI link:** shrinkage rate, inventory-count accuracy, loss-prevention incident rate, chargeback/fraud-loss rate, insurance-compliance audit pass rate.
