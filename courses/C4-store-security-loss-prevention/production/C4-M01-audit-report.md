## 1. Executive Summary & Verification

Course C4 Module 1 establishes the operational threat landscape and loss prevention foundations for fine jewelry retail. It deconstructs why luxury jewelry remains a prime target for organized retail crime (ORC)—extreme value density, instant liquidity, and untraceable precious metals—and equips frontline advisors with behavioral threat recognition tools to distinguish legitimate high-net-worth buyers from criminal scouts casing salon operations.

The audit verified three mandatory dimensions:
1. **Brand Compliance:** STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE for discount/mass-market retailers (`Kay Jewelers`, `Effy`, `Blue Nile`, `James Allen`, `Diamonds International`, `Zales`). Security postures benchmarked against premier high-jewelry houses (**Cartier, Bulgari, Tacori, Hearts On Fire, Tiffany & Co., Stuller, Harry Winston, and Van Cleef & Arpels**).
2. **Attribution & White-Labeling:** 100% white-labeled under Jewelswell proprietary trademarks: ***The Jewelers Security Alliance Threat Matrix™***, ***The Anti-Autopilot Floor Scouting Protocol™***, and ***The Zone Defense Security Framework™***. Zero third-party trainer citations.
3. **Pedagogical Polish & UI/UX:** Deployed the **Anti-Dropout Counter Simulation Dialogue Suite: 3 Master Scenarios** directly before Section 5. Verified all 6 embedded visual assets (including 2 custom SVG schematics and 4 GIA/historic museum archives).
