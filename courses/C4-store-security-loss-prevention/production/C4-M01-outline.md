# C4-M01 Outline: Opening and Closing: The Two-Person Rule and Alarm Response

## Module Metadata
- Course: C4 - Store Security & Loss Prevention
- Module: 1 of 8
- Persona: P1
- Working title: Opening and Closing: The Two-Person Rule and Alarm Response
- Learning objective: Associates can execute the correct two-person opening/closing sequence, explain why key segregation and the all-clear signal exist, follow nightly put-away discipline, and respond correctly to an alarm signal.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Execute the two-person opening/closing sequence correctly, including the all-clear signal and key segregation."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — JSA 2025 crime data (1,233 crimes, ~$145M losses) and 2024 robbery-timing data motivate why opening/closing is codified rather than left to judgment.
2. **Why Opening and Closing Are the Most Dangerous Times** — JSA, JCK, and UK block-insurance sourcing converge on the ambush-risk rationale.
3. **The Two-Person Rule and the All-Clear Signal** — Jewelers Mutual's five-step sequence; JSA's safe-distance rule; UK duress-code/panic-alarm protocol.
4. **Key Segregation: Why the Same Person Should Never Hold Both Sets** — Structural rationale (compromising two people vs. one); end-of-day key handoff signal; combination/code changes on staff departure.
5. **The Opening Sequence: Merchandise Out, Doors Still Locked** — Correct order of operations; window-display closed-store rule.
6. **The Closing Sequence: Customers Out, Then Merchandise Away** — In-safe insurance warranty concept; ~70% overnight benchmark; no-cover rule with JCK's burglar-logic quote.
7. **Daily Physical Checks** — Window-frame inspection, showcase tamper inspection, Suspicious Incidents Book.
8. **Alarm Response Is a Defined Procedure, Not an Ad-Hoc Call-Out** — Never enter alone; investigate all entry points including neighboring premises; weekend-heavy burglary timing; independent callback verification.
9. **Safe Specification Is Part of the Operating System** — TL-15/TL-30 inadequacy vs. TRTL-30x6; placement rules (no outside/shared walls); rear gate discipline.
10. **How This Shows Up in Real Job Specifications** — Associate- and manager-level job-posting language tying this directly to the role.
11. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.
