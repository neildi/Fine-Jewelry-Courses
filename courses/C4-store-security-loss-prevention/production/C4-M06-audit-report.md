## 1. Executive Summary & Verification

Course C4 Module 6 arms fine jewelry sales and operations teams with defensive protocols against non-physical payment fraud: Card-Not-Present (CNP) chargebacks, synthetic identity theft, counterfeit cashier's checks, and fraudulent wire confirmation scams. The module establishes strict merchant-protection standards—mandating physical EMV chip insertion with matching photo ID for in-store pickups, enforcing 7-day settlement holds on paper checks, and establishing Fedwire bank transfers as the non-negotiable settlement standard for transactions exceeding $10,000.

The audit verified three mandatory dimensions:
1. **Brand Compliance:** STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE for discount/mass-market retailers (`Kay Jewelers`, `Effy`, `Blue Nile`, `James Allen`, `Diamonds International`, `Zales`). Payment protocols modeled on international luxury maisons (**Cartier, Bulgari, Tacori, Hearts On Fire, Tiffany & Co., Stuller, Harry Winston, and Van Cleef & Arpels**).
2. **Attribution & White-Labeling:** 100% white-labeled under Jewelswell proprietary trademarks: ***The Layered Remote-Order Defense Stack™***, ***The Verified Settlement Hold Protocol™***, and ***The Fedwire High-Ticket Standard™***. Zero third-party trainer citations.
3. **Pedagogical Polish & UI/UX:** Deployed the **Anti-Dropout Counter Simulation Dialogue Suite: 3 Master Scenarios** immediately preceding Section 4. Verified all 6 embedded visual assets (including 2 custom SVG schematics and 4 GIA/historic museum archives).
