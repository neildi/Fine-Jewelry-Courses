# C4-M02 Huddle Card: Safes, Vaults, and Nightly Put-Away Discipline

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** The in-safe warranty in your insurance policy is a contract, not a suggestion. If you're not compliant when a loss happens, the insurer can deny or reduce the claim — regardless of how the loss occurred.

## Three Things That Have to Work Together
1. **Safe rating** — TL-15/TL-30 are considered inadequate; TRTL-30x6 gives burglars far less working time.
2. **Put-away discipline** — whatever your policy's percentage/threshold is, treat it as a hard floor, not a target to approximate.
3. **Record-keeping** — annual itemized inventory, invoices, and cost records, retained 7 years, stored off-premises (not just in the safe with the merchandise).

## Not Everything in the Vault Is "Ours"
- Memo = vendor-owned. Consignment = customer-owned. Customer repairs = never store-owned.
- A non-invoiced item is a red flag to resolve, not a category to shrug off.
- Mixing these up during a count eventually means you either overstate your own value or understate what you owe someone else.

## Placement Matters Too
Safes should never sit against an outside wall or a wall shared with a neighbor — it gives a crew concealment and extra time.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
If we had a loss tonight, could we prove exactly what was in the safe and what it cost — right now, without scrambling?
