# C4-M05 Huddle Card: Card, Check, and Remote-Order Fraud Prevention

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** "Looks like it will clear" and "has actually cleared" are not the same thing. Card fraud, check fraud, and remote-order fraud all live in that gap.

## Card-Present: Don't Skip the Basics
Terminal "approved" isn't enough on its own. Get the imprint AND the signature — that's what determines whether the card network or the store eats a stolen-card loss.

## The One Question for Remote Orders
"Why would this person, in a distant city, buy from US specifically instead of a dozen jewelers closer to them?" No good answer + pressure to move fast = slow down.

## Five Red Flags That Cluster Together
Unknown customer, remote shipping address, rush delivery demand, a too-smooth cover story, pressure to complete now. One alone might be nothing. All five together is the pattern.

## Checks: Wait for Real Clearance
Calling the bank to "confirm funds" does NOT protect you — a confederate can pull the money right after that call. Wait for funds to actually land in the store's account.

## EMV Isn't a Cure-All
Chip cards protect in-person transactions. They do nothing for phone/email orders. And if a chip card gets processed on a non-EMV terminal, liability shifts to the store.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Has anyone taken a remote or high-value order recently that had more than one of these red flags? What did we do about it?
