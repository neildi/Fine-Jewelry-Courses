# C4-M05 Outline: Card, Check, and Remote-Order Fraud Prevention

## Module Metadata
- Course: C4 - Store Security & Loss Prevention
- Module: 5 of 8
- Persona: P2
- Working title: Card, Check, and Remote-Order Fraud Prevention
- Learning objective: Associates can apply card-present procedure correctly (imprint, signature), recognize the clustered risk factors in remote/phone/email orders, understand why "cleared funds" verification calls don't protect against check fraud, and know the layered card-verification stack including EMV liability shift.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Apply card-present, check, and remote-order fraud controls, and know when to escalate a suspicious transaction."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Bridges from Module 4's behavioral pattern recognition to transaction-engineered fraud; shared "gap between looks-cleared and actually-cleared" vulnerability.
2. **Card-Present Discipline: Why the Imprint and Signature Matter** — Liability rule for stolen-card-in-person loss; chip-era misconception correction.
3. **Remote and Phone/Email Orders: The Riskiest Transaction Type** — Six clustered risk factors; the "why buy from you specifically" diagnostic question.
4. **Checks and "Cleared Funds": Why Calling the Bank Isn't Enough** — Confederate-withdrawal mechanic; check-specific red flags (out-of-state, no printed address, low check number, post-dated, customer-supplied verification number).
5. **Layered Card Verification: No Single Control Is a Silver Bullet** — AVS/CVV/fraud-review/tokenization/P2P encryption/EMV stack; EMV liability-shift detail for non-EMV terminal processing.
6. **A Documented Sales-Order Process, Not Ad-Hoc Judgment** — NAJ SecurityWise-style process framing (required fields, internal fraud database, chargeback response).
7. **A Worked Scenario: The Remote Order That Doesn't Add Up** — Illustrative synthesis applying all risk-factor lists to one call.
8. **Practice Exercise** — Self-audit of a recent high-value/unusual transaction against the module's red-flag lists.
9. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.
