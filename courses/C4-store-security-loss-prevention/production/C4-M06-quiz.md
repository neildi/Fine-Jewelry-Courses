# C4-M06 Quiz: Large Cash Transactions and Form 8300 Compliance

**Instructions:** Answer all questions. This module's quiz emphasizes correct application of the filing threshold, statement rules, and the absolute non-disclosure rule.

---

**1. What cash amount triggers a Form 8300 filing requirement?**
A) Any cash transaction, regardless of amount
B) More than $10,000 in cash in one transaction, or in related transactions
C) Exactly $10,000, but not a penny more or less
D) Only transactions involving international customers

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Any trade or business receiving more than $10,000 in cash in one transaction, or in related transactions, must file Form 8300.

---

**2. A customer pays $4,000 cash in March, $4,000 cash in April, and $3,500 cash in May, all toward completing the purchase of the same piece on layaway. Does this trigger Form 8300?**
A) No, because no single payment exceeded $10,000
B) Yes, because the related/connected payments total $11,500, which is aggregated against the threshold
C) No, layaway payments are never counted toward this threshold
D) Only the final payment counts, so it does not trigger the requirement

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Related transactions connected to the same underlying purchase are aggregated for threshold purposes — splitting payments across visits does not avoid the filing requirement if the cumulative total exceeds $10,000.

---

**3. Which of the following can serve as the required written statement furnished to the customer, per this module?**
A) A copy of the actual filed Form 8300
B) An invoice, as long as it contains all the required elements (business name/address, contact info, total reportable cash, and IRS-disclosure language)
C) A verbal confirmation during the transaction
D) No statement is required beyond the IRS filing itself

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: An invoice can serve as the required statement if it contains all required elements — but this is distinct from sending the form itself, which should never be done.

---

**4. By what date must the written statement be furnished to each person named on Form 8300?**
A) Immediately at the time of the transaction
B) On or before January 31 of the following year
C) Within 24 hours of filing
D) There is no deadline for furnishing the statement

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The written statement must be furnished to each named person on or before January 31 of the year following the transaction.

---

**5. Why should a business NOT send a copy of the actual filed Form 8300 to the customer as their statement?**
A) It is against IRS policy to ever provide any documentation to customers
B) The form itself contains sensitive information that shouldn't be shared this way
C) It costs extra to send the actual form
D) There is no real reason; it's simply traditional practice

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Sending a copy of the actual Form 8300 as the statement is not advisable specifically because of the sensitive information the form contains — a properly constructed invoice or separate statement should be used instead.

---

**6. If the "suspicious transaction" box was checked on a filed Form 8300, what is the rule regarding the named parties?**
A) You should inform them so they can correct any misunderstanding
B) Under no circumstances should the parties be made aware that a suspicious-transaction filing was made
C) You must tell them within 30 days
D) There is no special rule in this case

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This is an absolute, hard rule — parties named on a suspicious-transaction filing must never be made aware of that filing, as doing so would defeat the purpose of the reporting mechanism and could create separate legal exposure.

---

**7. What does the IRS encourage businesses to do when a customer is unwilling to provide required information for Form 8300?**
A) Skip the filing entirely since complete information isn't available
B) Document their attempts to obtain the required information, even if unsuccessful
C) Refuse to complete the sale under any circumstances
D) File a different, unrelated form instead

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The IRS encourages businesses to document their attempts to obtain required information from customers, which demonstrates good-faith compliance effort even when a customer is uncooperative.

---

**8. Why does this module emphasize that Form 8300 compliance applies regardless of whether a transaction seems suspicious?**
A) Because the requirement only applies to suspicious transactions
B) Because it's a procedural legal filing requirement tied to a cash threshold, independent of personal suspicion about the transaction
C) Because suspicion always overrides the filing requirement
D) Because only large corporations need to comply

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Unlike the pattern-recognition content in Modules 4-5, Form 8300 compliance is a threshold-based legal requirement that applies to any qualifying cash transaction, whether or not anything about it seems suspicious.
