## 1. Executive Summary & Verification

Course C4 Module 2 addresses the two most vulnerable operational windows in retail fine jewelry: store opening and evening closing. The module establishes absolute compliance around the **Two-Person Rule**, 360-degree perimeter checks, electronic visual all-clear signaling, dual-custody safe combinations, time-lock delay protocols, and rolling vault cart concealment procedures during evening take-down.

The audit verified three mandatory dimensions:
1. **Brand Compliance:** STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE for discount/mass-market retailers (`Kay Jewelers`, `Effy`, `Blue Nile`, `James Allen`, `Diamonds International`, `Zales`). Opening and closing routines modeled on premier maison standards (**Cartier, Bulgari, Tacori, Hearts On Fire, Tiffany & Co., Stuller, Harry Winston, and Van Cleef & Arpels**).
2. **Attribution & White-Labeling:** 100% white-labeled under Jewelswell proprietary trademarks: ***The Two-Person Perimeter Sweep Protocol™***, ***The Dual-Custody Vault Covenant™***, and ***The 5-Step Closing Lockdown Sequence™***. Zero third-party trainer citations.
3. **Pedagogical Polish & UI/UX:** Deployed the **Anti-Dropout Counter Simulation Dialogue Suite: 3 Master Scenarios** directly before Section 4. Verified all 7 embedded visual assets (including 2 custom SVG schematics and 5 GIA/historic museum archives).
