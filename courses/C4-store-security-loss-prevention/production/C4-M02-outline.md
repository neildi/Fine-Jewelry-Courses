# C4-M02 Outline: Safes, Vaults, and Nightly Put-Away Discipline

## Module Metadata
- Course: C4 - Store Security & Loss Prevention
- Module: 2 of 8
- Persona: P1
- Working title: Safes, Vaults, and Nightly Put-Away Discipline
- Learning objective: Associates can explain why safe rating and placement matter, follow the store's in-safe warranty as a hard compliance floor, and understand the record-keeping and special-handling categories that must travel alongside vault contents.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Explain why nightly put-away discipline and safe specification matter, and follow the store's in-safe warranty requirements."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames the safe/vault as the asset Module 1's two-person rule protects; three components (rating, put-away discipline, record-keeping) that must work together.
2. **Safe Rating Is Not a Detail — It's the Whole Point** — TL vs. TRTL rating explanation; placement rules (no outside/shared walls, rear gate discipline).
3. **The In-Safe Warranty: Your Insurance Policy Sets the Floor** — Contractual nature of in-safe warranties; ~70% benchmark caveat; tools that make full put-away achievable (trays, carts, descending inserts).
4. **What a Vault Actually Needs to Hold — Beyond Just Merchandise** — Jewelers Mutual's documentation floor (annual itemized inventory, invoices, cost records, sales receipts); 7-year retention, off-premises storage rationale.
5. **Building a Perpetual Inventory System, Not Just an Annual Count** — Unique inventory numbers, category codes, sales tags, documented movement forms.
6. **Special Handling Categories: Not Everything in the Safe Is "Your" Inventory** — Ten distinct categories (pawned, estate, memo, consignment, scrap gold, repairs, non-invoiced, custom, gold-by-gram/bulk stones, giftware) and why conflating them causes downstream errors.
7. **Insurance Reporting Cadence: Keeping the List Current** — Quarterly/half-year updates, annual January export plus photo archive, gold-price-driven revaluation.
8. **Practice Exercise** — Category-by-category vault walkthrough self-check.
9. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Ties to Other Modules
