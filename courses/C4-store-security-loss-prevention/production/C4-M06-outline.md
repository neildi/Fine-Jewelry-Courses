# C4-M06 Outline: Large Cash Transactions and Form 8300 Compliance

## Module Metadata
- Course: C4 - Store Security & Loss Prevention
- Module: 6 of 8
- Persona: P2
- Working title: Large Cash Transactions and Form 8300 Compliance
- Learning objective: Associates can identify when a cash transaction (or related series of transactions) triggers Form 8300, correctly furnish the required customer statement (never the form itself), follow the non-disclosure rule for suspicious-transaction filings, and recognize how layaway/deposit structures can trigger aggregate reporting.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Correctly identify when a transaction triggers Form 8300 and follow the required reporting and non-disclosure steps."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Distinguishes this module (legal filing requirement) from Modules 4-5 (pattern/fraud recognition); applies regardless of suspicion level.
2. **What Triggers a Filing** — $10,000 threshold on cash/related transactions; e-filing requirement since Jan 1, 2024; clarifies cash vs. card/check scope.
3. **The Required Filing and the Customer Statement** — Four required statement elements; invoice-as-statement option; January 31 deadline.
4. **What NOT to Send as the Statement** — Never send the actual filed form; suspicious-transaction non-disclosure hard rule.
5. **Documenting Your Attempts to Get Required Information** — IRS guidance on good-faith documentation when a customer won't provide full information.
6. **Connecting This to the Rest of Your Loss-Prevention Practice** — Cash isn't inherently suspicious; compliance is procedural, independent of suspicion.
7. **Related Transactions: Why Splitting Payments Doesn't Avoid the Requirement** — Aggregation principle; relevance to layaway/deposit/custom-order structures.
8. **A Worked Scenario: The Layaway Payment That Crosses the Threshold** — Illustrative synthesis showing cumulative aggregation across three payments.
9. **Practice Exercise** — Self-audit of store's layaway/deposit cash-tracking method.
10. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.
