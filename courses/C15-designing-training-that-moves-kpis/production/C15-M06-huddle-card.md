# C15-M06 Huddle Card

**Headline:** No baseline, no proof the training worked.

**Key points:**
1. A minimum pre/post design needs three things decided before training happens: which KPI, what the baseline number is now, and when you'll check again.
2. Training leadership in this industry is measured on conversion, ATV, and retention — not completion rates or satisfaction scores.
3. If a KPI doesn't move, run three checks before rewriting the training: was it delivered as designed, was the KPI realistic, was the check-in window long enough.

**Script to practice today:** "Before we roll this out: what's our baseline number right now, and what date are we checking it again?"

**Question to ask the team:** Which of our last three training rollouts has an actual before-and-after KPI number, and which ones do we just assume worked?
