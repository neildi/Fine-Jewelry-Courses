# C15-M04 Huddle Card

**Headline:** Knowing the content and delivering it well are two different skills.

**Key points:**
1. Named facilitator resources (NDC's Train the Trainer guides, NHPA's Trainer's Toolbox) exist specifically because delivering training is a distinct skill from knowing the material.
2. A train-the-trainer session should demonstrate the module once, then have each manager practice running it while getting feedback — not just explain how to deliver it.
3. Train-the-trainer is what lets a scenario bank or huddle sequence scale consistently across every store, not just the one the trainer personally visits.

**Script to practice today:** "I'll run this scenario once as your team would experience it. Then each of you runs it, and we'll tell you what worked."

**Question to ask the team:** Which store managers have never been specifically trained on how to run our huddle-based practice, versus just handed the content?
