# C15-M07 Huddle Card

**Headline:** Don't build a new roadmap — we already have one.

**Key points:**
1. This catalog's own Prompt Kit already defines a persona-to-course learning path for every role, from new associate to multi-store leader to trainer.
2. A curriculum roadmap sequences existing modules by career stage; it doesn't require writing new content.
3. When someone advances a career stage, their next roadmap should already be waiting — tie this to the succession work in C14's talent-pipeline module.

**Script to practice today:** "What's the next course or module in this person's existing roadmap — not what new thing should we build for them?"

**Question to ask the team:** Which of our learners just finished their current stage's roadmap and needs their next one assigned today?
