# C15-M05 Huddle Card

**Headline:** If it takes more than two minutes to read aloud, it's not a huddle card yet.

**Key points:**
1. Major industry providers (NDC, De Beers) build their flagship free retailer training as short-form microlearning — 2-5 minute videos and self-paced fast facts, not long sessions.
2. This project's own huddle-card format (1 headline, 3 key points, 1 script, 1 question) is already the built-in microlearning unit — no new format needs inventing.
3. Every huddle card must end with a specific "say this today" script line, or it's trivia, not training.

**Script to practice today:** "Today's one fact and one line to say: [insert the single most important point and its matching script]."

**Question to ask the team:** Which of our huddle cards actually take longer than two minutes to read aloud, and what needs to be cut?
