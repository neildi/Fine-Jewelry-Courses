# C15-M01 Outline / Media Record

**Working title:** Outcomes Before Content
**Alternates:** "Write the Outcome Before You Write the Slide"; "The Outcome-First Test for Training Design"

**Scenario hook:** A trainer builds a thorough, well-organized diamond-quality module for new hires, but conversion rates don't move three weeks later, because the module was built around a topic rather than an observable, KPI-linked behavior.

**Section outline:**
1. Why This Matters — reader connects how training leadership itself is measured (commercial KPIs, not activity volume) to how every module should be designed.
2. The Outcome-First Test — reader can distinguish topic-based from behavior-based outcomes using a comparison table.
3. Writing the Outcome Before the Content — reader can apply a one-sentence self-check before building any module.
4. Connecting Outcomes to Existing Course Frameworks — reader can pull existing behavior-and-KPI pairs from C2, C12, C13 rather than inventing new ones.
5. Objections, Mistakes and Edge Cases — reader avoids the "background knowledge first" trap and non-observable outcome wording.

**Facts used, with placement:**
- Director of Sales Training role requires deep KPI understanding, must demonstrate training ROI in sales/client satisfaction terms → Section 1
- Watches of Switzerland L&D role explicitly measured on conversion rates, ATV, client retention → Section 1
- Montblanc Head of Training scope and reporting line (context, not primary claim) → Section 1
- Cross-references to C2 (sales-conversation behaviors), C12 (KPI vocabulary), C13 (coaching/onboarding verification) as existing frameworks → Section 4
- NHPA Path to Success, HubSpot Sales Management Training Certification, ATD Workshop Series → "Go deeper"

**Gaps not covered in evidence:** No specific named "outcome-first" methodology or template was found verbatim in the supplied research; the comparison table and self-check question are original synthesis modeling the documented emphasis on KPI-linked training measurement, not a verbatim sourced framework — flagged for reviewer awareness.
