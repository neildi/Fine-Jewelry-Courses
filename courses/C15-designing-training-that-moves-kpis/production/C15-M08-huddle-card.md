# C15-M08 Huddle Card

**Headline:** Training built once and never updated is teaching last year's floor.

**Key points:**
1. JCK, National Jeweler, INSTORE, and Rapaport are all free, ongoing sources for the kind of market shifts that make training content go stale.
2. Use a simple filter before updating anything: does this change what an associate should say or do differently on the floor this month?
3. Trade-press notes are the input to Module 3's quarterly scenario-bank review — read weekly, refresh quarterly.

**Script to practice today:** "I read something this week that might change how we handle [X objection/scenario] — flagging it for our next scenario-bank review."

**Question to ask the team:** What's the last trade-press story that actually changed something in our training content, and when did that happen?
