# C15-M03 Huddle Card

**Headline:** Stop improvising role-play. Build the bank once, use it every week.

**Key points:**
1. Every scenario needs four things: the client's opener, the underlying dynamic, what a strong response demonstrates, and a scoring note.
2. Build at least two difficulty tiers per scenario — an easy version for new hires, a harder version with real-floor messiness for experienced associates.
3. Start from C2's eight named objections (price, "just looking," "I'll think about it," online comparison, lab-grown, financing, partner approval, competitor quote) rather than inventing a new list.

**Script to practice today:** "This week's scenario is [X], tier one for newer associates, tier two for everyone else — I'm watching for [the specific success indicator]."

**Question to ask the team:** Which of our eight core objections doesn't have a scenario-bank entry yet, and who's writing it this week?
