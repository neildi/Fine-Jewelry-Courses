# C15-M05 Outline / Media Record

**Working title:** Microlearning and Huddle Content
**Alternates:** "The Huddle Card Is the Microlearning Unit"; "What Makes Training Content Huddle-Ready"

**Scenario hook:** A trainer keeps writing huddle cards that are secretly full articles crammed onto one page, taking managers ten to fifteen minutes to deliver instead of the intended five.

**Section outline:**
1. Why This Matters — reader connects NDC and De Beers' short-form flagship training to the general standard for sales-floor microlearning.
2. What Makes Content Huddle-Ready — reader can distinguish huddle-ready from non-huddle-ready content using a comparison table.
3. The Huddle Card as the Native Format — reader connects this project's own production-required huddle card to the microlearning unit concept.
4. Sequencing Microlearning Across a Week — reader connects individual huddle cards to Module 2's five-day sequence.
5. Objections, Mistakes and Edge Cases — reader avoids the overloaded-card trap and the "microlearning = low quality" misconception.

**Facts used, with placement:**
- NDC Diamond Learning Center: 30 short videos, 4 self-paced modules, videos 2-5 min each → Section 1
- De Beers Group Institute of Diamonds free retailer app: self-paced micro-learning (videos, podcasts, games, fast facts) → Section 1
- This project's own Prompt Kit huddle-card repurposing spec (Part B7): 1 headline, 3 key points, 1 script, 1 question, under 200 words → Section 3
- Cross-reference to Module 2's five-day huddle sequence → Section 4

**Gaps not covered in evidence:** No specific named methodology titled "huddle-ready content" was found verbatim in the supplied research; the three-property definition and comparison table are original synthesis built to operationalize the documented short-form training standard and the project's own huddle-card spec — flagged for reviewer awareness.
