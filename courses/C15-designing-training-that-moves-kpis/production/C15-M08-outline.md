# C15-M08 Outline / Media Record

**Working title:** Keeping Content Current With the Trade Press
**Alternates:** "The Trainer's Weekly Reading Habit"; "From Trade Press to Scenario-Bank Update"

**Scenario hook:** A trainer's carefully built modules go stale because lab-grown pricing, financing products, and market context keep shifting while the training content stays frozen at its original build date.

**Section outline:**
1. Why This Matters — reader connects the four named free trade publications to the general need for ongoing content currency.
2. Building a Trade-Press Reading Habit Into Training Work — reader can build a simple weekly reading routine with a publication table.
3. Deciding What Actually Needs a Content Update — reader can apply a "does this change floor behavior" filter to trade-press content.
4. Connecting Trade-Press Monitoring to the Refresh Cycle Already Built — reader connects ongoing reading to Module 3's quarterly scenario-bank review.
5. Objections, Mistakes and Edge Cases — reader avoids both under-reading and over-reacting to every headline.

**Facts used, with placement:**

- National Jeweler: free-to-read news site, daily newsletter, market analysis and retail columns → Sections 1, 2
- INSTORE Magazine: free print/digital subscription, education podcast hub, sales-floor/hiring/coaching/store-culture content → Sections 1, 2
- Rapaport Library/magazine archive: diamond and jewelry industry education, retail training features, ethics → Sections 1, 2
- Cross-reference to Module 3's quarterly scenario-bank refresh discipline → Section 4
