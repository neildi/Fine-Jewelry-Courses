# C15-M02 Huddle Card

**Headline:** Design for the huddle, not the classroom.

**Key points:**
1. The daily huddle and weekly team meeting are already-built delivery vehicles for training — use them instead of competing with them for a separate session.
2. A five-day sequence of 5-10 minute huddle reps (introduce, practice, apply, report back, reinforce) beats a single long classroom session for actual behavior change.
3. Every piece of training content should come with a one-sentence practice instruction: who plays what role, what the manager listens for, what "done well" sounds like.

**Script to practice today:** "Pair up. One of you raises the objection, the other responds. Thirty seconds, then switch — I'm listening for [the specific behavior]."

**Question to ask the team:** Which of our recent training modules actually changed a behavior on the floor, and which ones were just well-attended?
