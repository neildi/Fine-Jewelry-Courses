# C15-M01 Huddle Card

**Headline:** Write the outcome before you write the slide.

**Key points:**
1. Training leadership is measured on commercial KPIs (conversion, ATV, client retention), not training-activity volume — every module should be designed backward from a KPI, not a topic.
2. A strong outcome is observable, timed, and names the KPI it should move; "understand the 4Cs" fails this test, "explain the 4Cs using two stones in under 3 minutes" passes.
3. Check this project's own courses (C2, C12, C13) for existing behavior-and-KPI pairs before inventing a new one.

**Script to practice today:** "What will this learner do on the floor tomorrow that they can't do today, and which KPI should move because of it?"

**Question to ask the team:** Which of our current training modules doesn't have a KPI attached to it, and what would that KPI be?
