# Huddle Card: Lab-Grown, Natural, Simulant

**Headline:** Lab-grown is a real diamond that formed differently. A simulant was never a diamond at all. Never blur those two.

**3 key points:**
1. Say "laboratory-grown diamond," every time, verbally and on the receipt line item. This is an FTC requirement, not a style choice, and the FTC has actually sent warning letters over this.
2. Lab-grown diamonds are made two ways, HPHT (high pressure/heat, months) or CVD (gas deposition, weeks). Neither is "more real."
3. Moissanite and cubic zirconia are NOT diamond, of any kind. Name the material explicitly if you're recommending one.

**Script to practice today:**
"This particular stone is a laboratory-grown diamond, chemically and optically a real diamond, just grown in a lab instead of mined. If natural origin matters to you, I have natural options too."

**Question to ask the team:**
Does every lab-grown piece in our case have a receipt template that shows "laboratory-grown diamond" as the actual line-item description, not a footnote?
