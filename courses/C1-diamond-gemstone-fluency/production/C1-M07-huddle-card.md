# Huddle Card: Pearls, Types and Grading

**Headline:** Pearls have 7 value factors, not 4Cs, and the shape difference between strands has a mechanical reason you can actually explain.

**3 key points:**
1. All pearls we sell are cultured, real, biologically formed, just started by a technician instead of an accident. Natural pearls are extraordinarily rare and basically never in normal retail.
2. Bead-nucleated pearls (Akoya, South Sea, Tahitian) tend round. Tissue-nucleated pearls (most freshwater) tend oval/baroque, no bead template.
3. Size is only ONE of seven value factors. Luster, surface, matching, nacre quality, shape, and color all matter too.

**Script to practice today:**
"Size is one factor, but pearls are graded on seven. This one has sharper luster and is more tightly matched, that's why it costs more even at a similar size."

**Question to ask the team:**
Can we explain, on the spot, why freshwater pearls in our case are less round than the Akoya strand?
