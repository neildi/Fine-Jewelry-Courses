# Huddle Card: The Colored Stone Top 10

**Headline:** Hardness alone lies about durability. Know the real care story for every stone in your case.

**3 key points:**
1. Tanzanite is soft (6-6.5) AND only comes from one place on Earth (Merelani, Tanzania). Rarity story + honest daily-wear caveat, both in the same pitch.
2. Emerald's 7.5-8 hardness hides real fragility. Almost all emeralds are oiled (F1 minor to F3 significant), never ultrasonic or steam clean them.
3. Some vivid orange/pink sapphires are beryllium-diffused, a real, disclosed treatment. Check documentation before answering "is this natural."

**Script to practice today:**
"Tanzanite is found in exactly one place on Earth, a tiny area in Tanzania. It's also naturally three different colors depending on the angle, heat treatment settles it into the blue-violet you see here."

**Question to ask the team:**
Do we know off the top of our heads which stones in our case are F1 vs F2 vs F3 emeralds, or beryllium-diffused sapphires?
