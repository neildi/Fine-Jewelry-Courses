# Huddle Card: Fluorescence, Inclusions and "Is This a Flaw?"

**Headline:** Fluorescence is not a flaw, it's not even one of the 4Cs, and it has a real physical cause you can actually explain.

**3 key points:**
1. Blue fluorescence (95%+ of cases) comes from the N3 defect, three nitrogen atoms trapped around a gap in the crystal. It's not a defect, it's chemistry.
2. Fluorescence and phosphorescence are NOT the same thing. Fluorescence stops the instant UV is removed. Phosphorescence (rare, under 1%) is an afterglow that lingers.
3. Inclusions are birthmarks, not flaws. They prove natural formation and make the stone identifiable, like a fingerprint.

**Script to practice today:**
"That's an inclusion, a tiny natural feature that formed with the diamond. It's already factored into the clarity grade, and at this grade you won't see it with the naked eye once it's on your hand."

**Question to ask the team:**
Has a client ever confused fluorescence with "glowing in the dark" (phosphorescence), and did we clear that up confidently?
