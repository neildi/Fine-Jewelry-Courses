# Huddle Card: Carat, Cut, Color, Clarity

**Headline:** Show, don't tell, the 4Cs, or you'll lose the sale to the store that does.

**3 key points:**
1. Carat is weight, not size. Compare two stones face-up to show spread, not just the number on the tag.
2. Cut is the only C a human made, and it's the most visible without magnification. Demonstrate brightness (hold still), fire (rock slowly), and scintillation (move it) as three separate things.
3. Inclusions are birthmarks, not flaws. Show one under the loupe and call it "what makes this stone identifiable," never apologize for it.

**Script to practice today:**
"Let me put this one and this one next to each other face-up. Tell me which looks larger to you." (Then explain why the lighter stone often wins that comparison.)

**Question to ask the team:**
Which one C do most of our clients actually anchor their decision on, cut, color, clarity, or carat, and are we demonstrating that one first?
