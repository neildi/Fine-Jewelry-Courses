* **Movement 1: The Foundation Frame (Leading Line):**  
  *"Every diamond or gemstone we set starts with the metal's story &mdash; because the metal is the foundation that protects your heirloom for the next fifty years."*
* **Movement 2: The Platinum Heft Test (The Tactile Wow Moment):**  
  *(Place a platinum ring in the client's palm, followed immediately by an identical-width white gold piece.)*  
  *"Feel that weight shift? That density is what true permanence feels like. Platinum is almost twice as dense as 14-karat gold. That extra heft means the prongs holding your diamond are solid, durable metal through and through &mdash; naturally hypoallergenic and pure white without any plating."*
* **Movement 3: The Patina Reframe (Displacement vs. Loss):**  
  *"When clients notice platinum developing a soft satin luster over time, they sometimes wonder if it is scratching. It is actually doing something remarkable: gold loses tiny flakes of metal with every polish and scrape, but platinum simply displaces &mdash; the metal ridges shift aside rather than wearing away. That rich patina is the hallmark of fine platinum that has lived life with you."*
* **Movement 4: The Bench Trust Close:**  
  *"Honesty on the counter means never hiding maintenance. If you choose white gold, we will gladly refresh your rhodium every 12 to 24 months to keep it dazzling. If you want zero maintenance and heirloom permanence, platinum is the investment that protects your stone forever."*
