# Huddle Card: Reading a GIA Report With a Client

**Headline:** Never hand over a report in silence, that's how you become the price-comparison tool for a competitor.

**3 key points:**
1. It's a "grading report," never a "certificate." A report documents quality, it does not guarantee value.
2. Always verify live on GIA Report Check in front of the client, it's the single highest-trust move on the floor.
3. If a client has a competing report (GIA, IGI, or otherwise), compare documents field by field, never disparage another lab.

**Script to practice today:**
"This is a GIA Diamond Grading Report. GIA is an independent nonprofit, they created the 4Cs. They have no stake in this sale." Then verify the number live on Report Check while the client watches.

**Question to ask the team:**
When was the last time you handed a report to a client without narrating it first, and what happened?
