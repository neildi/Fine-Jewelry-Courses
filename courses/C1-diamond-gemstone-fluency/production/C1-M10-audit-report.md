  *"Welcome — I am delighted you came in today. Every client who sits at this counter deserves our absolute focus and expertise."*
* **Phase 2: The 60-Second Technical-to-Emotional Bridge:**  
  *"Let me show you something most people never get to experience up close. This is not just a laboratory grade on paper &mdash; it is the reason this gemstone will catch light across the room for the rest of her life."*
* **Phase 3: The Two-Stone Theater (The Spread & Performance Contrast):**  
  *"Hold these two stones side-by-side on this grooved contrast pad, and tell me what your eye notices first. You aren't just buying a certificate &mdash; you are investing in the beauty your own eye just discovered."*
