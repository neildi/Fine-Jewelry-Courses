# Huddle Card: AGS 0-10 vs. GIA Nomenclature

**Headline:** Two labs, two number systems, same physical diamond, don't let a client think either one is fake.

**3 key points:**
1. AGS runs 0 (best) to 10 (worst) on cut, color, and clarity. GIA uses letters and Excellent-to-Poor. Neither converts to the other exactly.
2. AGS grades cut more finely (11 grades) using light-performance tools like ASET, a color-coded map of where light returns or leaks.
3. "AGS Triple Zero" means zeros in cut, color, AND clarity together, it's trade slang, not an official single stamp on the report.

**Script to practice today:**
"Both are respected, independent labs. They just use different scales, the way Celsius and Fahrenheit both measure temperature."

**Question to ask the team:**
Has a client ever brought in an AGS report and did we handle the "which lab is better" question confidently?
