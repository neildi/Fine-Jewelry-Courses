# Module 2 Self-Assessment Quiz: Reading a GIA Report

10 multiple-choice questions testing the terminal outcome: walking a client through a GIA report in under three minutes without triggering legal or trust exposure.

**1. Which GIA report type does NOT include a plotted clarity diagram?**
A. Diamond Grading Report
B. Diamond Dossier
C. Diamond Origin Report (printed version)
D. All GIA reports include a plotted diagram
**Answer: B** - The Diamond Dossier, issued for stones 0.15-1.99 ct, includes all 4Cs grading results but omits the plotted diagram, unlike the full Grading Report and printed Origin Report.

**2. What does GIA Report Check actually confirm?**
A. The exact resale value of the diamond
B. That the report information matches GIA's database, subject to stated limitations
C. That the diamond has never been previously owned
D. The diamond's current market price
**Answer: B** - Report Check verifies a match against GIA's archived data; it does not state value and its returned information depends on the report's issue date tier.

**3. Why should you never say "it's a certificate"?**
A. It's grammatically incorrect
B. GIA itself calls "certificate" a misnomer; a GIA report is an opinion of quality, not a certification of value
C. Only appraisals can be called certificates
D. Clients dislike the word
**Answer: B** - GIA's own terminology guidance states that "certificate" is a misnomer sometimes applied to grading reports, which are properly described as GIA's opinion of the stone's quality.

**4. A client asks what her diamond is "worth" while you're reading the grading report. What should you do?**
A. Estimate a figure based on the grades
B. Explain that the report has no value on it and route her to your store's appraisal process
C. Quote the retail price on the tag
D. Tell her the report guarantees the value
**Answer: B** - Value is an appraisal question, not a grading-report question; associates should never estimate or state a value from the grading report alone.

**5. A client brings in a report with a number that returns no match on Report Check. What is the correct next step?**
A. Assume it's a printing error and proceed with the transaction
B. Accuse the client of fraud
C. Calmly explain more verification is needed and involve your manager
D. Estimate the grades yourself without the report
**Answer: C** - An unmatched report number is a red flag requiring manager involvement and further verification, handled calmly rather than assumed to be either fraud or a simple error.

**6. What is the key difference between insurance replacement value and fair market value?**
A. They are the same number, just different names
B. Insurance replacement value reflects retail replacement cost; fair market value reflects a private buyer-seller price, typically lower
C. Fair market value is always higher than insurance value
D. Only fair market value is used for insurance
**Answer: B** - Insurance replacement value states the cost to replace an item new at retail, while fair market value reflects what a willing buyer would actually pay a willing seller, which is typically a lower figure.

**7. A client says an IGI-graded stone online has the same grades as yours but costs less. What is the best response?**
A. Tell the client IGI reports are worthless
B. Explain that different labs can grade the same physical stone slightly differently, and offer to compare the actual stones side by side
C. Immediately match the online price
D. Refuse to discuss another lab's grading
**Answer: B** - The honest, defensible position acknowledges that grading standards can vary slightly between legitimate labs, and redirects the comparison to the physical stones rather than grade labels alone.

**8. What is applied automatically to a diamond with a GIA Diamond Dossier, but is only optional for a full Diamond Grading Report?**
A. The plotted diagram
B. The girdle laser inscription
C. The proportion diagram
D. The fluorescence grade
**Answer: B** - GIA automatically inscribes diamonds accompanied by a Dossier or Focus Report, but girdle inscription is an optional additional service for a full Grading Report.

**9. Which of the following is one of the four high-risk phrases this module identifies?**
A. "This is a beautiful stone."
B. "This guarantees the value."
C. "Let's look at this under the loupe."
D. "The report was issued in 2023."
**Answer: B** - "This guarantees the value" falsely implies the report contains valuation, which it never does; the other options are all safe, factual statements.

**10. Why does an older GIA report sometimes return less information through Report Check?**
A. The report is more likely to be fake
B. GIA's data-return tiers are based on the report's issue date, with later reports returning more data types
C. Older diamonds are lower quality
D. Report Check only works for reports issued after 2022
**Answer: B** - GIA's Report Check has multiple date-based coverage tiers (2000, 2010, 2014, 2015, 2019, 2022); older reports simply fall into tiers that return less data by design, which is not evidence of a fake report.
