# Huddle Card: Treatment and Origin Disclosure

**Headline:** Disclosure isn't a courtesy, it's federal law with real penalties, and origin claims carry real money on the line.

**3 key points:**
1. Three-question test: not permanent? special care needed? significant value effect? If yes to any, disclose it.
2. Never state a geographic origin (Kashmir, Burma, Colombia) without lab documentation. A confirmed Kashmir sapphire can sell for millions more than an unconfirmed one that looks identical.
3. Diamonds have their own compliance system: the Kimberley Process, a certificate confirming rough diamonds are conflict-free before international trade.

**Script to practice today:**
"Origin can only be confirmed through laboratory documentation, never just from how a stone looks. Let me check what documentation exists for this piece."

**Question to ask the team:**
Do we know how our store documents diamond sourcing/Kimberley Process compliance if a client asks?
