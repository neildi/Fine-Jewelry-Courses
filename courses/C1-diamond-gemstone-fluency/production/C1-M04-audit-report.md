## 2. Proprietary Sales Psychology: The Origin Clarification Protocol™

* **Beat 1: Acknowledge & Affirm (Leading Line):**  
  *"That is a sharp question — and I love that you're asking it, because it tells me you want to understand exactly what you are investing in, not just buy a label."*
* **Beat 2: The Dual Wonder Moment (Value-Added Wonder):**  
  - *Natural:* "What you are holding formed one to three billion years ago, roughly 100 miles beneath the Earth's crust under immense heat and pressure — then was delivered to the surface in a volcanic eruption that hasn't happened anywhere on Earth in millions of years."
  - *Lab-Grown:* "What you are holding is the exact same carbon crystal structure — engineers compressed that billion-year process into weeks, using precision plasma deposition to build the diamond one atomic layer at a time."
* **Beat 3: Chemical Identity, Value Divergence:**  
  *"They are chemically, physically, and optically identical. Where they diverge is scarcity and story: one is finite, irreplaceable geology; the other is a renewable technological marvel priced accordingly. Neither fact makes one 'better' — they simply answer two different questions: 'What does this represent as an heirloom?' versus 'What does this look like on my hand today?'"*
* **Beat 4: Choice Celebration Close:**  
  *"There is no wrong answer here — only the one that honors your personal milestone. My role is to give you absolute clarity so you feel genuine joy every time you look down at your hand."*
