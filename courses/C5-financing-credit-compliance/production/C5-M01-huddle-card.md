# C5-M01 Huddle Card: Consumer Financing Basics

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Only charge a financed account for what the customer receives TODAY. If they're not walking out with it today, it's Authorization Only — never a full charge.

## The Two Rules
1. **Receiving it now?** → Charge now.
2. **Not receiving it now (custom order, special order)?** → Authorization Only today, Forced Purchase later when it's actually ready and delivered.

## Why It Matters
If you charge in full for a custom order and something changes before delivery — a delay, a redesign, a cancellation — you're unwinding a completed charge instead of just canceling a hold. That's a much harder problem for everyone, including the customer.

## Before Any Financing Sale
The credit agreement must be in the customer's hands BEFORE the sale proceeds — not sent afterward as a follow-up.

## One Thing to Remember
Not every employer in this industry offers financing — luxury maisons often skip it entirely. Know what your specific store actually offers.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Think of a custom order we've financed recently. Did we use Authorization Only correctly, or did we charge in full too early?
