# C5-M03 Huddle Card: Layaway vs. Financing vs. BNPL

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** These are three different tools for three different situations — not three names for the same thing. Match the tool to the customer, not the customer to whatever's easiest for you.

## Three Questions to Ask
1. Does the customer need the item TODAY? (No → layaway is fine. Yes → financing or BNPL.)
2. Do they want to avoid credit entirely? (Yes → layaway, regardless of timeline.)
3. How big is the purchase, and how fast do they want to pay it off? (Big + slower → financing. Smaller + fast → BNPL.)

## Quick Reference
- **Layaway** = no credit, store holds the item, pay first, take it home last.
- **Financing** = real credit, usually take it home today, deferred-interest terms apply.
- **BNPL** = short-term, equal installments, usually smaller purchases.

## Why It Matters
A customer matched to the right tool is more likely to actually complete the purchase and come back. A customer pushed into the wrong tool is a completion-rate and relationship risk.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Think of a recent sale. Which of the three tools did the customer actually need — and did we offer the right one?
