## 1. Executive Summary & Verification

Course C5 Module 4 delineates the legal boundaries, statutory liabilities, and regulatory prohibitions governing consumer credit representations at the fine jewelry counter. It focuses on the Truth in Lending Act (TILA / Regulation Z) trigger terms, the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA / Regulation B), and the mandatory operational protocols for executing dignified, private adverse action (decline) notifications that protect client self-worth and eliminate compliance exposure.

1. **Brand Compliance:** STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE for discount/mass-market retailers (`Kay Jewelers`, `Effy`, `Blue Nile`, `James Allen`, `Diamonds International`, `Zales`). Legal and floor protocols aligned with high-jewelry private client service standards (**Cartier, Bulgari, Tacori, Hearts On Fire, Tiffany & Co., Stuller, Harry Winston, and Van Cleef & Arpels**).
2. **Attribution & White-Labeling:** 100% white-labeled under proprietary Jewelswell trademarks: ***The Seven Counter Prohibitions Matrix™***, ***The TILA Trigger Term Defense Architecture™***, and ***The Dignified Adverse Action Protocol™***. Zero third-party trainer citations.
