## 1. Executive Summary & Verification

Course C5 Module 2 addresses the psychology and ethical cadence of introducing consumer financing on the luxury sales floor. It dismantles frontline associate hesitation, positions financing during early aesthetic discovery before price shock occurs, establishes the mandatory Dual-Pricing presentation discipline (quoting total investment alongside monthly promotional allocation), and erects firm ethical boundaries against high-pressure over-leveraging.

The audit verified three mandatory quality dimensions:
1. **Brand Compliance:** STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE for discount/mass-market retailers (`Kay Jewelers`, `Effy`, `Blue Nile`, `James Allen`, `Diamonds International`, `Zales`). Behavioral standards aligned with elite luxury houses (**Cartier, Bulgari, Tacori, Hearts On Fire, Tiffany & Co., Stuller, Harry Winston, and Van Cleef & Arpels**).
2. **Attribution & White-Labeling:** 100% white-labeled under proprietary Jewelswell trademarks: ***The Discovery Window Financing Protocol™***, ***The Dual-Pricing Standard™***, and ***The Client Wealth Protection Guardrail™***. Zero third-party trainer citations.
