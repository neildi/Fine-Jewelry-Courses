# C5-M04 Outline: FTC Disclosure Rules: Treatments, Lab-Grown, and Advertising Claims

## Module Metadata
- Course: C5 - Financing, Credit & Compliance
- Module: 4 of 6
- Persona: P2
- Working title: FTC Disclosure Rules: Treatments, Lab-Grown, and Advertising Claims
- Learning objective: Associates can explain why treatment and natural-vs-lab-grown disclosure must be affirmative rather than implied, avoid making unsupportable advertising/value claims, and understand why disclosure accuracy matters more in a financed-sale context.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Explain the FTC disclosure requirements around treatments, lab-grown vs. natural, and advertising claims well enough to avoid making an unsupported promise to a financed client."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Shifts from payment mechanics (Modules 1-3) to product-claim compliance; financing-relationship stakes framing.
2. **The FTC Jewelry Guides: The Regulatory Backbone** — JVC's framing of FTC Guidelines as "the legal frame around what you can promise a financed client"; related JVC Essential Guides topics (memo/consignment, UCC-1, platinum marking, irradiated gemstones).
3. **Treatment Disclosure: Say What Was Done to the Stone** — Core disclosure principle; treatments are legitimate trade practice but must be disclosed, not assumed away.
4. **Natural vs. Lab-Grown: A Distinction That Must Be Stated, Not Implied** — Affirmative disclosure requirement; false-impression risk even without explicit false statement.
5. **Advertising Claims: Don't Promise What You Can't Support** — Value/quality/savings claim discipline; escalate uncertain claims to a manager.
6. **Why This Connects Back to Financing** — Compounding stakes of a disclosure problem in a financed vs. cash sale.
7. **A Worked Scenario: The Ambiguous Lab-Grown Description** — Incorrect (misleading by omission) vs. correct (affirmative, complete) explanation side-by-side.
8. **Practice Exercise** — Self-audit of current treatment/lab-grown explanation phrasing.
9. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.
