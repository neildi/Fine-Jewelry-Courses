# C5-M02 Huddle Card: Running a Financing Application at the POS

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** "0% for 18 months" is only half the story. If ANY balance remains after the promo ends, interest applies retroactively to the WHOLE original amount — not just the remainder.

## Explain It Right, Every Time
"If you pay off the full balance within [X] months, you pay zero interest. But if even a small amount is left after that, you'll be charged interest on the entire original purchase, back to today's date — not just on what's left."

## BNPL ≠ Deferred-Interest Financing
Don't describe them interchangeably. BNPL is usually short-term, equal installments, no deferred-interest trap. Traditional store financing usually has the retroactive-interest mechanic. Know which one you're actually offering.

## If an Application Is Declined
- Don't guess why.
- Point them to the notification the financing partner sends.
- Pivot naturally: layaway, a smaller deposit option, revisiting later.
- Never imply you can personally override the decision — you can't.

## Daily Habit
Check the Daily Funding Report like you'd check a deposit cleared. It's the financing version of cash reconciliation.
