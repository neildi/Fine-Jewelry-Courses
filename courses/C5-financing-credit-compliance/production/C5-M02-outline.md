# C5-M02 Outline: Running a Financing Application at the Point of Sale

## Module Metadata
- Course: C5 - Financing, Credit & Compliance
- Module: 2 of 6
- Persona: P1
- Working title: Running a Financing Application at the Point of Sale
- Learning objective: Associates can accurately explain the deferred-interest retroactive-charge mechanic, distinguish BNPL from traditional deferred-interest financing, handle a declined application correctly, and use the daily/monthly financing reports as part of normal reconciliation practice.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: Extends the C5 terminal outcome on register-level financing mechanics into full application-handling competency.

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Bridges from Module 1's charge-timing rules into full application mechanics.
2. **How Deferred-Interest Promotional Financing Works** — The waive-if-paid-in-full-or-retroactive-charge mechanic; why this is the top source of customer confusion/complaints.
3. **Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) as a Related but Distinct Product** — Structural difference from deferred-interest financing; Reg Z/CFPB compliance framing.
4. **Handling a Declined Application** — Don't speculate on decline reasons; direct to financing partner's required notification; pivot to alternatives; never suggest override ability.
5. **Using the Daily and Monthly Reporting Tools in Practice** — Daily Funding Report as financing-equivalent of cash reconciliation; Credit Card Transaction Report for discrepancy-catching; Customer List as a discreet clienteling tool.
6. **A Worked Scenario: Explaining a Deferred-Interest Offer Correctly** — Incorrect vs. correct explanation side-by-side.
7. **Practice Exercise** — Verbal rehearsal of the two-part deferred-interest explanation.
8. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.
