# C5-M06 Quiz: Memo, Consignment, and UCC-1: Protecting the Store's Legal Interest

**Instructions:** Answer all questions. This module's quiz emphasizes correctly distinguishing ownership types and applying the capstone synthesis across the course.

---

**1. Who owns a memo piece of jewelry sitting in a store's case?**
A) The store, immediately upon receipt
B) A vendor, until the piece is actually sold
C) The customer, once they express interest
D) No one owns memo goods

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Memo goods are vendor-owned; the store owes the vendor either the item back (if unsold) or payment (if sold) — ownership does not transfer to the store simply by having the piece in its possession.

---

**2. Who owns a consignment piece of jewelry sitting in a store's case?**
A) The store
B) A customer who placed it with the store to sell on their behalf
C) A vendor
D) No one owns consignment goods

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Consignment goods are owned by a customer, not a vendor — the store owes the customer either the item back or the agreed proceeds, typically minus a commission.

---

**3. Why does this module say getting consignment ownership records wrong creates different stakes than getting memo records wrong?**
A) There is no real difference in stakes between the two
B) A consignment error creates a direct dispute with a customer who trusted the store with their own property, not just an internal/vendor bookkeeping issue
C) Consignment goods are never actually valuable
D) Memo errors are always more serious than consignment errors

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: While both require accurate tracking, a consignment ownership error directly affects a customer's own property and trust, distinct from a memo error which primarily affects the vendor relationship.

---

**4. What does a UCC-1 filing generally protect, per this module?**
A) A customer's right to a refund
B) A vendor's ownership or security interest in memo goods that are in someone else's physical possession, such as a store's showcase
C) A store's right to sell any jewelry in its case regardless of ownership
D) An associate's personal commission on a sale

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: A UCC-1 financing statement is a legal filing that lets a vendor formally establish and protect their ownership/security interest in memo goods, even though those goods are physically held by the store.

---

**5. In what kind of worst-case scenario does a UCC-1 filing matter most, per this module?**
A) A routine, uneventful sale
B) A bankruptcy, creditor dispute, or situation where multiple parties might claim rights to the same inventory
C) A customer simply browsing without buying
D) A slow sales day with no transactions

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: A properly filed UCC-1 demonstrates that memo goods were never store property, protecting the vendor's claim specifically in scenarios like bankruptcy or creditor disputes where ownership could otherwise be contested.

---

**6. Before financing a memo piece for a customer, what should be confirmed, per the worked scenario in this module?**
A) Nothing extra is needed beyond a standard financing sale
B) Whether the financing partner pays the store in full at time of sale, so the store's obligation to the vendor can be met promptly regardless of the customer's own payment timeline
C) The vendor's personal opinion on financing in general
D) The customer's favorite jewelry style

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Confirming that the financing partner funds the store promptly (per Module 1's charge-timing rules) clarifies that the vendor doesn't need to wait on the customer's multi-month financing timeline — the store can satisfy its memo obligation independently.
