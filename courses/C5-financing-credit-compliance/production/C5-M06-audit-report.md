## 1. Executive Summary & Verification

Course C5 Module 6 establishes the statutory compliance bedrock for jewelry representations on the retail floor under the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Guides for the Jewelry, Precious Metals, and Pewter Industries (16 CFR Part 23). It provides explicit guidance on diamond nomenclature and synthetic/lab-grown origin disclosures (§ 23.25), precious metal marking and alloy tolerances (§§ 23.3–23.8), gemstone treatment disclosure obligations (§ 23.22), and federal prohibitions against deceptive comparative pricing and inflated "appraisal" representations.

2. **Attribution & White-Labeling:** 100% white-labeled under proprietary Jewelswell trademarks: ***The FTC 16 CFR Part 23 Disclosure Standard™***, ***The Precious Metal Fineness Protocol™***, and ***The Ethical Gemological Representation Framework™***. Zero third-party trainer citations.
