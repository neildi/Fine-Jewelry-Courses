# C5-M05 Huddle Card: AML Basics for Jewelry Retail

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Jewelry is small, portable, and high-value — exactly the profile that makes it attractive for disguising the origin of money. Form 8300 (from C4) is one piece of that bigger picture, not the whole picture.

## Four Habits That Support AML Compliance
1. **Real clienteling** — genuinely knowing your customers helps you notice when something doesn't fit their pattern.
2. **Precise Form 8300 compliance** — exactly as covered in C4.
3. **Accurate inventory/provenance records** — so a piece's origin and the money behind it can actually be traced.
4. **Noticing unusual patterns** — not just large cash amounts, but clusters of odd behavior (first-time customer, cash near the threshold, rapid buy-return-rebuy).

## This Module Is Not Your Store's Full AML Program
A real program needs a written policy, a designated compliance officer, tailored training, and independent testing. If you're not sure your store has one, ask a manager — don't assume either way.
