# C5-M01 Outline: Consumer Financing Basics: How Retail Installment Credit Works at the Counter

## Module Metadata
- Course: C5 - Financing, Credit & Compliance
- Module: 1 of 6
- Persona: P1
- Working title: Consumer Financing Basics: How Retail Installment Credit Works at the Counter
- Learning objective: Associates can explain what a retail installment contract is, correctly apply the two register-level compliance rules (charge only for goods received now; Authorization Only/Forced Purchase for future delivery), and know that financing availability varies significantly by industry segment.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Explain how retail installment financing works at the counter, including the two register-level compliance rules."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames financing as a regulated credit product, not just a sales feature.
2. **What a Retail Installment Contract Actually Is** — CFPB definition; distinction from general-purpose revolving credit; store-branded/partner financing program structure.
3. **The Application Flow** — Multi-channel application process; the "credit agreement in hand before sale proceeds" requirement.
4. **Two Compliance Rules That Matter at the Register** — Charge-only-for-goods-received-now rule; Authorization Only → Forced Purchase sequence for future delivery.
5. **Why This Distinction Matters Beyond Compliance** — Practical customer-protection and store-exposure rationale for the two-step future-delivery process.
6. **What Reporting Looks Like on the Retailer Side** — Named financing-partner reports (Application Summary, Business Dashboard, Authorizations Report, etc.); associate job-duty framing.
7. **An Important Scope Note: Luxury Maisons Often Skip Financing Entirely** — Segment difference between chain/independent and luxury-maison employers.
8. **A Worked Scenario: The Custom Engagement Ring Deposit** — Illustrative synthesis contrasting correct Authorization/Forced Purchase sequencing against a premature full charge.
9. **Practice Exercise** — Self-audit of three financing scenario types against the two compliance rules.
10. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.
