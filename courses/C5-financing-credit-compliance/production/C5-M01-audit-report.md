## 1. Executive Summary & Verification

Course C5 Module 1 deconstructs the foundational economics and institutional architecture of consumer credit within high-ticket fine jewelry retail. It establishes the tripartite ecosystem (Customer, Salon Merchant, and Institutional Lender), demystifies the Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) as a merchant-absorbed cost of capital, contrasts revolving private-label lines against closed-end installment contracts, and details the digital waterfall underwriting engine.

The audit verified three mandatory quality dimensions:
1. **Brand Compliance:** STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE for discount/mass-market retailers (`Kay Jewelers`, `Effy`, `Blue Nile`, `James Allen`, `Diamonds International`, `Zales`). Financing standards and client communication modeled on premier luxury institutions (**Cartier, Bulgari, Tacori, Hearts On Fire, Tiffany & Co., Stuller, Harry Winston, and Van Cleef & Arpels**).
2. **Attribution & White-Labeling:** 100% white-labeled under proprietary Jewelswell trademarks: ***The Tripartite Credit Architecture™***, ***The MDR Value Absorption Principle™***, and ***The Algorithmic Waterfall Protocol™***. Zero third-party trainer citations.
3. **Pedagogical Polish & UI/UX:** Deployed the **Anti-Dropout Counter Simulation Dialogue Suite: 3 Master Scenarios** directly before Section 6. Clean luxury CSS layout verified with no unescaped syntax.
