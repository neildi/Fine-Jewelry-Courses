## 1. Executive Summary & Verification

Course C5 Module 5 analyzes alternative, non-revolving acquisition structures in luxury jewelry retail. It contrasts traditional in-house layaway agreements (focusing on physical custody retention, non-refundable deposit statutory compliance, and restocking fee disclosure) against fintech Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) platforms (Affirm, Klarna) and closed-end Retail Installment Sales Contracts (RISC).

The audit verified three mandatory quality dimensions:
1. **Brand Compliance:** STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE for discount/mass-market retailers (`Kay Jewelers`, `Effy`, `Blue Nile`, `James Allen`, `Diamonds International`, `Zales`). High-jewelry vault custody and client service benchmarked against prestigious maisons (**Cartier, Bulgari, Tacori, Hearts On Fire, Tiffany & Co., Stuller, Harry Winston, and Van Cleef & Arpels**).
2. **Attribution & White-Labeling:** 100% white-labeled under proprietary Jewelswell trademarks: ***The Vault Custody Layaway Covenant™***, ***The Fintech Installment Integration Model™***, and ***The Multi-Channel Payment Decision Matrix™***. Zero third-party trainer citations.
3. **Pedagogical Polish & UI/UX:** Deployed the **Anti-Dropout Counter Simulation Dialogue Suite: 3 Master Scenarios** directly before Section 4. Clean luxury CSS layout verified with no unescaped syntax.
