# C5-M05 Quiz: AML Basics for Jewelry Retail

**Instructions:** Answer all questions. This module's quiz emphasizes understanding why AML obligations exist and recognizing day-to-day supportive habits, not memorizing regulatory citations.

---

**1. Why can jewelry dealers be designated under AML (anti-money laundering) requirements, per this module?**
A) Jewelry has no real connection to financial crime risk
B) High-value, portable, easily-resold goods are an attractive vehicle for disguising the origin of money
C) Only online jewelry retailers face this designation
D) AML requirements apply exclusively to banks, never to retailers

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The underlying concern is that high-value, easily-transportable, easily-resold goods like jewelry are attractive for moving or disguising illegally-obtained money, which is why certain jewelry/precious-metals dealers are designated under this framework.

**2. How does Form 8300 (covered in C4) relate to the broader AML framework described in this module?**

C) Form 8300 replaces the need for any other AML consideration

**4. Which of the following is described in this module as a day-to-day habit that supports AML compliance?**
A) Avoiding any relationship-building with customers
B) Genuine clienteling that helps you notice when a transaction doesn't fit a customer's established pattern

Explanation: Building genuine client relationships naturally surfaces red flags that a purely transactional interaction would miss — this is one of the four overlapping habits this module connects to AML compliance.
