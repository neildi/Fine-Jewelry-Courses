# C5-M04 Huddle Card: FTC Disclosure Rules

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** State it plainly, then explain why it matters. Never let a customer infer a treatment or lab-grown status from price alone — say it directly.

## The Structure for Any Disclosure
1. State the fact plainly ("This is lab-grown" / "This stone has been heat-treated").
2. Explain why it matters to their decision (price, durability, care).

## Two Things That Must Always Be Stated, Not Implied
- **Treatments** — heat, irradiation, fracture filling, etc. Legitimate practice, but must be disclosed.
- **Natural vs. lab-grown** — never let price alone do the talking. Say it directly.

## Claims: If You Can't Back It Up, Don't Say It
"Worth double what you're paying" needs a real, verifiable basis. If you're not sure a claim holds up, ask a manager before repeating it.

## Why It Matters More With Financing
A financed sale is a longer relationship. A disclosure gap that surfaces 8 months into a payment plan is a much bigger problem than the same gap on a cash sale — it can turn into a chargeback or a complaint to the financing partner.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Say your usual explanation for a lab-grown stone or a treated gem out loud. Does it state the fact plainly, or does it lean on the customer not asking?
