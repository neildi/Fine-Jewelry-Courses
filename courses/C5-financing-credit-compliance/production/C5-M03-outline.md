# C5-M03 Outline: Layaway vs. Financing vs. Buy Now, Pay Later: Choosing the Right Option

## Module Metadata
- Course: C5 - Financing, Credit & Compliance
- Module: 3 of 6
- Persona: P1
- Working title: Layaway vs. Financing vs. Buy Now, Pay Later: Choosing the Right Option
- Learning objective: Associates can correctly distinguish layaway, financing, and BNPL as three structurally different "pay over time" tools, and use a three-question decision framework to recommend the right one for a given customer situation.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Correctly present financing, layaway, and BNPL as distinct options and match each to the right customer situation."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames the three tools as structurally distinct, not interchangeable.
2. **Layaway: The Store Holds the Item, the Customer Owns Nothing Yet** — No-credit structure; job-spec evidence treating layaway as a distinct process; best-fit customer profile.
3. **Financing: Credit Extended, Possession Now (Usually)** — Recap of Modules 1-2; best-fit customer profile.
4. **BNPL: Short-Term, Equal Installments, Different Mechanics Entirely** — Recap of Module 2's BNPL/Reg Z framing; best-fit customer profile.
5. **A Decision Framework, Not a Script** — Three-question framework (today's possession need, credit-avoidance preference, purchase size/payoff horizon).
6. **Side-by-Side Comparison** — Six-factor comparison table across all three tools.
7. **Worked Scenarios** — Three illustrative scenarios mapped to the correct recommendation.
8. **Practice Exercise** — Peer roleplay explaining the recommendation rationale for each scenario.
9. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Ties to Other Modules
