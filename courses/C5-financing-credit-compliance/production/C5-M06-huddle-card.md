# C5-M06 Huddle Card: Memo, Consignment, and UCC-1

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Every module in this course boils down to one question: who has a legitimate claim on this piece, this payment, or this relationship — and can we prove it?

## Know the Difference
- **Memo** = vendor owns it. We owe them the item back or payment.
- **Consignment** = a customer owns it. We owe them the item back or the agreed proceeds.
- Mixing these up isn't just a paperwork slip — it's a real dispute with a real party who trusted us with their property.

## UCC-1, in Plain Terms
It's how a vendor legally protects their claim on memo goods sitting in OUR case — so if something ever went seriously wrong (bankruptcy, a creditor dispute), their goods aren't mistaken for ours.

## Financing a Memo Piece? Check This First
Confirm the financing partner pays the store in full at time of sale — that's usually how the store meets its obligation to the vendor promptly, independent of the customer's own multi-month payment plan.

## The Whole Course, in One Line
Financing (who's paying), disclosure (what we're claiming), AML (can it be traced), memo/consignment (who actually owns it) — four pieces of the same discipline.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Do we carry any memo or consignment pieces right now? How are they flagged so we know at a glance during a sale?
