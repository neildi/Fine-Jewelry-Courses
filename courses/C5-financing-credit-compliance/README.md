# C5 Financing, Credit & Compliance

**Track:** Foundations
**Personas:** P1, P2, P3
**6 articles, 4-6 learner hours**
**Research anchor:** Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Workflows-and-Operating-Systems.md (Synchrony financing workflow, POS compliance rules); Free-Learning-Resources-Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Sales-Leadership.md (CFPB retail installment/BNPL guidance, OCC installment lending handbook, JVC Essential Guides); Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Job-Descriptions-KPIs-Qualifications-Compensation.md (AML/product-handling job-spec language at luxury maisons)

## Terminal outcomes

The learner can:
- Explain how retail installment financing works at the counter, including the two register-level compliance rules (charge only for goods received at time of sale; use Authorization Only/Forced Purchase for future delivery)
- Correctly present financing, layaway, and BNPL as distinct options and match each to the right customer situation
- Explain the FTC disclosure requirements around treatments, lab-grown vs. natural, and advertising claims well enough to avoid making an unsupported promise to a financed client
- Explain, at a foundational level, why jewelry dealers have AML obligations and how Form 8300 (covered in C4) fits into a broader compliance picture
- Correctly distinguish memo and consignment inventory from store-owned stock for financing/compliance purposes, and explain why UCC-1 filings matter for vendor-owned goods

**Assessment:** Observed financing-conversation roleplay; a manager-scored review of a simulated layaway-vs-financing customer conversation; a compliance quiz covering FTC disclosure and AML basics.
**KPI link:** financing attach rate, payment-plan quota attainment, compliance-audit pass rate, chargeback rate on financed sales, memo/consignment reconciliation accuracy.
