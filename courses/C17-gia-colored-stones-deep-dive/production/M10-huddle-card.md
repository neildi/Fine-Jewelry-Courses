# M10 Huddle Card — The Colored Stone Market

**One headline:** Colored stones travel a long pipeline from mine to case, and auction records are not retail prices. Understand the hubs, the labs, and the pricing drivers so you can answer "why does this cost what it costs" without guessing.

**3 key points**
1. **Mining → treatment (Chanthaburi) → cutting (Jaipur for most commercial; specialty cutters elsewhere) → dealing → shows (Tucson, Hong Kong) → auctions/certification → manufacturing → retail.** Each step adds real cost; markup is not 10×.
2. **Color > everything else.** Origin matters — but only when the quality is already there. A pale, included "Burmese" ruby isn't valuable just because of origin; a fine Mozambique ruby beats a mediocre Mogok.
3. **Certificates identify species, treatment, and (where possible) origin; they are expert opinions, not value appraisals, not mine-level traceability, and not proof of ethics.** GIA, GRS, Gübelin, SSEF, AGL, Lotus are credible names.

**1 script to practice today**

When a client brings up an auction record:
"That was a collector stone at the very top of the market — a rare origin, documented history, unheated, and at a size that almost never comes up for sale. The sapphires I'm showing you are beautiful, wearable commercial stones in a completely different price tier; let me show you what your budget buys at the quality level you'd actually want to wear every day."

**1 question to ask the team**

"Which colored stones in our inventory currently have NO lab report but are priced high enough ($3k+ is a reasonable threshold) that they should carry one? Let's make a list and get reports before they go back in the case."
