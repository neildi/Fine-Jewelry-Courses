# M10 Quiz — The Colored Stone Market

**1. Which Thai city is the historic and current global capital of corundum heat treatment and trading?**
a) Bangkok.
b) Chiang Mai.
c) Chanthaburi.
d) Phuket.

**2. Which Indian city is the world's largest colored-stone cutting center, especially known for emerald cutting and a huge jewelry manufacturing ecosystem?**
a) Mumbai.
b) Jaipur (Rajasthan).
c) Delhi.
d) Kolkata.

**3. Which annual February event in Arizona is the world's largest and most important colored-stone wholesale gathering?**
a) The Super Bowl.
b) The Tucson Gem Shows (AGTA, GJX, G&LW, JCK Tucson, and satellite shows).
c) The Arizona State Fair.
d) BaselWorld.

**4. Why don't auction-record prices for Kashmir sapphires (millions per stone) set the retail price for commercial blue sapphires in a jewelry store?**
a) Because jewelers are hiding the good stones.
b) Auction records reflect the very top 0.1% collector tier (the finest unheated stones with documented provenance, rare sizes, and pedigree); commercial Ceylon/Madagascar/Montana sapphires are a different market segment — beautiful, wearable stones at a different price point.
c) Because Kashmir sapphires aren't real.
d) Because auctions use a different currency.

**5. Which two major colored-gem mines does Gemfields operate (emerald and ruby, respectively)?**
a) Cullinan and Premier.
b) Kagem (Zambia, emerald) and Montepuez (Mozambique, ruby).
c) Muzo and Chivor.
d) Mogok and Hpakant.

**6. Which of the following is a correctly matched pair of lab to its base?**
a) GIA = Switzerland; GRS = USA.
b) GIA = USA (with labs worldwide); GRS = Thailand/Switzerland; Gübelin and SSEF = Switzerland; AGL = USA; Lotus = Thailand.
c) GIA = China.
d) All labs are the same.

**7. Which factor is the single most important value driver for colored stones of every species?**
a) Carat weight.
b) Species.
c) Face-up color (hue/tone/saturation).
d) Country of origin.

**8. A customer asks: "Is origin the same as traceability, and does this certificate tell me exactly which mine it came from?" The best answer is:**
a) Yes, any certificate tells you the exact mine and miner.
b) No — origin reports identify country/region of origin (Burma, Ceylon, Mozambique, Colombia, etc.) based on inclusions and chemistry, not the specific mine, and origin is not the same as mine-to-market traceability (which is still rare for colored stones).
c) Only certificates from auction houses tell you the mine.
d) All certificates are fake.

**9. An older colored-stone report from the 1980s accompanies a potentially high-value stone. What is the right approach?**
a) Take it at face value and price the stone based on it.
b) Assume it is wrong without looking.
c) Treat it as background — treatment detection and lab standards have advanced significantly since the 1980s (e.g., beryllium diffusion appeared c. 2001, lead-glass fill in the mid-2000s), and for high-value pieces recommend a fresh report from a modern recognized lab.
d) Throw it away.

**10. When a client says "I heard all jewelry is marked up 10×," which of the following is the most accurate response?**
a) Agree and apologize.
b) Say "markup is actually 20×" to impress them.
c) Describe the real pipeline (mining, treatment, cutting, certification, dealer steps, overhead) and note that for commercial colored stones total retail markup is typically in the 2–3× range from wholesale (far less at the high end where price is driven by scarcity and auction competition), while the very top collector pieces trade on rarity more than cost-plus markup.
d) Refuse to discuss pricing.

---

## Answer key

1. **c** — Chanthaburi.
2. **b** — Jaipur.
3. **b** — Tucson (February).
4. **b** — auction vs. commercial-grade distinction.
5. **b** — Kagem emerald and Montepuez ruby.
6. **b** — GIA (US-based, worldwide), GRS, Gübelin/SSEF (Switzerland), AGL (US), Lotus (Thailand).
7. **c** — face-up color is dominant for every species.
8. **b** — origin ≠ mine-level traceability.
9. **c** — for high-value stones, suggest a fresh modern report, because detection capabilities have advanced.
10. **c** — honest pipeline and margin description, without defensiveness or exaggeration.
