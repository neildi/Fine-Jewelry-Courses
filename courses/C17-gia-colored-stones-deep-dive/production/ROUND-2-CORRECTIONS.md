## Wave 6 (M15–M16) — capstones
- **M15 Pearls:** corrected the build brief, which described Ballerina "chocolate pearls" as a **freshwater** product. GIA's published studies (Wang et al. 2006; Du Toit 2008; the Spring 2016 "pistachio" study) identify them as **treated-color Tahitian cultured pearls** (*Pinctada margaritifera*) processed by a bleaching-type method, distinct from the older silver-nitrate (Ag) dyeing method. The correction is logged in `M15-factcheck.md` and in `VERIFICATION-REVIEW.md` §3.
- **M15 Pearls:** the AAA/AA/A letter scale is presented as unpoliced trade shorthand layered on top of the GIA 7 Pearl Value Factors, not as a standard.
- **M16 Jade:** Type B is framed as a legitimate disclosed product that **degrades**, never as an heirloom material. The 1863 jadeite/nephrite separation date is flagged for verification rather than asserted flatly. Pounamu text kept deliberately general and flagged for cultural review before any use beyond internal training.
