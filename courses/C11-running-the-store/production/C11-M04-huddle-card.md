# C11-M04 Huddle Card: Monthly Reporting, Planning, Inventory Reconciliation

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Monthly discipline is what makes quarterly and annual compliance possible. Skip it now, pay for it later — usually at the worst time.

## The Monthly Business Review
Numbers AND people, together, with district/ownership. You own hitting the monthly budgeted contribution — not just reporting whether it happened. Layaway report + transfer reconciliation by the 25th. Next month's schedule built to payroll budget.

## Rotating Audits = Structure, Not Suspicion
- Different department counted each month
- YOU personally own the diamond (highest-value) count
- Receiving and counting = different people, always
- Genuinely UNSCHEDULED inspections — no advance notice

## The Four-Step High-Value Sequence
1. Two people count INDEPENDENTLY
2. Compare the sheets
3. Third person recounts any DIFFERENCES
4. ONLY THEN adjust the system

Skipping steps defeats the whole point.

## Full Monthly Reconciliation
Run the actual POS physical-inventory count (full/case/partial). The "finish" step gives you a specific missing-item list — THAT'S what drives your audit and verification work, not a separate task.

## This Feeds Quarterly and Annual
Insurer list refresh (quarterly/half-yearly). Annual itemized inventory (7 years, off-premises). Form 8300 statements (Jan 31). None of this works if monthly wasn't done right.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Do we have a mid-month checkpoint for our monthly deadlines, or do we only look at them right before they're due?
