## Claims Reviewed

**1. The fixed-slot, five-report weekly business review model.**
Status: Sourced, within this project's Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Workflows-and-Operating-Systems.md research file, from Gem Logic's "weekly reporting" guidance, a jewelry-specific POS/software vendor source describing this exact structure (fixed slot, ideally Monday morning, same five reports in sequence, completed in under fifteen minutes when nothing unusual surfaces).

**2. The weekly cycle-count model (one category per week; unscheduled spot-checks; the specific discrepancy-investigation sequence).**
Status: Sourced, within the research file, from Gem Logic's "How to do inventory for a jewelry store" guide and JCK's "Winning the Battle Against Internal Theft" trade-press article. Both independently confirm the same core sequence (recount, check repair/approval status, check for scan errors, only then write off) and the "don't automatically attribute disappearances to miscounts, investigate clusters" cultural point, giving this claim solid cross-source corroboration within the research file.

**3. The weekly team meeting content (clienteling accountability, repair take-ins/next-day-due jobs, scheduled training topic).**
Status: Sourced, within the research file, from INSTORE's "How to structure your sales team meetings" article and Southern Jewelry News's "The most powerful words in business" article (the specific gratitude-process accountability point: a dedicated store process, a sending deadline, and genuine accountability for whether thank-you notes actually went out).

**4. The 2-2-2 clienteling cadence.**
Status: Sourced, within the research file, from Clientbook's published 2-2-2 strategy, a jewelry-specific clienteling-software vendor source. IMPORTANT NOTE: this cadence is expected to be developed in significantly greater depth from the associate-execution perspective in this project's separate C3 (Clienteling & CRM) course; this module addresses it specifically and narrowly from the store leader's weekly verification responsibility, which is a distinct but related angle.

**5. The framing of active spot-checking (rather than assuming compliance) as the leader's distinct weekly responsibility.**
Status: This is this module's own reasoned synthesis, extending the leader-vs-associate distinction pattern already established and fact-checked in Module 2 (see C11-M02 factcheck) to the clienteling-cadence-verification context specifically. Not a new externally-sourced claim requiring independent verification.
