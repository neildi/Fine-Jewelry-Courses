# C11-M02 Quiz: The Daily Rhythm: Open, Huddle, Floor, Close

**Instructions:** Answer all questions.

**1. According to JSA crime-timing data cited in this module, what does the pattern of jewelry-store robberies across a normal business day look like?**
A) Robberies only occur during a single, narrow window in the early morning
B) Robberies peaked between 5:00-6:00pm with a secondary peak at 12:00-1:00pm, and there was no 60-minute period during a normal business day when robberies did not occur at all
C) Robberies never occur during business hours, only during opening and closing specifically
D) Timing data shows no meaningful pattern in when jewelry-store robberies occur

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This timing pattern is exactly why the opening/closing sequence is so prescriptive and why floor-level vigilance is treated as an all-day responsibility, not just an opening/closing concern.

C) All staff enter together immediately when arriving, with no sequence required
D) Merchandise is displayed before any security check occurs

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This specific order is confirmed across multiple independent insurer, association, and trade-press sources, and is a genuine security protocol, not an arbitrary preference.

**3. How does a store leader's role in the daily huddle differ from an associate's role?**

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Leader-level huddle ownership is specifically about active facilitation and in-the-moment coaching, not passive participation.

**5. What is the correct order for the closing sequence described in this module?**
