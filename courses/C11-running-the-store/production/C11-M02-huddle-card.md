# C11-M02 Huddle Card: The Daily Rhythm

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Opening and closing are the riskiest moments of the day — real crime-timing data backs this up. The sequence matters, not just the steps.

## Opening — In This Exact Order
1. One person enters, one watches from a safe distance, phone ready
2. All-clear signal given → THEN others enter, door re-locked immediately
3. Merchandise out of the safe — doors still LOCKED
4. Verify every case is locked → THEN doors unlock

## The Huddle Is Yours to Run
Not just attend — RUN it. Name yesterday's big sale. Flag today's specific appointments. Say who needs help and on what. Even on a crazy-busy day, compress it — don't skip it.

## On the Floor All Day
- Cash flashed by a customer = distraction-theft red flag
- Rushed customer pushing to finish fast = warning sign
- Log anything suspicious, every time

## Closing — In This Exact Order
1. Clear ALL customers, lock the door
2. THEN move merchandise to the safe (never the reverse)
3. Never cover a loaded case with a cloth — that's a signal to a burglar, not camouflage
4. Dual sign-off on cash drawer and deposit. Window frames checked. Safe keys separate from premises keys.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Walk through our actual open/close sequence right now. Does it match this order, step for step?
