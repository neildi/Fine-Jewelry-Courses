# C11-M01 Fact-Check: From Sales Floor to Store Leadership: What Actually Changes

## Verification Status: Core leadership-transition claims sourced primarily from named, credible retail-practitioner reflections (not academic/peer-reviewed research); jewelry-specific financial/operational scope independently confirmed across multiple real jewelry-retail job postings

## Claims Reviewed

**1. The core "individual output to team output" shift as the defining transition into store leadership.**
Status: Sourced directly from a LinkedIn post by retail leader Tim Keeley ("Retail Store Manager Job Challenges: Leadership and..."), a named practitioner reflection with an active comment thread including other retail leaders independently corroborating the same experience (one commenter, self-described as a Store Manager for 12 years and 30 years in retail management, calling it "spot on"). Cross-consistent with Mintly's "Retail Career Path: Sales Associate to Store Manager" career guide ("you are no longer responsible only for your own sales; you are helping the entire team perform better"). This is credible practitioner-sourced evidence, not academic research, and is presented as such.

**2. The immediate, no-ramp-up arrival of P&L and financial/operational accountability upon promotion.**
Status: Sourced directly from the same Tim Keeley LinkedIn post ("Product knowledge. People leadership. P&L ownership. No warm-up period... In retail, day one throws all of it at you at once"), cross-consistent with Metaintro's "How to Become a Retail Store Manager" guide (explicitly listing "profit and loss accountability: direct responsibility for store financial performance" as a defining competency) and Silvana Araújo's LinkedIn post on "Store Manager Success in 90 Days" ("nobody teaches you how to become a Store Manager. One day you're a top performer. The next day you're expected to lead people, manage standards, drive sales, solve problems and deliver results").

**3. The jewelry-specific scope of financial/operational accountability (cash handling, inventory accuracy/shrinkage control, vendor relationships, regular reporting to ownership).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across multiple real, current jewelry-retail job postings: Angela Monaco Jewelry's "Fine Jewelry Store Manager" posting (explicit cash controls, inventory accuracy, vendor/consignment handling, and reporting duties to the Founder), Gold Elite Jewelers' "Jewelry Store Manager" posting (cash handling, POS management, stock audits, bookkeeping, payroll, budget oversight), and a Boston-based luxury jewelry retailer's Store Manager posting (P&L partnership with a Director of Stores, inventory and safety-protocol accountability). This is strong, multi-source, real-job-posting corroboration specific to the jewelry retail context, giving this claim solid grounding.

**4. The "first month is for watching, not changing" discipline.**
Status: Sourced directly from a LinkedIn post by retail leader Peter Damilare Oladipo ("6 Lessons for New Store Managers: Leadership and Trust"), which explicitly states this principle. This is a single named-practitioner source, presented as credible experiential wisdom from someone with 17 years in the role (as stated in the post) rather than an academic or empirically validated research finding.

**5. "Trust is built in the first crisis, not the first sale" and "calmest, not smartest" as the key leadership currency.**
Status: Sourced from the same Peter Damilare Oladipo LinkedIn post. Single named-practitioner source, presented as credible experiential wisdom rather than formal research.

**6. The behavior-vs-outcome coaching distinction ("praise the behaviour, not the outcome").**
Status: Sourced from the same Peter Damilare Oladipo LinkedIn post. Single named-practitioner source. This specific coaching principle is flagged for more thorough development and potential cross-corroboration with formal coaching/performance-management research in this catalog's future C13 (Hiring, Coaching & Performance Management) course.

**7. Worked scenario and practice exercise.**
Status: ORIGINAL illustrative synthesis created for this module, not drawn from any external source.
