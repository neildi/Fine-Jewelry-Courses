## Claims Reviewed

**1. Insurance inventory list refresh cadence (quarterly/half-yearly) plus January export of full list and photo archive to broker.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly from this project's own Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Workflows-and-Operating-Systems.md file (section 7.3/8.3), which attributes this cadence to Gem Logic and Jewelers Mutual guidance.

**2. Annual documented itemized physical inventory, retained 7 years, stored off-premises.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly from the same project research file, attributed to Jewelers Mutual guidance.

**3. Form 8300 filing within 15 days of a qualifying cash transaction over $10,000.**
Status: CONFIRMED directly against current IRS.gov sources: the "Instructions for Form 8300" (irs.gov/instructions/i8300) and the IRS's "Understand how to report large cash transactions" and "Form 8300 and reporting cash payments of over $10,000" pages all state the filing deadline is 15 days after the date the cash was received, with the deadline pushed to the next business day if the 15th day falls on a weekend or legal holiday. This directly confirms and supplements this project's own prior research file, which had cited only the January 31 payer-statement deadline without stating the 15-day filing window explicitly.

**4. Form 8300 payer statements furnished to the customer by January 31 of the following year.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly from this project's own Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Workflows-and-Operating-Systems.md file, attributed to the IRS, and consistent with IRS guidance requiring an annual customer notification following Form 8300 filings.

**5. USA PATRIOT Act / Bank Secrecy Act customer identification program and OFAC sanctions-list screening obligations for dealers in precious metals and stones.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly from this project's own Fine-Jewelry-Career-Guide.md file (Global Fine Jewelry Retail Career, Operations, and Competency Framework), which explicitly names these obligations.

**6. G7 sanctions requiring documentary proof of origin for non-industrial diamonds of 0.50 carats and larger, tied to Russian-origin diamond restrictions.**
Status: CONFIRMED as stated in this project's own Fine-Jewelry-Career-Guide.md file. This remains flagged as a MUST VERIFY item before finalizing store policy: international sanctions regimes governing diamond origin (G7/Russian-diamond restrictions) have been subject to phase-in changes and revisions since first introduced, and specific carat thresholds or scope details may have shifted since this project's research was compiled. This module explicitly directs the store leader to the Jewelers Vigilance Committee's Sanctions Resource Center (jvclegal.org/sanctions-resources) as the recommended current reference, consistent with this project's own prior research file's citation of that resource for exactly this purpose.
