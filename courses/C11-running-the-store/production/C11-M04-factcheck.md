## Claims Reviewed

**1. Monthly business review content (joint review of business and people results with district leadership; monthly-budgeted-contribution ownership; the layaway report/transfer reconciliation deadline of the 25th; next month's schedule built to payroll budget).**
Status: Sourced, within this project's Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Workflows-and-Operating-Systems.md research file, from two independent, real, current jewelry-retail job postings: Mejuri's Store Manager posting ("review daily, weekly, monthly business and people results... in partnership with the District Manager") and Days Jewelers' Office Manager posting (the specific 25th-of-month deadlines and payroll-budget scheduling requirement).

**2. Rotating departmental audit model (different department each month; personally-owned high-value/diamond count; receiving separated from counting; job rotation; unscheduled inspections by a senior manager and outside auditor).**
Status: Sourced, within the research file, directly from JCK's internal-theft-prevention guidance, a trade-press source specifically and comprehensively focused on this exact set of internal-control practices.

Status: Sourced, within the research file, from The Edge's "Physical Inventory" product documentation (a dominant jewelry POS vendor's own official guide, describing the three physical-inventory modes and the reconciliation/finish workflow step) and Mejuri's Store Manager posting (confirming the monthly cadence and operations-team partnership specifically).

**5. Quarterly/annual compliance anchors (insurer inventory list refresh each quarter or half-year; annual itemized inventory retained 7 years off-premises; Form 8300 payer statements furnished by January 31).**
Status: Sourced, within the research file, from three independent, directly-on-point sources for three distinct obligations: Gem Logic (quarterly/half-yearly insurer list refresh), Jewelers Mutual's inventory record-keeping guidance (the annual itemized inventory and 7-year off-premises retention requirement), and the IRS Form 8300 reference guide (the January 31 payer-statement deadline, a genuine federal regulatory requirement, not merely industry best practice).
