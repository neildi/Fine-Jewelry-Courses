# C11-M04 Outline: Monthly Reporting, Planning, Inventory Reconciliation

## Module Metadata
- Course: C11 - Running the Store
- Module: 4 of 10
- Persona: P1/P7 (First-Time Store Leader, per official course architecture)
- Working title: Monthly Reporting, Planning, Inventory Reconciliation
- Learning objective: Store leaders can run the monthly business review with district/ownership leadership, execute rotating departmental audits and the four-step high-value verification protocol, run a full monthly inventory reconciliation using POS physical-inventory modes, and connect monthly discipline to quarterly/annual compliance obligations.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: Directly fulfills the C11 terminal outcome "run the monthly reporting, planning, and inventory reconciliation cycle" per this project's official course architecture.

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames the monthly cycle as the timescale for stepping back to see business direction and complete the highest-value verification work.
2. **The Monthly Business Review: Numbers and People, Together** — Joint strategic planning with district/ownership leadership; the monthly budgeted-contribution responsibility; the 25th-of-month deadline examples.
3. **Rotating Departmental Audits: Structural Protection, Not Personal Suspicion** — The rotating-category model, personally-owned high-value counts, receiving/counting segregation, unscheduled inspections.
4. **The High-Value Verification Protocol: A Specific Four-Step Sequence** — Two independent counts, comparison, third-person recount, system adjustment only after.
5. **Monthly Inventory Reconciliation: Full Counts, Not Just Categories** — POS physical-inventory modes, the reconciliation/finish step, missing-item reports driving other audit work.
6. **The Quarterly and Annual Anchors Behind the Monthly Cycle** — Insurer list refreshes, the annual itemized inventory, Form 8300 payer statements, and their dependency on monthly discipline.
7. **A Worked Scenario: The Month-End That Almost Slipped** — Illustrative synthesis showing honest process-gap flagging over a rushed, inaccurate submission.
8. **Practice Exercise** — Mapping monthly deadlines and adding mid-month checkpoints.
9. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Production Notes
- The monthly business review content (joint review of daily/weekly/monthly business and people results with district leadership; the monthly-budgeted-contribution ownership; the layaway report and transfer reconciliation submitted by the 25th; next month's schedule built to payroll budget) is sourced, within this project's Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Workflows-and-Operating-Systems.md research file, from Mejuri's Store Manager job posting and Days Jewelers' Office Manager job posting — two independent, real, current jewelry-retail job postings confirming this pattern.
- The rotating departmental audit model (different department each month, personally-owned high-value/diamond count, receiving separated from counting, job rotation, unscheduled inspections by a senior manager and outside auditor) is sourced, within the research file, from JCK's internal-theft-prevention guidance, a trade-press source specifically focused on this exact set of practices.
- The four-step high-value verification protocol (independent counts, comparison, third-person recount of differences, system adjustment only after) is sourced, within the research file, from Gem Logic's "How to do inventory for a jewelry store" guide, a jewelry-specific software-vendor source describing this exact sequence.
- The monthly inventory reconciliation content (POS physical-inventory modes — full physical, case count, partial scan; the reconcile/finish step; missing-item reports; partnership with an operations team) is sourced, within the research file, from The Edge's "Physical Inventory" documentation (a dominant jewelry POS vendor's own product guide) and Mejuri's Store Manager posting (monthly cycle counts with the Operations team).
- The quarterly/annual compliance anchors (insurer inventory list refresh each quarter or half-year; annual itemized inventory retained 7 years off-premises; Form 8300 payer statements furnished by January 31) are sourced, within the research file, from Gem Logic, Jewelers Mutual's inventory record-keeping guidance, and the IRS Form 8300 reference guide, respectively — three independent, directly-on-point sources for these three distinct compliance obligations.
- The worked scenario and practice exercise are ORIGINAL illustrative syntheses, not externally sourced cases.
