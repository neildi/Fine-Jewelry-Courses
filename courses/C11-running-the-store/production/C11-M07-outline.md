# C11-M07 Outline: Vendor, Repair and Custom Pipeline Management

## Module Metadata
- Course: C11 - Running the Store
- Module: 7 of 10
- Persona: P1/P7 (First-Time Store Leader, per official course architecture)
- Working title: Vendor, Repair and Custom Pipeline Management
- Learning objective: Store leaders can track memo/consignment goods as a distinct liability category, ensure vendor terms are understood by the floor team, maintain pipeline-level (not job-by-job) visibility across the repair and custom-order pipelines using a specific weekly reporting structure, and recognize cross-category dependencies between vendor relationships and the repair/custom pipelines.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: Directly fulfills the C11 terminal outcome "manage the vendor, repair, and custom-order pipeline" per this project's official course architecture.

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames the store leader's role as pipeline-level ownership across three interconnected areas, distinct from the bench/associate-level depth this project's C8 course provides.
2. **Memo and Consignment Goods: A Distinct Liability, Not Just Inventory** — The liability/payment-due-on-sale distinction; POS stock-status tracking accuracy as a leadership responsibility.
3. **Vendor Terms Determine Real Floor Decisions** — Direct connection to C9-M05's vendor-terms content; ensuring floor-wide understanding of terms in effect.
4. **The Repair Pipeline: Visibility Across Every Job, Not Just the One in Front of You** — The specific weekly management report (completed/on-track/overdue); pipeline-level vs. job-by-job management.
5. **Custom Orders: The Same Pipeline Visibility, Higher Stakes** — Applying the same visibility principle to the C8-established custom-design workflow, with emphasis on proactive client updates.
6. **Where Vendor, Repair, and Custom Pipelines Intersect** — Recognizing cross-category dependencies rather than managing each pipeline in isolation.
7. **A Worked Scenario: The Custom Order Waiting on a Vendor** — Illustrative synthesis showing a cross-category dependency caught early through pipeline-level (not job-by-job) review.
8. **Practice Exercise** — Building and running a simple weekly pipeline snapshot across all three categories.
9. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.
