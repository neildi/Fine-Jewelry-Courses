# C11-M10 Outline: The Manager's Own Weekly Scorecard

## Module Metadata
- Course: C11 - Running the Store
- Module: 10 of 10 (final module)
- Persona: P1/P7 (First-Time Store Leader, per official course architecture)
- Working title: The Manager's Own Weekly Scorecard
- Learning objective: Store leaders can build and run a fixed weekly five-number review (turnover with prior-period comparison, net margin, repair pipeline status, customer/old-gold purchase activity, top-and-bottom sellers), track the three course-anchored KPIs (sales vs. plan, shrinkage rate, visual-merchandising audit score), read multi-week trends rather than single-week numbers, and tie scorecard gaps back to specific SOP revisions from Modules 8 and 9.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: Directly fulfills the C11 terminal outcome "build and use their own weekly leadership scorecard" per this project's official course architecture, and directly ties to the course's stated KPI link (sales vs. plan, shrinkage rate, visual-merchandising audit score).

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames the scorecard as the synthesis view pulling together every prior module's health signals into one weekly check.
2. **The Five-Number Weekly Review** — Turnover with YoY/4-week comparison, net margin, repair pipeline (completed/open/overdue), customer and old-gold purchase list, top-ten/bottom-ten sellers.
3. **The Three KPIs This Course Is Built Around** — Sales vs. plan, shrinkage rate, visual-merchandising audit score, each explicitly traced back to the modules that build the underlying discipline (Modules 2-3, 4 and 7, 5 respectively).
4. **Building the Scorecard: One Page, Same Slot, Every Week** — Low-friction, fixed-sequence design applying Module 8's SOP-simplicity principle to the scorecard itself.
5. **Reading the Scorecard, Not Just Filling It Out** — Multi-week trend-reading as the scorecard's real value, with worked examples of shrinkage and merchandising drift.
6. **Tying the Scorecard Back to the Store's SOP Binder** — Explicit closing-the-loop connection to Modules 8 and 9.
7. **A Worked Scenario: Catching a Drift Before It Becomes a Miss** — Illustrative synthesis showing early slow-mover detection via the weekly top-ten/bottom-ten list.
8. **Practice Exercise** — Building and running a four-week scorecard trial.
9. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap, and closes out the course.
