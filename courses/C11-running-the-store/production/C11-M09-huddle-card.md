# C11-M09 Huddle Card: Compliance Calendar: Insurance, Records, Filings

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Compliance misses don't show up as a bad quarter — they show up as an uncovered loss or a federal violation exactly when you can least afford it.

## Two Form 8300 Deadlines, Not One
- **15 days** after any cash transaction over $10,000 — file with the IRS
- **January 31** the following year — furnish the customer's payer statement
These are separate. Track them separately, or the second one will slip.

## Insurance Only Covers What's on the Current List
- Refresh the broker's itemized inventory list at least quarterly
- Full export + photo archive every January
- Full documented physical inventory annually, retained 7 years, stored off-premises

## Every Diamond Intake ≮0.50ct: Check Origin Documentation
G7 rules require proof of origin for qualifying non-industrial diamonds. This happens at intake, every time — not once a year.

## AML/OFAC Isn't Optional
Customer ID program + OFAC sanctions screening applies to every dealer in precious metals and stones. Fold the audit into your existing monthly rotating departmental review.

## Event-Triggered vs. Calendar-Triggered
Some obligations fire off a transaction (Form 8300 filing, origin docs at intake). Others fire off the calendar (insurance refresh, annual inventory, January payer statements). Mixing them up is how deadlines get missed.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
When did we last refresh our insurance schedule with the broker — and do we have a December reminder set to review this year's Form 8300 filings before January 31?
