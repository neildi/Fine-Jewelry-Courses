# C11-M03 Outline: Weekly Counts, Coaching, Client-Book Review

## Module Metadata
- Course: C11 - Running the Store
- Module: 3 of 10
- Persona: P1/P7 (First-Time Store Leader, per official course architecture)
- Working title: Weekly Counts, Coaching, Client-Book Review
- Learning objective: Store leaders can run a fixed-slot weekly business review using the five-report model, execute a weekly cycle-count rhythm with the correct discrepancy-investigation sequence, run a weekly team meeting focused on clienteling and repair-pipeline accountability, and actively spot-check the store's client-book cadence rather than assuming compliance.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: Directly fulfills the C11 terminal outcome "execute weekly cycle counts and case audits" and "run the weekly team meeting" per this project's official course architecture.

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames weekly routines as the timescale where a leader actually steers the store, distinct from daily maintenance and monthly strategy.
2. **The Weekly Business Review: Same Slot, Same Five Reports, Every Time** — The published fixed-slot, five-report model and why consistency matters more than the individual numbers.
3. **Weekly Cycle Counts: The Alternative to the Annual Big-Bang Count** — The one-category-per-week model, unscheduled spot-checks, and the correct discrepancy-investigation sequence.
4. **The Weekly Team Meeting: Accountability, Not Just Announcements** — Clienteling accountability, repair-pipeline review, and the weekly training-topic component.
5. **Client-Book Review: Checking the Cadence, Not Just the Count** — The 2-2-2 clienteling cadence and active spot-checking as a leader's specific weekly responsibility.
6. **A Worked Scenario: The Discrepancy That Wasn't Theft** — Illustrative synthesis showing the correct discrepancy sequence preventing an unnecessary security escalation.
7. **Practice Exercise** — Spot-checking real client records against the clienteling cadence.
8. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Production Notes
- The fixed-slot, five-report weekly business review model (weekly turnover split product vs. gift-card, net profit margin, repairs pipeline status, customer/old-gold purchase data, top/bottom sellers; same slot, ideally Monday morning, under fifteen minutes) is sourced, within this project's Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Workflows-and-Operating-Systems.md research file, from Gem Logic's "weekly reporting" guidance, a jewelry-specific POS/software vendor source describing this exact structure.
- The weekly cycle-count model (one category per week; supplemented by frequent case counts and unscheduled/random spot-checks; the specific discrepancy-investigation sequence of recount, check repair/approval status, check recent sales for a scan error, only then write off as loss) is sourced, within the research file, from Gem Logic's "How to do inventory for a jewelry store" guide and JCK's "Winning the Battle Against Internal Theft" trade-press article, both independently confirming the same core sequence and the "don't automatically attribute disappearances to miscounts" cultural point.
- The weekly team meeting content (clienteling accountability check, repair take-ins and next-day-due jobs, scheduled training topic) is sourced, within the research file, from INSTORE's "How to structure your sales team meetings" article and Southern Jewelry News's "The most powerful words in business" article (the specific gratitude-process accountability point).
- The 2-2-2 clienteling cadence (2-day thank-you, 2-week value-add check-in, 2-month next-purchase prompt) is sourced, within the research file, from Clientbook's published 2-2-2 strategy, a jewelry-specific clienteling-software vendor source; this project's own C3 (Clienteling & CRM) course, when built, is expected to develop this cadence in greater depth from the associate-execution side, while this module addresses it specifically from the store leader's weekly verification responsibility.
- The specific framing of active spot-checking (rather than assuming compliance) as the leader's distinct weekly responsibility is this module's own reasoned synthesis, extending the research file's leader-vs-associate distinction pattern (already used in Module 2) to the clienteling-cadence context.
- The worked scenario and practice exercise are ORIGINAL illustrative syntheses, not externally sourced cases.
