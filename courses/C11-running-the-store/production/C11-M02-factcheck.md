# C11-M02 Fact-Check: The Daily Rhythm: Open, Huddle, Floor, Close

## Verification Status: This module draws directly and extensively on this project's own rigorously-sourced primary research file (Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Workflows-and-Operating-Systems.md), which is itself grounded in insurer guidance, trade-press reporting, and real jewelry-retail job postings; no new external research was required for this module's core claims

## Claims Reviewed

**1. JSA 2025 crime statistics (1,233 crimes against U.S. jewelry firms, down from 1,420 in 2024; total dollar losses rising to roughly $145 million).**
Status: Sourced, within this project's own Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Workflows-and-Operating-Systems.md research file, from National Jeweler and JCK trade-press coverage of the Jewelers' Security Alliance's 2025 crime report.

**2. Robbery-timing data (peak 5:00-6:00pm with 26 events, secondary peak 12:00-1:00pm with 21 events, no robbery-free 60-minute period during a normal business day; grab-and-run thefts clustered 1:00-6:00pm).**
Status: Sourced, within the research file, directly from JSA's own 2024 Annual Crime Report.

**3. The two-person opening/closing sequence (entry/observer pair with an all-clear signal; doors locked during setup; case verification before opening; customers-out-before-merchandise-away at close).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT within the research file across four independent sources: Jewelers Mutual's 24/7 security guidance (republished via The Plumb Club), JSA's retailer security guide (PDF), JCK's security primer ("Security: A Life and Death Issue for Jewelers"), and MN Jewellers Block Guidelines (UK insurance-association guidance). This is strong, multi-source, cross-jurisdictional (US and UK) corroboration of a consistent core protocol.

**4. The daily huddle content (topics covered; leader-level active facilitation vs. associate-level participation).**
Status: Sourced, within the research file, from INSTORE's "How to structure your sales team meetings" trade-press article, which provides the specific sample agenda items ("who needs help today," "always highlight the big sales the day before," etc.) used in this module.

**5. Floor-level security-exception content (cash-flashing as a distraction-theft red flag; suspicious-incident logging).**
Status: Sourced, within the research file, from National Jeweler (quoting JSA directly: "nobody comes into your jewelry store and flashes $10,000 in cash... you're probably going to be the victim of a distraction theft") and JSA's retailer security guide (the Suspicious Incidents Book practice).

**6. Closing-sequence content (the ~70% in-safe overnight benchmark; the "why's that covered" burglar-logic quote; leader-level cash reconciliation and deposit responsibilities).**
Status: Sourced, within the research file, from JCK's "The Most Preventable Crime" article (the 70% in-safe benchmark) and "Security: A Life and Death Issue for Jewelers" article (the covered-showcase point, directly quoting the burglar-logic line), with the leader-level deposit/reconciliation responsibilities confirmed via real job postings (Vallera Jewelers' manager posting explicitly listing "oversees opening and closing procedures, cash reconciliation, and bank deposits"; Days Jewelers' Office Manager posting specifying next-business-day deposit delivery).

**7. Worked scenario and practice exercise.**
Status: ORIGINAL illustrative synthesis created for this module, not drawn from any external source.
