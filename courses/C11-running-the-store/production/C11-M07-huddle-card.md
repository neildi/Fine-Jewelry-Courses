# C11-M07 Huddle Card: Vendor, Repair and Custom Pipeline Management

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** You don't need to track every job closely. You need PIPELINE visibility — how many are on track, how many are overdue, right now, across everything.

## Memo/Consignment ≠ Owned Inventory
It's a liability until sold. Payment is due only after the sale. Track it as its own category — don't let it blend in with owned stock.

## Know the Vendor Terms Before the Floor Does
Minimum purchase commitment? Consignment split? Every associate selling that line needs to know — or they'll make a promise the actual agreement doesn't support.

## Repairs: Three Numbers, Every Week
- Completed / ready for pickup
- Open and WITHIN deadline
- OVERDUE — flag these for immediate follow-up

If you can say these three numbers right now, you're managing the pipeline. If you can only describe individual jobs, you're not.

## Custom Orders: Same Idea, Higher Stakes
Awaiting approval? In production, on schedule? Slipped? And are we proactively updating clients DURING production — not just at pickup?

## Watch the Crossovers
A custom order can be blocked by a VENDOR shipment. A repair can be waiting on a specific part. The real bottleneck isn't always where the job "lives."

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Right now, without checking anything, can you name how many repairs are overdue? If not, that's this week's fix.
