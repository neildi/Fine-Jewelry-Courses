# C11-M01 Huddle Card: From Sales Floor to Store Leadership

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** The job stops being about YOUR numbers and starts being about the TEAM's numbers. That's the whole shift, and it changes everything else.

## What Actually Changes
- Success = team output, not personal output. The selling skills that got you promoted don't automatically make you good at making OTHERS better sellers.
- P&L, cash, inventory shrinkage, vendor relationships, reporting — all arrive on day one. No warm-up period.

## The First Month
WATCH before you CHANGE anything. Every new leader wants to leave a mark in week one. Don't. Learn how the floor actually moves first.

## Trust Isn't Built By Your First Big Sale
It's built by how you handle the first REAL problem — a missed shipment, an angry client, a real mistake. Handle it calmly and fairly. That's what the team is actually watching.

## Calmest, Not Smartest
You don't have to know everything. You have to stay calm when something goes wrong. The team takes its emotional cue from you.

## Coach the Behavior, Not the Score
"Great job hitting target" teaches chasing numbers.
"Great job staying with that customer through the objection" teaches the actual skill.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Think of a real problem that happened recently. How was it actually handled? What would "calm, fact-based, no blame" have looked like instead?
