# C11-M05 Huddle Card: Visual Merchandising and Case Zoning

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** A case is doing two jobs at once — security container AND sales tool. Good merchandising doesn't fight security. It supports it.

## Hot Zones vs. Cold Zones
- HOT (top-right, center): your highest-margin pieces go here — most eyes, most angles
- COLD (bottom-left): statement pieces or steady sellers that don't need help getting noticed

## One Focal Point. Odd Numbers.
Each zone = ONE clear focal point. Multiple competing pieces cancel each other out.
Group in 3s, 5s, 7s — never even numbers. A trio of bangles reads intentional; two pairs of earrings just look sparse.

## The 20/40/40 Rule
- Top 20% of items (priciest/priority): solo space or a real vignette
- Next 40%: low-density trays, real breathing room
- Remaining 40%: tidy, higher-density — but never jammed

## Lighting Is Functional, Not Decorative
Warm white LED, 2700-3000K, high CRI. Angled to kill shadow and glare. This is what makes gold and diamonds actually look like gold and diamonds.

## Don't Cluster Your Slow Movers
Sprinkle them among popular pieces of similar style. A whole case of "slowpokes" looks unwanted, even if each piece is fine on its own.

## Merchandising Supports Security, Not the Other Way Around
An uncluttered case is easier to visually audit AND easier to retrieve one piece from without disturbing five others.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Pick one case right now. Does it have ONE clear focal point? If not, what would you change?
