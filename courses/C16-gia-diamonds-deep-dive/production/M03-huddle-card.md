# M03 Huddle Card — Finding & Mining (one page)
**One-liner:** Deposits are found by their fingerprints (indicator minerals), not by hope; and the industry's modern history is the swing from *carat volume* (South Africa, then Argyle) to *value per carat* (Botswana's big D parcels, Letseng, Namib marine).
**Three facts:** 4.5 billion carats total ever mined worth $300B (Janse 2007); Canada went from discovery to 7th-by-weight/5th-by-value producer in ~4 years (2002); Argyle = 40% of world carats in 1994 — and most of them brown-to-yellow.
**One script:** Client: "Is Canadian diamond better?" → "Better isn't the word — the reports grade identically. Canada's story is provenance: a mine-to-market chain you can trace, plus Arctic mines that choose fewer, whiter parcels. The value difference shows in the certificate you hold, not the passport of the rough."
**Do-not:** treat any "new mine" headline as supply-relevant until steps 4-5 (economic drill results) exist.
