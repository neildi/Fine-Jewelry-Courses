### 2. Pedagogical Overview & Curricular Alignment
Course C16 Module 1 anchors the historical transition from arbitrary merchant terminology to modern, standardized diamond grading. It traces the founding of GIA by Robert Shipley, explains why the color scale deliberately starts at "D" to make a clean break from pre-existing uncalibrated systems, formalizes the metric carat (200 mg) established in 1907, and establishes the difference between insurance appraisals and third-party scientific laboratory reports.
