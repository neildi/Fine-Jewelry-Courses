# M01 Outline — Before the Report: How the 4Cs Were Invented, and the Birth of the Modern Diamond Industry

**GIA assignment sources:** A1 (Beyond the Essentials) + A2 (Birth of the Modern Diamond Industry), 12 citations, 5 digest-grade.
**Digest-grade sources fetched full/partial:** Gilbertson 2016 (4Cs history, full); Scarratt & Shor 2006 (Cullinan centennial, PDF chunk 1/5 + landing); Janse 1995 Africa Part I (PDF, lead section); Shigley Historical Reading Part 1 (full page); Bedini et al. 2012 Vallerano (PDF extract via GIA doc link + indexed snippets).

## Learning objective
The learner can explain where the grading vocabulary came from, why "standard language" was itself the industry's founding invention, and how the 1867–1908 South African sequence (Kimberley diggings → Premier mine → Cullinan → Crown Jewels) created the industrial structures that still set diamond prices today.

## Sections
1. Hook: the client who asks "who decided D was the top?" — and why most pros can't answer.
2. Why this matters: authority, client trust, and the mechanism-first habit.
3. Four centuries of confusion: merchant language pre-1940 (from tincture to "made well"); carat as the only stable term. [Gilbertson]
4. Antiquity's counterexample: the Vallerano ring — value attached to the uncut crystal; Pliny's verdict. [Bedini et al.; Shigley intro]
5. Shipley's invention: GIA 1931, AGS, the mnemonic, the 1949 course text quote, the Babson-Hannaford tours, De Beers adopting the 4Cs only in 1962 (and renaming "purity"→"clarity"). [Gilbertson]
6. 1867–1872: how South Africa moved the world's supply center — Eureka, Star of South Africa, blink klippe, garnet indicators, dry diggings, kimberlite pipes. [Shigley; Janse]
7. The Cullinan case study: discovery, mine economics (Prinsloo deal, 60% Transvaal tax, production figures), the two Crown Jewels stones and their 1990s re-examination; the Premier/Cullinan mine's "large-diamond monopoly" (>25% of all 400+ ct stones). [Scarratt & Shor]
8. Supply, demand and syndicates: Industrial Revolution demand meets a supply shock; the consolidation answer [Janse history; Shipley 1949 title-level].
9. Retire these three myths (De Beers didn't invent the 4Cs; the engagement ring long predates the slogan; "flawless" was not always a word with a definition).
10. Carry it to your counter: three scripts + the "language layer" idea.
11. Media: GIA-01 (History of the 4Cs video) in §5; GIA-02 (color grading video) in §10.
