# M04 Huddle Card — The Market (one page)
**One-liner:** For ~100 years rough was sold at fixed administered prices through De Beers' sights; since 2007 an increasing share clears via tenders and live auctions — so retail price swings are inherited volatility, not your supplier's mood.
**Three facts:** DTC sold 10 six-day sights/yr take-it-or-leave-it and *never cut prices*; De Beers ~75-80% of world rough in the century through ~2000; tenders/auctions give "true market price" *and* volatility — Shor: the evidence supports volatility.
**One script:** "Why is the price different from last year?" → "Rough diamonds used to be priced by one house that never cut prices; now a big share clears at open tenders. Your quote moves with the market now — the grading report doesn't."
**One habit:** when quoting price *trends*, cite mechanism (sights→auctions) not numbers. Never quote 2013-14 price-drop figures from memory.
**Links:** Shor 2014 G&G 50:4 (free PDF) — the definitive 12-page read.
