# M02 Quiz (10Q)
1. Two depth regimes that produce gem diamonds + their names. (**keel 150-200 km lithospheric; 360-750 km superdeep**)
2. Which carbon-family is mostly type Ia, which mostly IIa? (**lithospheric Ia; CLIPPIR/superdeep IIa**)
3. Expand CLIPPIR and the inclusion assemblage that proves it.
4. Why can metallic-iron inclusions exist only from deep, oxygen-poor mantle? (**metal regulates free oxygen; surface rocks never contain metallic Fe**)
5. What did researchers examine instead of gems, and why is that clever? (**polishing offcuts — research-usable waste of high-value parcels**)
6. Two inclusion clocks: what each dates. (**Re-Os in sulfides = formation event; δ13C = carbon source/recycling**)
7. True/False: a GIA report can state a polished diamond's mine. **False — provenance is custody/statistical, not certificate-graded.**
8. What are fibrous black carbonado-like growths made of and where on the market? (**microcrystalline polycrystalline, industrial — most of mined weight never hits retail**)
9. Why are resorbed/irregular shapes *expected* for CLIPPIR? (**partial dissolution by hot mantle fluids post-growth**)
10. One sentence: why large D-VVS1 stones are structurally scarce. (**their origin family is rare, produces large-but-fragmentary stones, and is inclusion-poor by formation medium**)
