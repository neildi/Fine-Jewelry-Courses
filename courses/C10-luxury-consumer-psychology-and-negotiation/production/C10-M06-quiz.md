**1. According to the peer-reviewed academic research cited in this module, what mechanism drives salespeople to provide more attentive service to certain clients?**
A) Salespeople provide equal service to all clients regardless of any visible signals
B) Salespeople attribute a higher purchase budget to clients who display visible signs of status, activating more attentive service based on readily accessible appearance cues rather than anything the client has actually said or done
C) Service quality is determined entirely by random chance with no identifiable pattern
D) Only a client's explicitly stated budget ever affects the level of service they receive

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This documented mechanism means appearance-based assumptions measurably affect service quality, independent of a client's actual purchasing potential.

**2. What did the large-scale Racial Bias in Retail Study find regarding the gap between staff intentions and shopper experience?**
A) Retail employees and shoppers reported identical, fully aligned perceptions of what drove in-store treatment
B) A majority of retail employees cited behavioral attributes as their basis for interactions, while shoppers overwhelmingly reported experiencing appearance-driven treatment — a gap suggesting the bias often operates below conscious awareness
C) The study found no measurable difference in how shoppers were treated based on appearance
D) This study has no relevance to appearance-based bias in any retail context

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This gap is exactly why good intentions alone don't prevent appearance-based bias — it often operates unconsciously, below the level of deliberate awareness.

**3. What is "quiet luxury" or "stealth wealth," and what are some of its real, documented motivations?**

**4. According to research on old money vs. new money status signaling, how does established wealth typically differ from newer wealth in its need to signal status?**
