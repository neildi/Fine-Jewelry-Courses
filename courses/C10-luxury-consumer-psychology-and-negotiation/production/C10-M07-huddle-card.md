# C10-M07 Huddle Card: Cross-Cultural Luxury Buying

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** The core motivations (personal, social, identity) are universal. HOW they're expressed — and which colors/numbers/gestures carry meaning — varies a lot. This is background knowledge, not a script that overrides what a specific client tells you.

## Face Consumption — Social Motivation, Intensified
In Confucian-heritage cultures (China and others), "who else will know and respect this purchase" can carry MORE weight than in many Western contexts. Research shows Chinese consumers are significantly more reference-group-influenced than American consumers on average. This is Module 1's social-oriented motivation, just more pronounced.

## The Gifting Facts Worth Memorizing
- **Four** = avoid in China/Japan/Korea (sounds like "death"). **Eight** = lucky in China (sounds like "prosperity"). **Thirteen** = avoid in the West.
- **Red** = luck/celebration across most of Asia. **White** = mourning across much of East Asia, South Asia, parts of Eastern Europe (opposite of Western bridal-purity association).
- **Right hand only** (or both hands) for passing/receiving in the Gulf and India — never left hand alone.
- An unusually expensive gift can create social PRESSURE in some cultures, not just gratitude.

## Use This as Background, Not a Script
Don't assume every client from a given background wants the identical thing. LISTEN (Module 2 skills) to what THIS client actually says and does. This module just helps you avoid an obvious, avoidable misstep.
