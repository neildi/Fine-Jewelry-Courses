**1. According to foundational cross-cultural consumer research cited in this module, what is generalizable across cultures regarding luxury consumption motivation, and what varies?**
A) Luxury consumption motivations are entirely different in every culture, with no common ground at all
B) The basic motivational drivers of luxury consumption are generalizable across cultures; what varies is how individual consumers perceive, prioritize, and culturally express those same underlying motivations

Explanation: This means Modules 1-2's core motivational framework (personal, social, identity-related) remains the right foundation with any client, while this module adds culturally specific expression on top of it.

**2. What is "face consumption," and what are its three subdimensions?**
A) Face consumption is unrelated to any of the motivational categories covered earlier in this course

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This reframes face consumption as a specific cultural expression of the social-oriented motivation category already established in Module 1, rather than an entirely separate concept.

**3. What did cross-cultural survey research comparing Chinese and American consumers find regarding reference-group influence and face?**
