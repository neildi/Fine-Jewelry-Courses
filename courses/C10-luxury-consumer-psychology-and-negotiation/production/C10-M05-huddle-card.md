# C10-M05 Huddle Card: Negotiating with Confidence

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Don't reflexively discount. PAUSE. ACKNOWLEDGE. DIAGNOSE. Then respond to what's actually going on.

## The Marked Price Is a Real Anchor
Research on 119 negotiation studies confirms it: first offers anchor the whole negotiation. Abandoning your price the SECOND someone pushes back undermines an anchor you already legitimately set.

## The Sequence, Every Time
1. Pause (3-5 seconds, like the silence discipline from C9)
2. Acknowledge: "That's fair to raise."
3. Diagnose: "Is it the number itself, or something else — is this the right piece, do you need to think it through with someone?"

## The Number Is a Symptom
Budget concern? Value uncertainty? Comparing to something else? Needs to justify it to a partner? These call for COMPLETELY different responses. A generic discount often solves NONE of them.

## Concessions: Trade, Don't Give Away
If a concession is genuinely warranted, trade it for something — a commitment today, in exchange for a service add-on instead of a straight discount. Never hand it over unconditionally just to end an awkward moment.

## Say the Price Like You Mean It
No apology. No pre-emptive justification before anyone's even objected. A nervous price presentation invites the exact pushback you're trying to avoid.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice the pause-acknowledge-diagnose sequence out loud with a colleague. Notice how different the follow-up conversation is once you know WHY the price felt off.
