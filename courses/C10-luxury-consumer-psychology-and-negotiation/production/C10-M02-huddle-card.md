# C10-M02 Huddle Card: Reading the Buyer

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Don't just identify the right motivation category — use the client's OWN words back to them. That's where the real trust gets built.

## Three Signals to Listen For
- **Personal:** "This is for me." "I deserve this." (Audience = just them)
- **Social:** "People will notice." "It needs to make a statement." (Audience = other people)
- **Identity/self-completion:** "I've worked for this." "This is who I am now." (About a transition or earned status)

## The Landmark-Event Question
Ask: "What happened that made you want to do something like this for yourself?"
Research shows self-gifts after a BIG life event get measured against that event's significance — not against other jewelry. This question surfaces exactly that.

## Reflect Their Words, Don't Paraphrase
If they say "make a statement" — say "make a statement" back to them. Don't swap in your own vocabulary. Using THEIR specific words is a real, distinct trust-builder.

## When Signals Mix (Which Is Most of the Time)
Don't force one category. Notice which language they keep RETURNING to. Lead with that. Acknowledge the rest — don't ignore it, don't over-serve it.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Recall a recent client conversation. What specific WORDS did they use — not the general idea, the actual words? Could you have reflected those back more directly?
