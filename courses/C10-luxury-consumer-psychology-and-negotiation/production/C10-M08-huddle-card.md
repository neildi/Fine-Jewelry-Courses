# C10-M08 Huddle Card: Capstone — A Full Luxury Negotiation Simulation

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift / Pre-Simulation Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Every skill from this course has to hold up TOGETHER, in one real conversation — not one at a time in isolation.

## The Seven-Point Checklist (Run Through This Before Any Real Negotiation)
1. **Motivation (M1-M2):** Personal, social, or self-completion? Am I listening for THEIR words, not assuming?
2. **Scarcity/Exclusivity/Proof (M3):** Would every claim I'm about to make survive the client checking it?
3. **Anchoring (M4):** Is my first price real? Am I avoiding any fake "decoy" option?
4. **Negotiation (M5):** Pause. Acknowledge. Diagnose. Trade concessions, don't give them away.
5. **HNW/Discretion (M6):** Am I making ANY assumption based on how this client looks?
6. **Cross-Cultural (M7):** If relevant, am I using this as background awareness — or as a rigid assumption overriding what they're actually telling me?
7. **All Together:** Does the WHOLE conversation feel honest AND effective at the same time?

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Think of the hardest real negotiation you've had recently. Which of these seven points would have been hardest to hold onto under real pressure?
