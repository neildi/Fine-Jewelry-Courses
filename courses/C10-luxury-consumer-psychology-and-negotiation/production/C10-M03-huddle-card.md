# C10-M03 Huddle Card: Scarcity, Exclusivity, and Social Proof

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** These three principles are real and powerful. Cialdini's own research found they work whether they're TRUE or FAKED. That's exactly why faking them is a real ethical line, not just a style choice.

## The Verifiability Test
Before you say ANY scarcity, exclusivity, or "popular" claim, ask: could this survive the client checking it themselves?
- "We only have three left, and the designer isn't making more" — PASSES. Specific, true, checkable.
- "These are flying off the shelves" (when they're not) — FAILS. Vague, unverifiable.

## Scarcity: Genuine Only
Real scarcity (discontinued line, one-of-a-kind piece, limited designer run) creates honest urgency on its own. No embellishment needed. Be SPECIFIC about what's limited.

## Exclusivity: Don't Oversell It
Real exclusivity (a true one-of-a-kind piece, a genuine limited designer relationship) — describe it honestly. Don't imply something is more exclusive than it actually is.

## Social Proof: Watch Who You're Talking To
"This is our most popular piece" reassures SOME clients and REPELS others — especially clients who see themselves as having real expertise or who specifically don't want what everyone else has. Read the client (Module 2 skills) before reaching for this one.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Think of one item in our current inventory with a genuinely true scarcity or exclusivity fact. Draft the honest, specific way to say it out loud.
