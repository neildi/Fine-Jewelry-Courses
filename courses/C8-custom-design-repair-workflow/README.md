## Terminal outcomes

The learner can:
- Place an incoming job on the repair-to-custom-design continuum and apply consistent intake, tracking, and communication discipline regardless of complexity
- Run a complete, documented intake process that sets accurate client expectations and protects the store if a piece's condition is later questioned
- Explain the CAD-to-casting workflow well enough to guide a client through a fully custom design project from concept to finished piece
- Correctly scope, time-estimate, and explain the most common bench repairs (sizing, retipping, re-shanking, stone replacement, chain/clasp repair)
- Price repair and custom work accounting for labor, materials, and margin, and explain that pricing transparently to a client
- Run a redesign/remounting consultation that respects a client's heirloom materials while setting honest expectations about what's achievable
- Apply a consistent quality-control standard before any repair or custom piece is handed back to a client
- Manage a full repair and custom-design pipeline across multiple simultaneous jobs at varying complexity and stage

**Assessment:** Observed intake roleplay (condition documentation, scope-setting, and client communication for a sample job); a manager-scored review of a written repair/custom-work price quote for three job scenarios; a capstone pipeline-management simulation across a mixed batch of jobs (delivered in Module 8).
**KPI link:** repair-to-sale conversion rate, average repair ticket value, custom design pipeline volume and cycle time, on-time completion rate, redo/comeback rate.
