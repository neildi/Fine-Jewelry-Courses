# C8-M02 Outline: Intake: Documenting Condition, Setting Expectations, and the Job Ticket

## Module Metadata
- Course: C8 - Custom Design & Repair Workflow
- Module: 2 of 8
- Persona: P1
- Working title: Intake: Documenting Condition, Setting Expectations, and the Job Ticket
- Learning objective: Associates can conduct a magnified, multi-angle pre-existing-condition inspection before accepting a piece, take a consistent evidentiary photo sequence, write specific rather than vague condition notes, assemble a complete intake record, and correctly explain what a liability waiver does and doesn't cover.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Run a complete, documented intake process that sets accurate client expectations and protects the store if a piece's condition is later questioned."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames intake as both the best liability protection and the foundation for accurate estimates, building directly on Module 1's job-ticket framework.
2. **Inspect Before You Accept: The Pre-Existing Condition Check** — Magnified inspection targets (thinning metal, porosity, stress fractures, worn prongs, hidden solder seams); stone-specific pre-existing-chip documentation; the inspect-rotate-confirm-document principle.
3. **What to Photograph, and When** — The four-shot sequence (overall, repair-area/damage, hallmarks/inscriptions, magnified stone detail); before/after photo pairing.
4. **Writing Down What You See: Condition Notes That Actually Hold Up** — Specific, located, neutral condition-note language vs. vague notes; recording the client's own stated concern.
5. **The Complete Intake Record** — Full checklist synthesis (contact/order info, item description, stated concern, condition/photos, quote/deposit/balance, signature).
6. **The Liability Waiver: What It Actually Does and Doesn't Do** — Negligence-based liability limitation; capped liability; exclusion for undisclosed pre-existing conditions/treatments; why documentation is what actually activates waiver protection.
7. **A Worked Scenario: The Chip the Client Didn't Notice** — Illustrative synthesis of proactively disclosing and documenting a pre-existing defect discovered during inspection.
8. **Practice Exercise** — Full intake roleplay (inspection, photo sequence, condition-note writing, proactive disclosure).
9. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Production Notes
- The magnified pre-existing-condition inspection targets (thinning metal, porosity, stress fractures, worn/bent prongs, hidden solder seams, structural fatigue; stone-specific pre-existing chip/abrasion/inclusion documentation) are sourced directly from Stuller's "6 Tips for Metal, Diamond, & Gemstone Intake Every Bench Jeweler Should Know" — Stuller is a major jewelry-industry supplier and trade resource, making this a credible, directly-on-point primary trade source.
- The "inspect, rotate, confirm, and document" operating principle and the specific inspection checklist (condition review, hallmark/maker's-mark inspection, prong/stone-seating check, post-repair verification) are sourced directly from Smart G-Scope's jewelry inspection checklist, a detailed, purpose-built pre/post-repair visual workflow guide.
- The four-shot photo sequence (overall item, repair-area/damage close-ups, hallmark/inscription photos, magnified stone-detail photos) and the before/after pairing practice are CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across three independent sources: JewelIntake's intake-process guide, Luxare's mail-in-repair workflow guide, and LoupeLens' smartphone gemstone-inspection guide, all describing highly similar photo sequences and the specific practice of repeating identical shots after work completion.
- The specific, located, neutral condition-note language standard is sourced from DealSpeak.ai's repair-intake-coaching guide, which explicitly models this exact note-writing approach (naming a location, a visible feature, and a neutral observation) as a coaching best practice.
- The complete intake record checklist (customer/order details, item description, stated concern/requested work, condition/photos, quote/deposit/balance, signature) is CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across JewelIntake's ten-step checklist and Luxare's mail-in intake-form field list, both independently describing highly overlapping required intake elements.
- Liability waiver mechanics (negligence-based liability limitation; liability capped at the lesser of stated estimated value or repair/replacement cost; explicit exclusion for undisclosed pre-existing conditions and gem treatments) are sourced directly from three real, primary repair-agreement/waiver documents: Schumacher Diamond Cutters' disclaimer form, Krikawa Jewelry Designs' repair agreement form, and a sample form from ISI Print's custom-samples document (Romm's jewelry store's actual agreement language), all independently using closely matching negligence-based, capped-liability, and pre-existing-condition-exclusion language. This is a strong, multi-source confirmation of a consistent industry-standard waiver structure.
- The worked scenario and practice exercise are ORIGINAL illustrative syntheses, not externally sourced cases.
