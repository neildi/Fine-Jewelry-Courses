# C8-M03 Huddle Card: The CAD-to-Casting Workflow

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** The timeline isn't arbitrary. An 8-12 hour kiln burnout doesn't speed up just because a client is in a hurry — the physical process itself is your best, most honest explanation.

## The Five Stages (Plain-Language Script)
1. **Pattern** — CAD file becomes a physical wax/resin pattern (3D-printed or hand-carved)
2. **Investing** — pattern gets a sprue, goes into a flask, plaster poured around it
3. **Burnout** — kiln melts the wax away entirely (~8-12 hours) — this is why it's called "lost wax"
4. **Casting** — molten metal forced in (centrifugal or vacuum) to fill every detail, even thin prongs
5. **Finishing** — quench, break away plaster, cut sprues, file/set stones/polish

## Direct Wax vs. Resin Printing
Direct wax = ready to cast immediately, cleaner burnout. Resin = needs UV curing first, more burnout risk. Know which your shop/vendor uses.

## Where Defects Actually Come From
- **Porosity** → trapped air during investing
- **Incomplete fill** (thin/missing prongs) → metal cooled too fast, not enough casting force
- **Investment inclusions** → cracked plaster shell during the pour
None of these are visible until finishing — that's why QC (Module 7) matters.
