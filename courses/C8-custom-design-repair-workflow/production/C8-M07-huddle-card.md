# C8-M07 Huddle Card: Quality Control and the Client Handoff

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Don't skip steps because the first few checks looked fine. Run the FULL sequence, every time, on every piece — busy day or not.

## The Four-Part Final Inspection
1. **Stone security** — gentle lateral pressure on EVERY stone, loupe in hand. Prongs uniform, tips smooth.
2. **Surface/finish** (4x-10x magnification) — porosity, tool marks, solder marks, unexpected scratches
3. **Function** — clasps/hinges cycle-tested, not just opened once. Ring size verified on a mandrel.
4. **Spec match** — compare to the ORIGINAL intake photos and agreed scope. Does it actually match what was approved?

## Warranty Talk — Be Precise
- Covers: OUR workmanship defects (a solder joint we did failing, a clasp we installed breaking)
- Does NOT cover: normal wear, pre-existing conditions, damage from misuse, third-party service (often VOIDS it entirely)
- "Lifetime" often needs scheduled inspections (commonly every 6 months) to STAY valid — say this clearly
- Remedy is usually capped: re-do the work or refund the service fee — not unlimited replacement

## The Handoff Is Not Just "Here You Go"
- Walk through intake photos vs. finished piece
- Give specific care guidance (cleaning, rhodium timeline, chemical exposure cautions)
- Explain the warranty accurately — don't oversell it
- For custom/redesign pieces: slow down. This might be the client's actual "reveal" moment.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice narrating the four-part inspection out loud on your next repair — even a simple one — so it becomes automatic, not something you only do when you remember to.
