# C8-M02 Huddle Card: Intake

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Document condition BEFORE you accept a piece, not after. "Inspect, rotate, confirm, document" — every single time, no exceptions.

## The Magnified Inspection Checklist
- Thinning metal, porosity, stress fractures
- Worn or bent prongs
- Hidden solder seams (signs of earlier repair)
- For stones: existing chips, abrasions, inclusions — BEFORE any work starts

## The Photo Sequence (Same Every Time)
1. One clear overall shot
2. Close-up of the repair area + any existing damage
3. Hallmarks/maker's marks/inscriptions
4. Magnified stone detail (10x loupe + phone camera works)
→ Repeat the SAME shots, SAME angles, SAME lighting AFTER the work is done.

## Write This, Not That
- ❌ "Some wear noted"
- ✅ "Shank at 6 o'clock position shows visible thinning; north prong bent ~2mm"

## The Complete Record
Customer/order info + item description + client's stated concern (their words) + condition notes + photos + quote/deposit/balance + signature.

## What a Waiver Actually Covers
Negligence only, usually capped at estimated value. Does NOT cover pre-existing conditions or gem treatments you didn't disclose at intake. The WAIVER is only as strong as the DOCUMENTATION behind it.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice the exact phrase you'd use to point out a pre-existing chip to a client BEFORE accepting the piece — calm, factual, not alarming.
