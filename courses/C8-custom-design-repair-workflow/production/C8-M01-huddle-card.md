# C8-M01 Huddle Card: The Repair and Custom Design Continuum

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Repair and custom design aren't two different jobs — they're one continuum. Same job ticket system, same communication cadence, same bench jeweler, whether it's a 5-minute sizing or a 10-week custom build.

## The Continuum
1. **Simple bench repairs** — sizing, retipping, clasp fix (same-day to a few days)
2. **Moderate repairs/restoration** — re-shanking, stone replacement, antique restoration
3. **Redesign/remounting** — existing materials (often heirloom stones) reworked into something new
4. **Fully custom design** — brand new piece, CAD to casting to finish

## Every Job Needs the Same Core Intake
- Unique job ticket
- Clear scope of work
- Photos of starting condition
- Tracked status: Intake → Awaiting Approval → In Progress → Quality Check → Ready for Pickup

## The Same Communication Rhythm, Every Time
Intake confirmation → approval (if scope/price needs sign-off) → updates if anything changes → completion notice → pickup reminder.

## Chain of Custody Matters at EVERY Stage
Every handoff — counter to safe, store to outside specialist, back again, to the client — gets logged against the SAME ticket. This is how you answer "where's my ring?" with confidence, every time.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Next time a "simple" job turns out to be bigger than expected, practice pausing and getting documented client sign-off before doing the extra work — don't just proceed.
