# C8-M06 Huddle Card: Redesign Consultations

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Document everything BEFORE you touch it. Recutting and melting are irreversible — once done, there's no going back to "before."

## Before Anything Else
- Photograph every side, every mark, every existing setting
- Confirm WHO has decision-making authority (heirlooms often belong to more than one person, emotionally or practically)

## The Discovery Questions (Ask All Five)
1. History — who wore it, what does it mean?
2. What MUST be preserved — the stone? The setting? Just the memory? (Ask, don't assume)
3. Lifestyle — how will this actually be worn?
4. Combining — if multiple pieces, which specific elements matter?
5. Timeline/budget — including any assessment needed before a firm price

## Recutting — Say the Real Numbers
- Round-to-round recut: ~15% carat weight loss
- Cushion (old mine) to round: up to ~40% weight loss
- PLUS a real risk of breakage on the wheel — that's separate from the weight loss
- Recutting is irreversible AND often REDUCES value to the collectors who'd pay the most for the original character
- Default recommendation: reset as-is first. Recommend an independent second opinion before any real recut.

## Melting Isn't the Only Option
Hand-disassembly + mechanical reassembly ("art of assembly") keeps the actual original metal intact — worth mentioning when a client is attached to the metal itself, not just the stones.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice asking "what specifically needs to be preserved here?" instead of assuming it's the stone or the setting.
