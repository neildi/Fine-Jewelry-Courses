# C8-M08 Huddle Card: Capstone — Managing the Pipeline

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Blocked jobs and queued jobs are NOT the same thing. Flag what's stuck. Focus bench time on what can actually move.

## The Pipeline Stages (Everyone Should Know Every Job's Stage)
1. Backlog/intake — received, not yet triaged
2. Scheduled — triaged, queued, has a target date
3. In progress — actively being worked (with sub-flags: waiting on parts/stones, waiting on approval, at outside vendor)
4. QC — at the bench, awaiting final inspection (Module 7)
5. Ready for pickup — inspected, documented, waiting on the client

## Prioritize on More Than "Who Arrived First"
- Promised due date
- What's actually blocking it (don't keep revisiting a stuck job — flag it and move on)
- Right technician for the job's specific skill demand
- Comeback/previously-delayed jobs get real precedence — that client already waited once

## Don't Book Every Hour
Leave ~10-20% capacity as buffer. Something always comes up once a job's actually on the bench — approval delays, extra repair needed, a stone that doesn't fit. Zero slack = one delay becomes everyone's delay.

## Communicate at Every Real Status Change
Approved, delayed, ready — every one of these should trigger an update. Don't make clients call to ask "is it done yet?"

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Look at today's active jobs as a team. Which ones are truly blocked right now vs. just next in line? Say it out loud.
