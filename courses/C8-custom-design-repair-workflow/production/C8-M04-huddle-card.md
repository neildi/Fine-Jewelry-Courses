# C8-M04 Huddle Card: Common Bench Repairs

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** "Same-day" is honest for simple, stone-free jobs. It's NOT honest for anything with stones, structural rebuilding, or platinum. Don't promise what the process can't safely deliver.

## Ring Sizing Cheat Sheet
- **Down-sizing** (removing metal): faster, 2-4 days for plain gold, sometimes same-day
- **Up-sizing** (adding metal): slower, 7-14 days for 1.5+ size changes (sourcing matching metal takes time)
- **Metal matters**: gold/silver = fastest | platinum = slower, more skill needed | tungsten = usually can't resize at all
- **Stones matter**: solitaire = simplest | pavé/halo = 5-14 days (careful heat + prong recheck) | full eternity = often NOT resizable

## Prong Work
- **Retipping** (adding metal to a worn tip): 1-5 business days
- **Rebuilding** (broken/severely worn prong): similar range, can run longer for complex settings
- Process: assess → clean → apply metal → finish/polish → final security check

## Chain & Clasp — Fastest Category
- Single link re-solder: 15-25 min
- Multi-link replacement: 45-75 min
- Clasp swap: 20-40 min
(This is BENCH time — total turnaround with queue/shipping is often longer. Say both numbers.)

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice explaining WHY a specific job takes the time it does — direction, metal, stones, design detail — instead of just stating a number.
