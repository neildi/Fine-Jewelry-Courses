# C7-M10 Huddle Card: Capstone — Running a Buying Event

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** A long line is NOT a reason to skip a single step. Every module's standard — ID checks, authentication, honest valuation, documentation — applies to EVERY client, even the 50th one in line.

## Two Ways to Run an Event
- **Partner-run** (National Rarities-style): they market it, bring specialist buyers, pay you ~10% commission. Documented ~175 extra visits per event. Lower effort, shared margin.
- **In-house**: you keep 100% margin, but you handle marketing, staffing, security, and cash planning yourself. Needs real in-house expertise.

## Before the Event Starts
- Marketing: direct mail + digital + social, planned weeks ahead
- Staffing: plan for MORE volume than you expect, not less
- Cash: talk to your bank in advance about reserves and same-day replenishment
- Insurance: confirm your coverage actually extends to event-level cash/inventory volume — don't assume

## During the Event — Nothing Changes
- Module 6's testing protocol (thermal → electrical, XRF where warranted): still every time
- Module 8's ID/documentation/reporting: still every transaction
- Module 9's conversation sequence (emotion → valuation → upgrade): still every client

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Picture the busiest possible day. Name the ONE step you'd be most tempted to rush — then commit out loud to not rushing it.
