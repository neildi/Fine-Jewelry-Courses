# C7-M09 Huddle Card: The Trade-In Sales Conversation

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Emotion first, honest valuation second, upgrade opportunity LAST. Never skip ahead. A client once smashed his own wedding band on the counter with a hammer rather than just handing it over — read the room before anything else.

## The Sequence (Never Skip Steps)
1. **Read the emotional register.** Is this transactional, or tied to a divorce/death/big life change? Listen more than you talk. Move somewhere private if needed.
2. **Offer to document the story.** "Want me to take a few photos together, so you have a record?" — genuinely helps clients process letting go of a piece.
3. **Deliver the valuation honestly** (Module 4's structure — explain, don't just state the number). Flag consignment/auction (Module 7) if the piece deserves it, even if that means no immediate sale.
4. **THEN, if appropriate, raise the upgrade.** Assumptive and confident, not a tentative ask: "Let me show you a couple pieces in that range I think you'd love" — not "would you like to see anything else?"

## Why This Matters for the Business
A trade-in toward an upgrade is a BETTER transaction than a standalone sale of similar size. That's why upgrade-credit programs exist. But it only works if steps 1-3 happen first — rushing to the pitch kills trust and the sale.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice the exact phrase you'd use to offer to photograph a piece's story before evaluation — make it sound natural, not scripted.
