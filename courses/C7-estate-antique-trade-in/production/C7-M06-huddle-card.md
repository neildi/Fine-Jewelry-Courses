# C7-M06 Huddle Card: Authentication

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** A thermal "diamond" reading is NOT proof of diamond — it just rules out CZ and glass. Moissanite passes thermal tests all the time. ALWAYS follow up with electrical conductivity on anything with real value at stake.

## The Metal Rule
Acid test = surface only. Even a whisper of gold plating passes it, hiding a brass or tungsten core underneath.
**XRF = the real answer.** Scan 2-3 points on every piece (band + interior + setting for a ring). One scan can miss a localized repair or plating job.

## The Stone Rule (Two-Step, Always)
1. Thermal tester → rules out CZ/glass, NOT moissanite
2. Electrical conductivity tester → THIS is what separates diamond from moissanite (diamond = insulator, moissanite = semiconductor)
**Neither test tells you natural vs. lab-grown.** That needs a lab. Don't overclaim.

## Fraud Patterns to Watch For
- Stone doesn't match the setting's actual prong/bezel dimensions (substitution)
- Hallmark too crisp, wrong spacing, or stamped over solder (falsified)
- Modern casting/laser welds on a "genuine antique" claim
- Glued stones (adhesive residue, shallow seat, shifts under gentle pressure)

## If You Find Something
Document first. Talk to the client factually, no accusations — they may not know either. Escalate to a manager. Never let convenience decide the finding.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice saying out loud: "A positive diamond reading on a thermal tester doesn't guarantee diamond — it only rules out the easy fakes."
