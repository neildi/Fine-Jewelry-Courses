# C7-M01 Huddle Card: The Secondary Market

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Estate = who owned it before. Vintage = roughly 20-100 years old. Antique = 100+ years old, no exceptions. Vintage-style = brand new, just looks old. Four different words, four different meanings — don't mix them up with clients.

## The Quick Reference
- **Estate** — previously owned, ANY age (could be brand new or ancient)
- **Vintage** — roughly 20-100 years old
- **Antique** — 100+ years old (hard threshold, no debate)
- **Vintage-style** — newly made, old-looking design. NOT actually old. Say "-style" every time.

## Trade-In Isn't a Category — It's a Deal Structure
A client's piece (any age, any category) gets assessed and applied as credit toward a new purchase. That's it. Don't confuse it with "estate" or "antique."

## Why This Matters for the Business
The secondary/resale jewelry market is genuinely big and growing (~9-10% annual growth by most estimates). Clients bringing in old pieces or asking about pre-owned options are a real, growing part of our business — not an inconvenience.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Next time a client uses one of these four words loosely, practice gently and respectfully clarifying which one actually applies — without making them feel corrected or embarrassed.
