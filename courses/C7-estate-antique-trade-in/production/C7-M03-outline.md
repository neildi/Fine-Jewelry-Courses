# C7-M03 Outline: Dating and Attribution: Putting the Clues Together

## Module Metadata
- Course: C7 - Estate, Antique & Trade-In
- Module: 3 of 10
- Persona: P2
- Working title: Dating and Attribution: Putting the Clues Together
- Learning objective: Associates can conduct a systematic loupe-based examination of a piece, weigh independent lines of evidence to state a properly qualified attribution claim, recognize married jewelry and treat its components distinctly, handle genuinely conflicting evidence without forcing false certainty, and recognize when a piece warrants referral to outside expertise.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Date and attribute a piece using period style, construction technique, and hallmark/makers'-mark evidence." (synthesis/application portion, following Module 2's evidence-type foundation)

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames this module as the synthesis discipline built on Module 2's three evidence types.
2. **The Systematic Examination Process** — 10x triplet loupe standard, technique, sequential examination order, documentation-throughout practice.
3. **Weighing Evidence: Building Toward a Confident Conclusion** — Precise testable claims; weighing independent vs. repeated evidence; qualified date ranges over invented precision; distinguishing whole object from components.
4. **Married Jewelry: When a Piece Isn't All One Thing** — Definition, legitimate history, why components may need separate dating/valuation and honest disclosure.
5. **When the Evidence Conflicts** — Preserving disagreement rather than forcing certainty; testing whether a conflict is real; listing plausible explanations before concluding.
6. **When to Bring in Outside Expertise** — Recognizing limits of in-house attribution; referral triggers; forward reference to Module 7.
7. **A Worked Scenario: The Conflicting Ring** — Illustrative synthesis of honestly distinguishing a married piece's components rather than forcing a uniform date.
8. **Practice Exercise** — Systematic examination sequence practice plus qualified-claim-statement practice.
9. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Production Notes
- The 10x triplet loupe standard (three lenses correcting chromatic/spherical aberration; 10x as the GIA/gemological industry standard for hand identification) is CONFIRMED CONSISTENT AND UNANIMOUS across six independent sources (CaratX, GemTrue, International Gem Society, JYB Selected, AlfaMirage, manuals.plus/loupe guide), all agreeing on 10x as the standard with 14x/20x as supplementary diagnostic tools.
- Loupe technique (holding 1-2 inches from eye, bringing object to loupe not loupe to object, low-angle directed lighting, systematic table-crown-pavilion-girdle examination sequence) is corroborated across GemTrue, All Diamond UK, and manuals.plus, all describing matching technique with consistent specific distances and sequencing.
- The evidence-weighing framework (stating precise testable claims; weighing independent vs. derivative/repeated evidence sources; using qualified date ranges rather than invented precision; distinguishing whole object from components) is adapted from Collectaneum's general provenance-evidence methodology for collectibles, which is not jewelry-specific but describes a rigorous, transferable evidence-discipline framework directly applicable to jewelry attribution; this module applies that general framework to the specific jewelry-dating context established in Module 2.
- Married jewelry definition and its legitimate, non-fraudulent history is sourced directly from Lang Antiques' "Married Jewelry" glossary entry (Lang Antiques' Antique Jewelry University is a well-established, specifically jewelry-trade-focused reference source, also cited in this course's research anchor documents).
- The conflicting-evidence discipline (test whether the conflict is real, list plausible explanations, document rather than force certainty, recognize when a conclusion should remain open) is adapted from Collectaneum's provenance-conflict methodology, applied here to jewelry-specific attribution scenarios rather than the general collectibles context it was originally written for.
- The worked scenario and practice exercise are ORIGINAL illustrative syntheses, not externally sourced cases.
