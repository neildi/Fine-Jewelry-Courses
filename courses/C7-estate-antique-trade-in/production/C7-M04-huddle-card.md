# C7-M04 Huddle Card: Trade-In Evaluation

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Trade-in offers are built from WHOLESALE and MELT value, never from what the client originally paid at retail. That gap is normal — explain it, don't apologize for it.

## The Melt Value Formula (Know This Cold)
**Weight (grams) × Purity (decimal) × Spot price per gram = Melt value**
- Spot price is quoted per troy ounce (31.1035g) — divide by that to get per-gram price
- This is the objective FLOOR value for the metal — no guesswork

## Diamond Reality Check
- Natural diamonds: typically recover ~20-40% of retail on resale
- Lab-grown: often close to zero (low, falling wholesale cost)
- This is structural — true of EVERY diamond, not a flaw in this specific one

## The Four-Part Offer
1. Metal melt value
2. Stone wholesale value (not retail!)
3. Condition adjustment (up for great condition/brand, down for damage)
4. Store's margin requirement (rough rule of thumb: ~50% of realistic pre-owned resale)

## The Conversation That Actually Works
Don't just say the number. Say the STRUCTURE:
"Here's how we get to this number..." → markup explanation → wholesale basis → specific factors for THIS piece.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice calculating melt value out loud for a hypothetical piece, then practice explaining a below-expectation offer using the structure, not just the number.
