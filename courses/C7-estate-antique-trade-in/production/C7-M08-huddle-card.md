# C7-M08 Huddle Card: Legal and Compliance Considerations

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Holding periods and reporting deadlines are NOT the same everywhere. Know YOUR jurisdiction's specific numbers — don't assume a figure from a different state applies to us.

## Federal AML — The Threshold to Know
$50,000+ per year in precious metals/gems (50%+ of price) — on EITHER the buying side or selling side — triggers a required written AML program. No SAR filing required under this specific rule (that's a separate thing).

## State/Local Rules — General Pattern (CONFIRM YOUR OWN JURISDICTION)
- Police reporting: often within 24 hours
- Holding period before resale/alteration: commonly 15-30 days (varies!)
- Police can add a hold order (often up to 90 days) even after the standard period ends, on probable cause of theft
- Record retention: multi-year, commonly 3 years

## LeadsOnline
The industry-standard reporting platform. Your upload connects to nationwide stolen-property databases (including NCIC). This is how stolen pieces actually get caught — sometimes long after the sale.

## The Complete Buy-Sell Record
1. Verified government ID
2. Specific, detailed item description
3. Photos BEFORE any cleaning/repair
4. Price + payment method
5. Date and time

## Form 8300 ≠ AML Program
Form 8300 = cash over $10,000 in a transaction. Separate rule, separate purpose. Don't mix them up.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Look up (or ask a manager) exactly what OUR store's local holding period and reporting requirement actually is — don't rely on a general estimate.
