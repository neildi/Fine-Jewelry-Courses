# C7 Estate, Antique & Trade-In

**Track:** Specialist
**Personas:** P1, P2, P3
**10 articles, 8-10 learner hours**
**Research anchor:** Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Job-Descriptions-KPIs-Qualifications-Compensation.md; Fine-Jewelry-Career-Guide.md; Free-Learning-Resources-Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Sales-Leadership.md; secondary-market and trade-in program research; period-style/construction/hallmark identification research; examination/attribution methodology research; trade-in valuation research; estate/trade-in appraisal-type research; authentication and testing-equipment research; consignment and auction pathway research; legal/compliance research (FinCEN primary AML guidance, 31 CFR 1027.210, Florida/California/Wisconsin/Los Angeles secondhand-dealer statutes, LeadsOnline platform documentation)

## Terminal outcomes

The learner can:
- Precisely distinguish estate, vintage, antique, and vintage-style jewelry, and explain trade-in as a distinct transaction structure
- Date and attribute a piece using period style, construction technique, and hallmark/makers'-mark evidence
- Evaluate a trade-in piece and set a fair offer that protects margin without alienating the client
- Explain the differences between fair market value, retail replacement value, and liquidation value, and produce the correct appraisal type for a given purpose
- Identify common signs of reproduction, alteration, or outright fakes in estate and antique pieces
- Decide when a piece belongs in-house (resale, trade-in inventory) versus when it should be referred to consignment or auction
- Apply the legal and compliance obligations specific to buying pre-owned jewelry from the public (AML considerations, stolen-property checks, documentation)
- Run a trade-in sales conversation that is simultaneously an honest appraisal and a genuine upgrade-sale opportunity
- Represent estate and antique pieces with historically accurate, non-misleading marketing and sales language
- Plan and run an in-store estate/trade-in buying event from start to finish

**Assessment:** Observed piece-evaluation roleplay (dating, condition triage, and offer-setting on a sample item); a manager-scored review of a written appraisal-type recommendation for three client scenarios; a capstone buying-event simulation.
**KPI link:** gross margin on secondary pieces, acquisition volume, consignment conversion rate, inventory turnover on estate/vintage stock, trade-in-to-upgrade conversion rate.
