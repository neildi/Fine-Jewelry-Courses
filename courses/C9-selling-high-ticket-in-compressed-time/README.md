# C9 Selling High-Ticket in Compressed Time

**Track:** Specialist
**Personas:** P1, P2, P3
**8 articles, 6-8 learner hours**
**Research anchor:** Fine-Jewelry-Career-Guide.md (Onboard Fine Jewelry Specialist role profile and seminar-to-showroom conversion KPI; luxury sales psychology on pressure tactics undermining trust); Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Career-and-Skills-Reference.md; trunk show operational research (JCK Online, JA New York, Clientbook, Deep Earth Marketing); high-ticket urgency/objection-handling research (bestbuyincentives.com, Forbes)

## Terminal outcomes

The learner can:
- Explain why compressed-time formats (trunk shows, cruise/travel-retail seminars, pop-up and VIP events) require a fundamentally different sales approach than standard multi-touch clienteling
- Distinguish genuine, honestly-stated urgency from manufactured pressure, and apply that distinction in real client conversations
- Run a compressed discovery conversation that surfaces a client's real priorities in minutes without becoming shallow
- Build fast, credible trust with a client who has no prior relationship history with the associate or store
- Handle a high-ticket decision moment directly within a single-visit or single-event time window, without resorting to pressure tactics
- Manage the logistics and staffing specific to compressed-time selling events (trunk shows, pop-ups, private viewings)
- Recover a compressed-time opportunity that didn't close on the spot, using appropriate fast follow-up
- Apply this course's full framework in a capstone simulation of a complete compressed-time selling event

**Assessment:** Observed roleplay of a compressed discovery-to-close conversation within a simulated time limit; a written analysis distinguishing real vs. manufactured urgency across sample scripts; a capstone event-simulation exercise.
**KPI link:** seminar-to-showroom conversion rate (travel/cruise retail), trunk show/event revenue and close rate, average ticket size at compressed-time events, post-event follow-up conversion rate.
