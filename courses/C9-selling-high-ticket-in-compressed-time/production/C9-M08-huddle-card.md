# C9-M08 Huddle Card: Capstone — Running a Full Compressed-Time Event

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift / Pre-Simulation Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Every skill from this course has to hold up AT THE SAME TIME, under real overlapping pressure — not one at a time in isolation.

## The Seven-Point Checklist (Run Through This Before Any Event or Simulation)
1. **Urgency (M1):** Is every deadline or scarcity claim I might use today actually TRUE?
2. **Discovery (M2):** Am I protecting real time for situation → gap → impact → vision, even when busy?
3. **Fast Trust (M3):** Am I leading with warmth before competence, every single time, with every new client?
4. **The Close (M4):** Trial closes throughout, one calm direct ask, then SILENCE?
5. **Event Logistics (M5):** Two-person minimum during setup/teardown? Do I know today's vendor terms?
6. **Onboard/Travel (M6):** If relevant today — substantive content over giveaway hooks; calm if someone checks prices?
7. **Follow-Up (M7):** For anyone who doesn't close today, do I have a real 24-hour follow-up plan ready?

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Pick the ONE of these seven you personally find hardest to hold onto when things get busy. Say it out loud. That's the one to watch today.
