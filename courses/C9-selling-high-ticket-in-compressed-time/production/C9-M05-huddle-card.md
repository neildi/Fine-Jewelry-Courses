# C9-M05 Huddle Card: Running the Room

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Trunk show season is a real security target, not a hypothetical. The Jewelers' Security Alliance calls it "a magnet for criminals." Treat setup and teardown as the MOST vulnerable windows, not an afterthought.

## Security Non-Negotiables
- One high-value item out at a time. Rest stays locked.
- TWO staff minimum during setup AND teardown — the most vulnerable windows of the whole event
- Inventory counts: on arrival, multiple times during, and before close — not just start and end
- Know today's alert phrase BEFORE doors open
- Visiting designer's inventory: get the list/photos in advance, know what's insured by whom

## Staff the Room for Real Conversations
- Full conversation (discovery to close) = 30-40 minutes
- One consultation station = ~12-16 clients per full day
- Work backward from expected RSVPs — don't just assign "whoever's scheduled"
- Understaffing doesn't just slow things down — it forces Module 2-3's discovery and trust work to get rushed for EVERY client that day

## Know the Vendor Terms Before You Sell
Minimum purchase commitment vs. consignment split — ask your manager which applies today. It affects how you talk about the line, whether you can hold or reorder a piece, and what happens to what's left unsold.

## Day-Of Sequence
Arrive 2+ hours early. Verify inventory against the manifest. Full team briefing: security plan, alert phrase, vendor terms, staffing assignments. Don't skip the briefing to save fifteen minutes — it costs more once the doors open.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
What's today's alert phrase? Does everyone on the floor actually know it?
