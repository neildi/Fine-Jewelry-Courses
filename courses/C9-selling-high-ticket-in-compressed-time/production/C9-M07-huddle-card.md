# C9-M07 Huddle Card: Fast, Honest Follow-Up

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** A client who didn't close today isn't a failure. What you do in the next 24 hours matters more than almost anything else you can control.

## The Window Closes Fast
General sales research consistently shows response speed matters enormously — leads contacted within minutes convert far better than those contacted hours or days later. We can't promise the exact same numbers apply to jewelry sales, but the pattern is real: enthusiasm fades fast. Follow up SAME DAY or NEXT MORNING, every time.

## The Three-Touch Cadence
1. Within 24 hours: personal message referencing the SPECIFIC conversation
2. A few days later: something of real value (a photo, an answer to their question)
3. 1-2 weeks out: one final, low-pressure check-in — then let it rest

## Honest Follow-Up vs. Pushy Follow-Up
- Reference the ACTUAL conversation, not a template
- Offer real value at every touch, not just a repeated ask
- State a real constraint if one exists — don't invent one
- Make it clear "no" is completely fine

## Remember Module 1
A client who already said "let me think about it" is EXTRA alert to pressure. Don't manufacture urgency in your follow-up — it'll be noticed, and it'll cost more trust than it gains.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Think of a client who didn't close recently. What's one genuine piece of value (not a sales push) you could follow up with today?
