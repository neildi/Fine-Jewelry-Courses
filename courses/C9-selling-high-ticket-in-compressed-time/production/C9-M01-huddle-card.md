# C9-M01 Huddle Card: Why Compressed-Time Selling Is Different

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** In compressed-time selling, there's usually no "come back next week." Deferral = lost sale. That changes everything about how you handle the moment.

## The Formats This Course Covers
- Trunk shows (often just a single afternoon)
- Onboard cruise seminars (convert the room before the ship moves on)
- Pop-ups, VIP nights, other short promotional windows

## Real Urgency vs. Fake Pressure — Know the Difference
- REAL: "This piece travels with the designer tomorrow — after today, it's gone from our store." (True, specific, verifiable)
- FAKE: "Someone else was just asking about this one..." (Vague, unverifiable, designed to pressure)
- Luxury clients spot fake pressure fast. It costs more trust than it's worth.

## The Discipline
Find the ACTUAL constraint in front of you today. State it plainly. Don't hide it (you lose the one real advantage) and don't inflate it (you lose trust).

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Name one real compressed-time format your store runs. What's the one true, specific constraint you could honestly tell a client about it?
