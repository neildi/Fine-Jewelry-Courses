# C9-M02 Huddle Card: Compressed Discovery

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Under time pressure, the instinct to skip straight to product is backward. Discovery is what makes the presentation actually land.

## The Four-Layer Sequence
1. **Situation** — "What's the occasion?"
2. **Gap** — "What have you looked at, and what wasn't right about it?"
3. **Impact** — "What does this need to represent for you?" (protect real time for this one)
4. **Vision** — "If you found exactly the right piece today, what would that look like?"

## Time-Box It
In a 10-minute conversation: ~2 min warm opening, ~3 min situation/gap, ~2-3 min impact/vision, rest for presenting informed by what you just learned.

## Read the Room
- Ease, relaxed tone, uncrossed arms = growing trust, keep moving
- Restlessness, minimal answers, guarded tone = discovery isn't done yet. SLOW DOWN, don't push forward.

## Confirm Before You Present
"So it sounds like the most important thing is X, and you want it to feel like Y — did I get that right?" Costs 15 seconds. Saves you from presenting the wrong thing.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice the impact question ("what does this need to represent for you?") out loud with a colleague. Notice how different the answer is from a generic "what are you looking for?"
