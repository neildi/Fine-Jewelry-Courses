# C9-M05 Quiz: Running the Room: Trunk Show and Pop-Up Event Logistics

**Instructions:** Answer all questions.

---

**1. According to the Jewelers' Security Alliance, as cited in this module, why is trunk show season considered a genuine security concern rather than a hypothetical one?**
A) Trunk shows carry no more security risk than any ordinary business day
B) Trunk shows concentrate unusually high-value merchandise, often including a visiting designer's inventory, into a temporary setup without a store's normal built-in security infrastructure, making the season "a magnet for criminals"
C) Security risk at trunk shows is purely a matter of individual store negligence, unrelated to the event format itself
D) Trunk shows are actually lower-risk than normal store operations because fewer people are present

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This is a direct, explicit warning from the industry's own security alliance, establishing that the elevated risk is real and documented, not speculative.

---

**2. Why are the setup and teardown windows of an event specifically identified as the most vulnerable periods?**
A) Setup and teardown are actually the safest periods because the event hasn't started yet
B) Attention is naturally divided during setup and teardown and merchandise is more exposed than during the show itself, which is why a two-person minimum is specifically recommended during these windows
C) Security concerns only apply once clients are physically present in the room
D) Setup and teardown require no special security consideration at all

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This is why multiple independent security sources specifically call out setup and teardown, rather than treating the whole event as a single uniform risk period.

---

**3. What is the recommended practice for displaying high-value items during a trunk show or similar event?**
A) Display as many pieces as possible simultaneously to maximize visual impact
B) Display only one high-value item at a time, keeping the rest secured in locked cases, and inspecting each piece before returning it to the case
C) Display practices have no real bearing on event security
D) High-value items should never be displayed at all during any event

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This single-item display practice is confirmed across multiple independent security sources as a core risk-reduction practice.

---

**4. What is the purpose of a predetermined staff alert phrase?**
A) To publicly announce a security concern to everyone in the room, including clients
B) To signal a potential problem to fellow staff members without alarming clients, decided on before the event rather than improvised in the moment
C) Alert phrases are only useful for advertising sales promotions during the event
D) Alert phrases should be different every single time to avoid any pattern of use

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: A phrase agreed upon in advance lets staff communicate discreetly about a potential issue while keeping the client experience calm and undisturbed.

---

**5. According to the staffing benchmark cited in this module, roughly how many client conversations can a single consultation station handle across a full event day?**
A) Only 1-2 conversations total, regardless of event length
B) Roughly 12-16 conversations, based on a 30-40-minute-per-conversation benchmark
C) An unlimited number, since conversation length has no real bearing on staffing needs
D) Exactly 100 conversations regardless of staffing or conversation length

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This benchmark should be used to work backward from expected attendance when building a staffing plan, rather than assigning staff without regard to actual expected conversation volume.

---

**6. Why does this module argue that understaffing an event relative to expected attendance is a serious problem, beyond simply making the day feel busier?**
A) Understaffing has no real effect on the quality of client interactions
B) Understaffing forces the discovery and trust-building work covered in earlier modules to compress further than the format can actually support, degrading the outcome for every client
C) Understaffing is only a problem if it results in a client complaint
D) More staff always produces better results regardless of expected attendance

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This connects event-level staffing decisions directly back to whether the individual sales-conversation techniques from Modules 2-4 can actually be applied properly.

---

**7. Why should every associate working a vendor's trunk show understand the specific commercial terms in effect (minimum purchase commitment vs. consignment split)?**
A) Commercial terms are irrelevant to the associates actually working the sales floor
B) These terms directly shape real decisions made during the event — how aggressively to push a specific line, whether a piece can be held or reordered, and what happens to unsold inventory
C) Only the store owner needs to understand vendor terms; associates should remain unaware of them
D) Vendor terms only matter after the event has already ended

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Understanding the actual terms in effect helps associates make informed, appropriate decisions on the floor rather than guessing at what's actually being agreed to with the vendor.
