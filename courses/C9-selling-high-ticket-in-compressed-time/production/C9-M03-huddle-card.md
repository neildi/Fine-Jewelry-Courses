# C9-M03 Huddle Card: Building Fast Trust With No Relationship History

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Warmth comes BEFORE competence. Always. Leading with expertise before warmth is established reads as a threat, not a reassurance — no matter how impressive the expertise actually is.

## Warmth Signals (Show These FIRST)
- Genuine, relaxed expression (not performative)
- Open posture, comfortable eye contact
- Match THEIR energy and pace — don't impose your own

## Competence Signals (These Land AFTER Warmth)
- Clear, jargon-free explanation
- Confident problem-solving
- Keeping every promise made DURING this conversation — even small ones ("let me check that" — then actually check it)

## Fast-Trust Techniques
- Genuine small talk (specific to THIS moment) beats generic small talk (weather) every time
- Paraphrase back what you heard — visible proof you listened
- Match their pace — don't rush a slow talker, don't linger with someone in a hurry
- Phone away, full attention — divided attention kills warmth fast
- Small honest admissions ("I'm not sure, let me check") build more trust than they cost in polish

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice the SAME opening line two ways: leading with product expertise vs. leading with genuine curiosity about the client. Which one actually made you want to keep talking?
