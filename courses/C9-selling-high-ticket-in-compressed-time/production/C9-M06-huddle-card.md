# C9-M06 Huddle Card: Onboard and Travel Retail

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** The ship's itinerary IS a real deadline. Don't add fake pressure on top of it. This category has a real reputation problem — we're here to be the exception, not confirm the stereotype.

## The Real Constraint
"We won't be back in this port for months" or "this sea-day promotion ends today" — these are TRUE. Say them plainly. Don't escalate pressure further as the cruise nears its end — that's exactly the "desperate pitch" pattern travel guides warn passengers about.

## Seminar-to-Showroom, Done Right
- Lead with SUBSTANTIVE content (real gemology, real education) — not a free-gift hook
- Warmth before competence applies to a ROOM just like it applies one-on-one
- The invitation to visit the showroom should feel like an offer, not a trap

## In the Showroom
Everything from Modules 2-4 applies directly: real discovery, warmth-first trust, trial closes, calm direct close, then silence.

## If They Check Their Phone Mid-Conversation
STAY CALM. Comparing prices independently is smart and expected — travel guides tell people to do exactly this. Defensiveness confirms every bad assumption they might have walked in with. "Take your time" is the right response.

## Handle Skepticism Head-On
Many passengers walk in already wary — often for good reason. Recommend independent certification (not from us). Be transparent about duty and total cost. This builds MORE trust than pretending the skepticism doesn't exist.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
What's the one TRUE constraint of today's itinerary you can state plainly to a client, without adding anything extra to it?
