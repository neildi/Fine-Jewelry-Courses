**MUST VERIFY — RESOLVED (2026-09-27):** The Form 8300 dollar threshold is confirmed at **more than $10,000 in cash** in a single transaction or in two or more related transactions within a 12-month period. The business must file Form 8300 within **15 days** of receiving the cash that crosses the threshold, and must furnish a written payer statement by **January 31** of the following year. Source: IRS.gov, "Instructions for Form 8300," "IRS Form 8300 Reference Guide," and IRS Fact Sheet FS-2023-19, all confirmed live on 2026-09-27.
