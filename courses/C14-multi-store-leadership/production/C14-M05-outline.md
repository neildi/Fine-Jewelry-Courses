# C14-M05 Outline / Media Record

**Working title:** Compliance and Risk Oversight at Scale
**Alternates:** "The District Compliance Calendar You Can Actually Enforce"; "Loss Prevention Above the Store Level"

**Scenario hook (1 sentence):** A new district leader discovers her five stores are each running a different, informally-inherited version of the compliance calendar, and one has fallen behind on cash-reporting and inventory retention rules without anyone noticing.

**Section-by-section outline:**
1. What "Compliance at Scale" Actually Covers — reader can name the four compliance domains (cash reporting, inventory/insurance records, high-value verification, legal/marketing compliance) a district leader is accountable for.
2. The District Compliance Calendar — reader can build one master calendar table covering daily through annual cadences, sourced from the workflows research.
3. Segregation of Duties and Rotating Audits — reader can explain why rotation and separated receiving/counting roles reduce risk.
4. Reading Store-Level Red Flags During Visits — reader can list at least three red flags to check during a store visit, linked back to Module 2.
5. What District Leaders Are Actually Measured On — reader can connect compliance oversight to their own documented KPI set (loss prevention, expense control, P&L).
6. Objections, Mistakes and Edge Cases — reader can identify the "gotcha" framing trap and the false reassurance of "nothing's gone wrong."
