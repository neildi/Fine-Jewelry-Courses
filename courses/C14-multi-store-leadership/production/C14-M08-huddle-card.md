# C14-M08 Huddle Card

**Headline:** Executives want the output, not the process.

**Key points:**
1. The VP-level and district-manager-level scorecards overlap on a handful of metrics: comparable sales, conversion, ATV, client retention, expense control/P&L, and productivity — that's what belongs on page one.
2. When presenting a miss, name the number first, then the specific KPI driver, then the action — never bury the headline in context.
3. Store-visit detail, compliance calendars, and talent maps are backup material for follow-up questions, not primary presentation content.

**Script to practice today:** "District comp sales are up 4% against a 2% plan. Store C is down 6% — here's the driver, and here's the one thing I'm doing about it this month."

**Question to ask the team:** If you had thirty seconds with the VP right now, what's the one number and one action you'd lead with?
