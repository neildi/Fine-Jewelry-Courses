# C14-M08 Outline / Media Record

**Working title:** Presenting District Results to Executives
**Alternates:** "The One-Page District Scorecard"; "What Executives Actually Want to Hear"

**Scenario hook:** A new district leader brings forty-five slides to his first executive review and is stopped by the VP after twenty minutes, having covered only two of his six stores, because he never distinguished operating detail from what the business needed to hear.

**Section outline:**
1. Why This Matters — reader connects the narrow VP-level and district-manager-level scorecards to what belongs in an executive presentation.
2. What Executives Actually Want to See — reader can name the overlap metrics between the two documented scorecards.
3. The One-Page District Scorecard — reader can build a compressed scorecard using only those overlap metrics.
4. Framing a Miss Without Burying It — reader can structure a driver-plus-action answer to a KPI miss.
5. What to Leave Out — reader can distinguish operating detail (Modules 2, 5, 6, 7) from executive-facing content.
6. Objections, Mistakes and Edge Cases — reader avoids over-preparation and the context-before-headline framing trap.

**Facts used, with placement:**
- VP of Retail Sales scorecard: comparable store sales, conversion, ATV, client retention, store productivity, lifetime client value, budgets, labor productivity, profitability, inventory-aligned forecasting → Sections 1, 2, 3
- District/Regional Sales Manager scorecard: unit sales performance, loss prevention, expense control, P&L, merchandise presentation, overall district administration → Sections 1, 2, 3
- VP of Retail Sales requires 10+ years multi-unit retail leadership experience, reports to CEO → Section 1 (context)
- Cross-references to Module 2 (store visits), Module 3 (comparative KPI diagnosis), Module 4 (coaching), Module 5 (compliance), Module 6 (communication cadence), Module 7 (talent pipeline) as source material that feeds but does not directly appear in executive presentations → Sections 3, 5

**Gaps not covered in evidence:** No specific slide count, meeting length, or presentation software was found in the supplied research; the "one-page scorecard" format and specific dialogue examples are original synthesis modeling the documented scorecard priorities, not verbatim sourced templates — flagged for reviewer awareness.
