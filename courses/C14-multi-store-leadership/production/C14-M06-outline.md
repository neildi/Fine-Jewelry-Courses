# C14-M06 Outline / Media Record

**Working title:** District Communication Cadence
**Alternates:** "Building a Rhythm Instead of Making Calls"; "The Three Layers of District Communication"

**Scenario hook:** A district leader spends hours each week on unstructured one-off calls with six store managers and still gets surprised by problems, because there's no fixed cadence for what gets reported when.

**Section outline:**
1. Why This Matters — reader connects the existing store-level huddle/weekly-meeting rhythm to the district-level equivalent they need to build.
2. The Three Layers of District Communication — reader can name daily/weekly/monthly layers and who owns each.
3. Building the Weekly District Check-In — reader can run a fixed five-question weekly call with every store.
4. The Monthly Business Review: What Belongs On It — reader can distinguish monthly-review content from weekly-check-in content.
5. Cascading Information Down Without Losing It — reader can build an accountable written bulletin system.
6. Objections, Mistakes and Edge Cases — reader can identify the "quiet store" risk and the bulletin-without-accountability trap.

**Facts used, with placement:**
- Daily huddle content (new products, appointments, promotions, repair status, clienteling plans) → Sections 1, 2
- Weekly five-report Monday KPI review, 15 minutes → Sections 1, 3
- Weekly team meeting content (clienteling accountability, training topic) → Sections 1, 2
- Store Manager reports to District Manager reporting line → Section 1
- Monthly business review with District Manager (joint results review, strategic planning) → Sections 1, 4
- Month-end reporting deadline (25th: layaway report, transfer reconciliation) → Section 4
- Quarterly/annual brand KPI scorecards, mystery-shop performance → Section 4 (table)
- Weekly crime bulletin with signed training log (as a model for accountable cascade communication) → Section 5

**Gaps not covered in evidence:** No named district-level communication software or platform was found in evidence; omitted rather than invented. No specific length/duration was found for a monthly business review; not stated as a fact in the article.
