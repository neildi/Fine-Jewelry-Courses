# C14-M07 Huddle Card

**Headline:** If you can't name your bench, you don't have one.

**Key points:**
1. Multi-unit leadership roles explicitly require a staff-development track record — this isn't optional, it's part of what you're evaluated on for your own next promotion.
2. A district talent map names who's ready now, who's ready in 6-12 months, and what gap each person still needs to close — informal impressions don't count.
3. A store with no identified successor is a district risk, not a private embarrassment — flag it and fix it together.

**Script to practice today:** "If your store manager left tomorrow, who on your team could step up — and have you actually told that person you're developing them?"

**Question to ask the team:** Which of our stores has no identified successor for its current manager, and what's our plan to close that gap this quarter?
