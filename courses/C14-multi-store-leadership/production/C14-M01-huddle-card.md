# C14-M01 Huddle Card: From Running a Store to Running Managers

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** A struggling district leader visits a store to inspect it. A great one visits to develop the person running it.

## The Core Shift
Not: personally solve every problem you find.
Instead: build managers who solve problems before you ever hear about them.

## Why Your Store-Manager Instincts Can Work Against You
As a store manager, you had a daily operating rhythm to follow. As a district leader, there's no script — you have to find the real issues yourself, across multiple stores, each with different problems. Running a district like "one big store" doesn't work.

## Four Capabilities That Actually Matter Now
1. Develop others' strengths
2. Let go of control
3. Look at the big picture every day
4. Don't get pulled into doing any single store's job

## Leading From a Distance Is Genuinely Different
You can't build relationships through daily presence anymore. It takes deliberate communication — clear expectations, a real cadence of contact, tools to stay connected between visits.

## Follow-Up Is Where the Magic Happens
Delegating without following up teaches managers that what you asked for was optional. Following up consistently — without taking over — builds real accountability.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Think of the last time you personally fixed a problem during a store visit. What coaching question could have let the manager fix it themselves instead?
