# C14-M02 Huddle Card: The Store Visit: Agenda, Audit, Debrief

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** A store visit that starts cold, with no reference to last time's commitments, tells the manager those commitments never really mattered.

## The Five-Part Rhythm
1. **Prepare** — review the numbers AND last visit's commitments before arriving
2. **Observe** — walk the floor as a customer would, before any checklist
3. **Coach** — ask questions before offering solutions, in real time
4. **Verify** — did the prior commitments actually happen?
5. **Follow up** — named owner, specific timing, a plan to check back

## Five Audit Domains, Every Time, Every Store
1. Merchandising and visual standards
2. Inventory and stock management
3. Store operations
4. Customer experience
5. Compliance and loss-prevention

Same structure everywhere = results you can actually compare across stores.

## Evidence, Not Impressions
Photo it. Write it down specifically. "The display was messy" helps no one. A dated photo and a specific note does.

## Real-Time Coaching + a Focused Debrief
Coach what you see, on the spot, when it happens. But the formal debrief? Two or three priorities. Not ten. A manager who leaves with ten things to fix fixes none of them well.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Before your next visit: what were the specific commitments from last time, and have they actually happened?
