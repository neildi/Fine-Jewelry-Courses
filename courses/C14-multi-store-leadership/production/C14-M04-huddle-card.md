# C14-M04 Huddle Card: Coaching Managers on Coaching

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Don't bypass a struggling manager to coach their associate directly. Coach the manager on the metric instead — that's the whole job now.

## Know Which Hat You're Wearing
Strategist. Financial analyst. Operator. Coach. The coach hat's biggest trap: telling instead of asking, solving instead of teaching.

## Coach the Manager, Not the Associate
Your job: figure out which manager needs coaching on which specific metric. Then coach THAT manager. Skipping around them to fix their team yourself undermines their authority and teaches nothing.

## Model It, Then Watch Them Try
Show a manager a real debrief — observation, impact, alternative — with them watching. Next visit, watch THEM attempt it. Give feedback on their coaching technique, not the associate's performance.

## Invest in Your "B" Players
The most struggling store isn't always the best use of your time. A small improvement in a solid, middle-performing manager often moves the whole district more. Turn B's into A's. Have A's mentor C's.

## Accountability Means Coaching Behavior, Not Just Numbers
Hold managers accountable for actually running real debriefs and GROW conversations — not just for raw sales, which can move for reasons outside their control.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Pick one "B" player manager in your portfolio. What's the one specific coaching behavior you could model for them at your next visit?
