# C14-M03 Huddle Card: Comparative KPI Analysis Across Stores

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Ranking stores on raw revenue is comparing apples to a much bigger apple orchard. Normalize for size first, or you're drawing the wrong conclusion.

## The Great Equalizer: Sales Per Square Foot
**Formula:** Total sales ÷ selling square footage
This lets a 1,200 sqft boutique and a 4,000 sqft flagship compete on the same footing.

## The Trap: Inconsistent Definitions
If one store's "square footage" includes the stockroom and another's doesn't, the whole comparison breaks — even if the numbers look reasonable. Define "selling area" once. Apply it everywhere, the same way.

## Building the District Scorecard
1. Standardize data definitions FIRST (revenue must mean the same thing everywhere)
2. 6-8 KPIs, covering revenue, labor, customer experience, operations
3. Benchmark against your OWN best store first, then industry figures
4. Build a real review cadence — no cadence, no accountability

## Don't Compare Apples to Toddler Apples
A store six months into its first year doesn't belong in the same comparison bucket as a mature flagship. Segment by maturity, format, and region before ranking anyone.

## Read Numbers in Pairs
Strong sales/sqft + weak customer experience score tells a different story than strong on both. Never trust a single isolated number.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Pull our raw revenue ranking across all our stores. Now calculate sales per square foot. Does the ranking change? What does that tell us?
