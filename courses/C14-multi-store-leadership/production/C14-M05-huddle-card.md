# C14-M05 Huddle Card

**Headline:** Compliance isn't a memo — it's an audited habit.

**Key points:**
1. Every store should be working from the same master compliance calendar — daily cash controls through annual inventory retention — not a version inherited informally from a past manager.
2. Segregation of duties (separate counting from receiving, rotate who audits which department) catches problems a single trusted person's habits can hide.
3. "We've never had a problem" is a reason to check a store sooner, not a reason to skip it.

**Script to practice today:** "Show me your last bench-sweep sheet and your suspicious-incident log before we talk about sales this visit."

**Question to ask the team:** Can every manager on this call name their next compliance deadline and where their annual inventory is stored, without looking it up?
