## 1. Executive Summary & Verification

Course C2 Module 2 focuses on high-ticket luxury discovery: moving associates away from pre-mature product pitches and interrogation-style questioning to an elegant, permission-based discovery framework.

The audit verified three core pillars:
1. **Brand Compliance:** 100% free of discount/mass-market retailers (`Kay Jewelers`, `Effy`, `Blue Nile`, `James Allen`, `Diamonds International`, `Zales`). Anchored to premier luxury ateliers: **Cartier, Bulgari, Tiffany & Co., Harry Winston, Tacori, Hearts On Fire, and Stuller**.
2. **Sales Psychology & White-Labeling:** Embedded proprietary Jewelswell frameworks: ***The 7-Pillar Discovery Architecture™***, ***The Conversational Permission Protocol™***, and ***The Three-Piece Curation Matrix™ (Anchor, Contrast, Stretch)***. Zero third-party trainer citations.
3. **Pedagogical Enrichment:** Added interactive Counter Simulation Dialogue Cards illustrating nuance in style translation, lifestyle wear compatibility, and no-friction investment framing, accompanied by an original vector schematic: ***Figure 2.1: The 7-Pillar Discovery Architecture™ & Curation Funnel***.

## 2. Brand & IP Remediation Matrix

| Element | Prior Baseline | Remediated Compliant Implementation | Strategic Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Curation Methodology** | Generic 3-piece presentation | Standardized as ***The Three-Piece Curation Matrix™*** (Anchor, Contrast, Stretch) with explicit counter presentation choreography. | Prevents showroom paralysis and equips advisors with a replicable luxury presentation discipline. |
| **Discovery Model** | Seven informal questions | Formalized into ***The 7-Pillar Discovery Architecture™*** (Occasion, Recipient, Aesthetic, Wear Pattern, Existing Jewelry, Timing, Investment Range). | Gives junior associates a mental compass rather than a memorized script. |
| **Budget Inquiry** | Asking price directly | Reframed under ***The Investment Range Permission Protocol™*** with two-tier reference point alternatives. | Eliminates client embarrassment and preserves romance on high-ticket purchases. |
| **Luxury Reference Points** | Generic jewelry retail | Integrated luxury counter benchmarks from **Cartier, Bulgari, Tiffany & Co., and Harry Winston**. | Elevates student aspiration and professional conduct to elite guild standards. |
