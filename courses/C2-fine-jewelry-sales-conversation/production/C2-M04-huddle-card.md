# Huddle Card: Buying Signals and When to Stop Talking

**Headline:** When a client asks about sizing, timing, or color, the sale is already won. Stop presenting and ask for it.

**3 key points:**
1. A buying signal is any question or comment a client makes once trust and value are established — "Can you size this?" or "How soon could I get it?" both mean the same thing: I'm ready.
2. The most common way we lose a sold client is talking past the signal — re-explaining features they already accepted instead of moving to close.
3. After you ask a trial close or closing question, stop talking. The first person to speak next usually loses the sale.

**Script to practice today:**
"Of course, I can have that sized and ready by Thursday. Should I go ahead and write that up for you?" — then stay silent.

**Question to ask the team:**
Think of the last sale you lost after the client seemed ready. What did you keep saying after they gave you the signal, and what could you have asked instead?
