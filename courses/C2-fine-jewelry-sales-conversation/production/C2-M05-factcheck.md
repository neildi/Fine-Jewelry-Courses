## MUST VERIFY
- **The 60% Brain Squad statistic and the 60%/70% false-objection figures:** both are self-reported by the originating trade publications (an INSTORE reader survey and a columnist's professional estimate, respectively) rather than independently verified academic studies. Presented in the article as trade-press findings, which is an appropriate hedge, but a reviewer should avoid stating either as a precise, externally-verifiable statistic in marketing use.
- **The "8 out of 10 say yes" referral-for-discount figure:** attributed to one named sales trainer's field observation, not a controlled study. Should be softened or re-attributed if used outside this internal training context.

## Outstanding before publication
No MEDIA REQUEST placeholders remain. All three media items are Tier 5 trade-press or industry-vendor link-outs, appropriate given this module covers sales technique rather than gemological standards (where GIA/AGS/FTC would be the expected Tier 1-2 sources).
