# Huddle Card: Price Objections Without Discounting

**Headline:** "That's too expensive" is rarely about the price. Diagnose it before you touch the tag.

**3 key points:**
1. Ask "Compared to what?" or "Is it the price, or just more than you want to spend right now?" before responding — this tells you whether it's a value problem or a budget problem.
2. If it's a budget problem, use option negotiation: adjust the carat, metal, or stone to fit the number. Never discount the piece they're looking at.
3. After you state a price or a value case, stop talking. The first person to speak next usually gives away margin.

**Script to practice today:**
"I understand — that's a real number. Can I ask, is it the price itself, or is it just more than you were planning to spend today?"

**Question to ask the team:**
Think of the last time you offered a discount before the client actually asked for one. What could you have asked instead to find out what was really going on?
