# C2 Module 7 Production Record

## Inputs
Module 7: Online Comparison and the Showrooming Client
Persona: P1 The New Associate (secondary P2, P3)
KPI: Close rate; average ticket; customer retention and referral rate
Terminal outcome: Respond to a client comparing an in-store piece to an online listing by verifying the comparison is genuinely apples-to-apples, then using side-by-side selection, tangible quality proof, and unique value to win the sale without reflexively matching or discounting

## Working title and alternates
1. Online Comparison and the Showrooming Client (selected, matches Prompt Kit Part A module title)
2. Alternate: The Phone on the Counter
3. Alternate: Why They're Still Standing in Your Store

## Scenario hook
A client pulls up a $3,800-cheaper online listing for what looks like the same solitaire; the associate must verify the comparison before reacting, rather than apologizing or discounting on reflex.
