## 1. Executive Summary & Verification

Course C2 Module 1 establishes the frontline behavioral architecture for fine jewelry sales advisors. The module shifts associates away from the transactional, closed-ended reflex (*"Can I help you?"*) to high-ticket luxury hospitality, spatial proxemics (the 45-degree angle), sensory decompression, and consultative conversation openers.

The audit verified three mandatory dimensions:
1. **Brand Compliance:** STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE for discount/mass-market retailers (`Kay Jewelers`, `Effy`, `Blue Nile`, `James Allen`, `Diamonds International`, `Zales`). All counter dynamics re-anchored to world-class heritage maisons: **Cartier, Bulgari, Tacori, Hearts On Fire, Tiffany & Co., Stuller, Harry Winston, and Van Cleef & Arpels**.
2. **Shane Decker Sales Psychology White-Labeling:** Shane Decker's empirical sales psychology—namely, preventing robotic scripts, training situational observation, and creating a dynamic counter repertoire—has been 100% white-labeled under Jewelswell proprietary trademarks: ***The Anti-Autopilot Floor Observation Matrix™*** and ***The Dynamic Repertoire Protocol™***. All personal and company references have been eradicated.
3. **Pedagogical Polish & UI/UX:** Built high-retention interactive Counter Simulation Dialogue Cards for wowing statements, leading lines, and the Validate-Liberate-Pivot sequence, accompanied by an original vector schematic: ***Figure 1.1: The 90-Second Decompression & 45-Degree Stance Architecture™***.
