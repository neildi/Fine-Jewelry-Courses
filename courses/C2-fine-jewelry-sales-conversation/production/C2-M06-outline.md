# C2 Module 6 Production Record

## Inputs
Module 6: "I'll Think About It" and the Follow-Up That Wins
Persona: P1 The New Associate (secondary P2, P3)
KPI: Close rate; average ticket; follow-up conversion rate
Terminal outcome: Respond to "I'll think about it" with a diagnostic question and a concrete next step, then run a structured follow-up sequence that wins the sale later instead of losing it silently

## Working title and alternates
1. "I'll Think About It" and the Follow-Up That Wins (selected, matches Prompt Kit Part A module title)
2. Alternate: The Sale That Was Never Actually Lost
3. Alternate: What Happens After They Say No Decision Yet

## Scenario hook
A client leaves undecided after a strong presentation; the associate hands over a business card with no follow-up plan, and the client buys a similar ring elsewhere eleven days later, after a competitor called within 48 hours.
