# C2 M03 Huddle Card — Present, Don’t Recite

## The loop

1. **Connect:** Recall what the client said.
2. **Show:** Name one visible or documented feature.
3. **Translate:** Explain how it may serve their stated preference.
4. **Invite:** Try on, compare, handle where permitted.
5. **Check:** “What are you noticing?”
6. **Disclose:** Use approved product records; agree the next step.

## Say this

“You said you wanted [preference]. I chose this because [feature] may give you [relevant outcome]. Would you like to compare it with [contrast]?”

## Never improvise

- Treatment or origin
- Durability guarantees
- Craft or artisan claims
- Resale, appreciation, or insurance value
- Price or service terms outside approved records

## Daily drill
Present one piece in 60 seconds. Include one client preference, one verified feature, one invitation, and one checking question.
