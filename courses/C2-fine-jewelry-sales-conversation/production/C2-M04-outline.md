# C2 Module 4 Production Record

## Inputs
Module 4: Buying Signals and When to Stop Talking
Persona: P1 The New Associate (secondary P2, P3)
KPI: Conversion rate; close rate; average ticket
Terminal outcome: Recognize verbal and non-verbal buying signals in real time, and shift from presenting into a trial close or close instead of talking past the sale

## Working title and alternates
1. Buying Signals and When to Stop Talking (selected, matches Prompt Kit Part A module title)
2. Alternate: The Sale You Talked Yourself Out Of
3. Alternate: Stop Presenting, Start Closing

## Scenario hook
A client asks to size a three-stone anniversary ring, a clear buying signal, but the associate keeps presenting the 4Cs instead of closing, and the client leaves saying "let me think about it."
