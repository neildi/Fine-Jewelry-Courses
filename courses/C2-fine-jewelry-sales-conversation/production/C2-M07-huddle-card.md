# Huddle Card: Online Comparison and the Showrooming Client

**Headline:** A client with a lower online price is still standing in your store. That's an opportunity, not a threat.

**3 key points:**
1. Before reacting to any online price, verify it's genuinely the same thing: same carat, cut, clarity, certifying lab, and whether setting/service are included.
2. Let them choose between a few pieces by eye first, before you reveal specs or prices. Their own eyes make your case better than any argument.
3. Never discount reflexively. If you can't responsibly match a price, say so honestly — the trust you build often outlasts this one sale.

**Script to practice today:**
"That's great that you're doing your research — can I see what you found? I just want to make sure we're comparing the exact same thing."

**Question to ask the team:**
Think of the last time a client showed you a lower online price. Did you verify it was truly comparable before responding, or react to the number first?
