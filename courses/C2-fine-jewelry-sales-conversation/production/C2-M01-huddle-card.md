# Huddle Card: The First 90 Seconds

**Headline:** "Can I help you?" gives clients an easy exit. Replace it with something that can't be answered "no."

**3 key points:**
1. Retire "Can I help you?" and "How are you today?" Both sound scripted and invite a one-word dismissal.
2. Use statements ("There must be an occasion coming up") or non-business questions ("How was traffic getting here?"). Neither can be waved off with "no."
3. When you hear "just looking," validate it fully, then ask an open question: "That's great, please look all you want. So what brought you in to look?"

**Script to practice today:**
"That's great, please look all you want. So, what brought you in today, just browsing, or is there something specific on your mind?"

**Question to ask the team:**
How many of us are still saying "Can I help you?" out of habit, and what's one non-business opener we can each commit to trying today?
