## MUST VERIFY
- **The FTC CARS Rule analogy:** the CARS Rule itself applies specifically to auto dealers, not jewelry retailers. The article uses its underlying principles (disclosure, consent, no misrepresentation) as a transferable ethical framework, which is clearly caveated in the outline, but a reviewer should confirm the article's phrasing doesn't imply the rule itself legally governs jewelry sales.
- **The observe-flag-offer-accept ethical upsell framework:** adapted from general, non-jewelry-specific ethical-selling writing. Effective as a practical framework, but not a jewelry-trade-press finding; a reviewer may want a jewelry-specific citation if one is later identified.
