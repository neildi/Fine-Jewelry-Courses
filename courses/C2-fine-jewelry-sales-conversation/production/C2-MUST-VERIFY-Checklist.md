### Module 5 — Price Objections Without Discounting
**2 items**

- **The 60% Brain Squad statistic (INSTORE reader survey) and the 60%/70% false-objection figures (Southern Jewelry News)** — Both self-reported by the originating trade publications rather than independently verified academic studies. **Action:** avoid stating either as a precise, externally-verifiable statistic in marketing use.

### Module 7 — Online Comparison and the Showrooming Client
