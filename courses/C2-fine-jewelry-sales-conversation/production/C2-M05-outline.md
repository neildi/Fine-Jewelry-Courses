# C2 Module 5 Production Record

## Inputs
Module 5: Price Objections Without Discounting
Persona: P1 The New Associate (secondary P2, P3)
KPI: Average ticket; gross margin; close rate
Terminal outcome: Diagnose whether a price objection is about value or budget, then respond with reframing, value-anchoring, or option negotiation instead of defaulting to a discount

## Working title and alternates
1. Price Objections Without Discounting (selected, matches Prompt Kit Part A module title)
2. Alternate: The Two Words That Cost You Margin
3. Alternate: Answer the Cost, Not the Price

## Scenario hook
A client hesitates at a $15,000 solitaire price; the associate fills the silence with an unprompted discount, teaching the client that no price in the store is firm.
