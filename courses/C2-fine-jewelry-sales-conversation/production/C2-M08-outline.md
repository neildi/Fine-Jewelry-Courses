# C2 Module 8 Production Record

## Inputs
Module 8: Add-Ons, Protection Plans and Care Products Done Ethically
Persona: P1 The New Associate (secondary P2, P3)
KPI: Average ticket; attachment rate; customer trust and retention
Terminal outcome: Present insurance referrals, appraisals, protection plans, and care products as genuine service using clear disclosure and an ethical upsell framework, without misrepresentation, duplicate coverage, or pressure

## Working title and alternates
1. Add-Ons, Protection Plans and Care Products Done Ethically (selected, matches Prompt Kit Part A module title)
2. Alternate: The Add-On That Isn't Really a Service
3. Alternate: Service, Not Attachment Rate

## Scenario hook
An associate pitches a $200 protection plan with no explanation of what it covers or how it differs from insurance, closing the add-on through compliance rather than understanding.

## Gaps (not in evidence, not asserted)
- The FTC CARS Rule is specific to auto retail; this module applies its underlying consumer-protection principles (disclosure, consent, no misrepresentation) to jewelry by analogy, not as a claim that the CARS Rule itself governs jewelry sales.
- The ethical upsell framework (observe-flag-offer-accept) is adapted from general sales-ethics writing, not a jewelry-specific named source; flagged for a lighter citation tier.
