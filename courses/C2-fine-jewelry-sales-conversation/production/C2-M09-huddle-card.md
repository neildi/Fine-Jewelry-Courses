# Huddle Card: Three Ways to Ask for the Sale

**Headline:** Up to 60% of jewelry sales presentations never actually ask for the sale. Don't be part of that number.

**3 key points:**
1. The Assumptive Close: speak as though the sale is already happening ("yours," not "ours"), then ask about a detail like gift-wrap, sizing, or pickup time.
2. The Alternate Choice Close: offer two options where both lead to a completed sale — "Would you like to pay with a check or a credit card?"
3. The Reflexive Close: when a client asks a logistics question (sizing, deposit, timing), answer it while simultaneously asking for the sale.

**Script to practice today:**
"I can have the ring sized by tomorrow morning. What time would you like to pick it up?"

**Question to ask the team:**
Think of the last presentation you gave where you didn't clearly ask for the sale. Which of the three techniques would have fit that moment?
