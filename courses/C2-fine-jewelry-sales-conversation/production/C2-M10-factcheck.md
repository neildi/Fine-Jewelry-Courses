## MUST VERIFY
- **The Ebbinghaus forgetting curve's exact percentages:** widely cited secondary sources state different specific figures (e.g., "50% within a day," "90% within a week") for the same underlying 1885 research, and this research pass did not verify a single authoritative percentage. The article deliberately avoids stating a specific percentage for this reason and should continue to avoid doing so unless a reviewer sources the original or a rigorously verified secondary citation.
- **The three-part huddle structure (3/8/4 minute breakdown):** adapted from general, non-jewelry-specific sales-practice-routine content. Effective as a practical structure, but not attributed to one single authoritative or jewelry-trade source; a reviewer may want to simplify the timing guidance to avoid implying false precision.
