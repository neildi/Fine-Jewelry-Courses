# Huddle Card: Add-Ons, Protection Plans and Care Products Done Ethically

**Headline:** An add-on is a service, not an attachment-rate target. Pitch it like one.

**3 key points:**
1. Know the real difference between a store warranty and jewelry insurance before you pitch either one — most warranties don't cover loss or theft, and clients deserve to know that.
2. If you offer an in-house appraisal, say plainly that it's prepared by the store, and mention the independent-appraisal option for significant pieces.
3. Use observe-flag-offer-accept: notice something specific about this client's situation, name it, offer real options including one that costs nothing, and accept a "not today" gracefully.

**Script to practice today:**
"Since you're getting this resized for her, a lot of clients also add our care kit — it keeps the setting looking new between professional cleanings. Want me to walk you through what's in it?"

**Question to ask the team:**
Think of the last add-on you pitched. Was it specific to that client's real situation, or the same script you use for everyone?
