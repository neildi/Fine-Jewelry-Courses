# C2 M02 Huddle Card — Discover Before You Curate

## The rule
**Ask, listen, summarize, then show.** Do not lead with an assumption or a case full of options.

## Seven areas

1. Occasion: “What would you like this piece to mark?”
2. Wearer: “Tell me about their style on an ordinary day.”
3. Style: “What caught your eye?”
4. Lifestyle: “Every day, or particular occasions?”
5. Existing jewelry: “What do you reach for most now?”
6. Process: “Are you exploring, comparing, or narrowing down?”
7. Range: “May I ask what investment range feels comfortable?”

## The recap
“You want [meaning], with [style], for [wear pattern], and you are open to [range/process]. Did I get that right?”

## Then curate
Show 2–3 pieces:

- Closest match
- Useful contrast
- Trade-off option

## Avoid

- Relationship or occasion assumptions
- Budget questions before value has been created
- Five questions in a row
- Recording unnecessary personal information

## Daily drill
Use one discovery question before naming a product in your next client conversation.
