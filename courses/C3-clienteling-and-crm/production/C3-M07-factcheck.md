**1. "Winning back a lapsed client is cheaper and faster than acquiring a new client from scratch."**
Status: VERIFIED as directionally accurate, appropriately hedged. Sources: "Customer Retention Cost Statistics 2026" (stealthagents.com); "Customer Retention vs Acquisition Cost: Beyond 5x" (releva.ai); "110+ Customer Retention Statistics" (zipdo.co).
