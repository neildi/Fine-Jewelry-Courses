# C3-M07 Huddle Card: Winning Back a Lapsed Client

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Reconnect on the relationship before you reoffer anything. A discount-first win-back message signals desperation and skips the step that actually brings clients back for good.

## Three Dormancy Tiers
1. **Recently quiet (3-6 mo):** Light, no-agenda touch — they're probably still warm.
2. **Genuinely lapsed (6-18 mo):** Reconnect with a specific detail, then offer something relevant.
3. **Long-dormant (18+ mo):** Reintroduce yourself clearly — don't assume the relationship picked up where it left off.

## The Win-Back Sequence
Reconnect (real detail) → Acknowledge (briefly, no over-apologizing) → Offer (specific, not generic) → Ask (soft, low-stakes) → Leave the door open (no matter the response)

## Handling Responses
- **Warm response:** Move at their pace, don't rush to close.
- **Polite, non-committal:** Log it as a win — this is a successful relationship touch, not a failed sale.
- **No response:** Wait weeks, not days, and vary your next reason.
- **"Leave me alone":** Honor immediately, log it, never contact again.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Pick one lapsed client from your book. Which dormancy tier are they in, and what's one specific, true detail you could reconnect on?
