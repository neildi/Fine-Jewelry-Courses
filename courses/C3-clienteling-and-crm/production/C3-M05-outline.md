## Scenario hook
Module 2 covered the mechanics (capture, cadence, timing) of date-based outreach; this module adds the specific knowledge (which anniversary year means what) and the judgment (when a recorded date becomes the wrong thing to act on) that mechanics alone can't provide.

## Gaps (not in evidence, not asserted)
- The condolence-message source is written for general professional client relationships, not jewelry retail specifically; this module explicitly adapts it to a jewelry clienteling context rather than presenting it as jewelry-trade-specific guidance.
- No claim that every anniversary year between the milestone years listed lacks its own tradition; this module deliberately covers only the major milestone years per the source, not an exhaustive year-by-year list.
- The life-milestone list beyond anniversaries (engagement, graduation, promotion, retirement) is presented as commonsense occasion categories consistent with Module 1's capture guidance, not attributed to a single external source.
