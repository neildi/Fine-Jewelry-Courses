# Huddle Card: Getting the Most from Your CRM

**Headline:** What lives only in your memory disappears the day you're out sick. Write it down, the same way, every day.

**3 key points:**
1. Before your shift, check what today already owes clients: repairs due, appointments (with the file open), and any follow-ups due today from your clienteling calendar.
2. Log details the moment a conversation happens: price, date, size, name. "Recorded wish lists get followed up; the ones in a notebook do not."
3. Once a week, review your open threads. Anything quiet for 14+ days gets one of three treatments: follow up now, schedule a specific follow-up, or close it out.

**Script to practice today:**
Right after a client conversation, before helping the next person, log one specific detail: a price discussed, a date mentioned, a size taken, or a name. Do this every time today.

**Question to ask the team:**
Pick one client thread that's gone quiet. Is it time to follow up now, schedule a specific follow-up, or close it out honestly?
