# Huddle Card: Personalized Outreach Without Sounding Like Spam

**Headline:** "How do you know that?" is the question that turns personalization into surveillance. Always answer it before the client has to ask.

**3 key points:**
1. Name the source of any personal detail you reference. "You mentioned during your last visit..." reads as attentive; the same fact with no context reads as tracked.
2. Marketing texts need real, documented consent (a specific opt-in, not just a past purchase), quiet hours (no sends before 8am or after 9pm), and immediate STOP compliance.
3. Skip urgency and financial-promise language ("act now," "free," "guaranteed") even in casual messages. Specific and calm beats hyped, every time.

**Script to practice today:**
Rewrite one upcoming message so it explicitly names where a personal detail came from, instead of stating it with no context.

**Question to ask the team:**
Do we have documented consent on file for marketing texts, separate from a client's purchase history? If we're not sure, who do we ask?
