# C3-M06 Huddle Card: Personalized Outreach Without Sounding Like Spam

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** If you could send the exact same message to fifty other clients unchanged, it's not personalized — it's a blast wearing a personalization costume.

## The Four-Part Test (say it out loud before you hit send)
1. **Specificity** — Does this reference something true and unique about THIS client?
2. **Reason to exist** — Is there a client-centered reason to reach out today?
3. **Value before ask** — Does the client get something before being asked for anything?
4. **Easy exit** — Can they ignore or decline this without friction or guilt?

## Quick Channel Guide
- **Text:** fast, casual — only if the client has texted back before or asked for it
- **Email:** detail, images, soft touch, no immediate reply expected
- **Call:** big moments, sensitive follow-ups, high-value pieces
- **Handwritten note:** milestone purchases and real thank-yous

## Say This, Not That
- Not: "As one of our valued clients..." → Say: "I remembered you mentioned..."
- Not: "Don't miss out!" → Say: "No rush at all, but..."
- Not: "We wanted to reach out about..." → Say: "Quick thought—"

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Pull up your last outreach message. Run it through the Four-Part Test right now — does it pass all four?
