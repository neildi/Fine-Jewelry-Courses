# Module 5 Self-Assessment Quiz: Life-Event Triggers

10 multiple-choice questions testing the terminal outcome: recognizing and responding appropriately to the full range of life-event triggers.

**1. What is the traditional gemstone associated with a 10th wedding anniversary?**
A. Pearl
B. Diamond
C. Ruby
D. Emerald
**Answer: B** - Three-stone diamond jewelry, symbolizing "past, present, and future," is a popular way to mark this milestone.

**2. Why is emerald often recommended as earrings or a pendant rather than a ring for a 20th anniversary?**
A. Emerald is too expensive for a ring
B. Emerald is relatively soft and more prone to damage, so pieces with less impact exposure are safer choices
C. Emerald only comes in earring settings
D. There is no durability consideration involved
**Answer: B** - This reflects a genuine gemological durability consideration, not just style preference.

**3. What does "the 25th anniversary is the silver year" mean in this module's context?**
A. Silver is the only acceptable metal for that anniversary
B. Silver is the traditional metal association for that milestone, valued partly for its high luster
C. It refers to a monetary gift amount
D. It has no connection to jewelry gifting traditions
**Answer: B** - Tradition assigns specific gemstones or metals to milestone years, and 25 is associated with silver.

**4. Which of these is a life milestone worth capturing in a client profile, beyond birthdays and anniversaries?**
A. A client's grocery shopping habits
B. A graduation, promotion, or retirement mentioned in conversation
C. Unrelated details about a client's coworkers
D. Speculation about a client's future plans
**Answer: B** - These are genuine future occasions worth remembering, even without an immediate sale.

**5. What does "the occasion engine" refer to in this module?**
A. A specific piece of CRM software
B. Predictable seasonal gifting windows (like Valentine's Day or Mother's Day) driven by the calendar rather than individual client dates
C. A machine used to manufacture jewelry
D. An automated discount generator
**Answer: B** - These seasonal windows create predictable demand you can plan around, independent of any specific client's personal dates.

**6. What should happen if you learn a client has experienced a significant loss and has an anniversary reminder scheduled soon?**
A. Let the automated reminder go out as scheduled
B. Pause the relevant automated trigger immediately
C. Send the reminder but add an apology
D. Delete the client's entire profile
**Answer: B** - An automated system can't account for life circumstances changing; a human needs to catch and pause this manually.

**7. What is the three-part structure recommended for a respectful condolence message?**
A. Question, advice, follow-up
B. Acknowledge, offer, close
C. Apologize, explain, promote
D. Ignore, wait, escalate
**Answer: B** - Acknowledge the loss plainly, offer something concrete that fits your role, and close in a way that removes any obligation to respond.

**8. What is the recommended timing for an initial condolence message, if you learn of a loss promptly?**
A. Immediately, within the hour
B. Within about 24 to 48 hours
C. Exactly one week later
D. Only after a full month has passed
**Answer: B** - This window is generally considered appropriately prompt without being overwhelming.

**9. What should be avoided in a condolence message to a client?**
A. Brevity and warmth
B. Intrusive questions about what happened, advice on how to grieve, or language that centers your own feelings
C. Offering to pause or adjust work on your side
D. Removing any pressure to respond
**Answer: B** - These add emotional burden to an already difficult time, rather than offering genuine support.

**10. What should happen to an anniversary-related automated trigger for a client going through a divorce?**
A. Nothing; automated triggers should never be changed
B. The trigger should be updated or paused, since the underlying occasion no longer applies
C. The trigger should be sped up to remind them more often
D. The client should be removed from the CRM entirely
**Answer: B** - Recognizing when a recorded date is no longer appropriate to act on is a core judgment skill this module teaches.
