## MUST VERIFY
- **The "10-20 points higher repeat-purchase rate" figure:** reported by WJewel, a jewelry retail software vendor with a commercial interest in this framing. Directionally consistent with general retention literature, but the precise range should be treated as one vendor's reported observation, not an independently audited industry statistic.
- **The "2-4 touches per year" ceiling:** presented by WJewel as operational guidance based on their own client base experience, not a peer-reviewed study. Reasonable, cautious default guidance, but not independently verified.
- **Any vendor-specific ROI or case-study figures:** should never be generalized to "any store using any CRM." This module deliberately excluded such figures from the published article text for this reason.
