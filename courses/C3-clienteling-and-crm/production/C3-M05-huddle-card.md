# Huddle Card: Life-Event Triggers

**Headline:** Know which anniversary means what. And know when a scheduled reminder is exactly the wrong thing to send.

**3 key points:**
1. Milestone anniversaries have real gemstone traditions: gold (1st), sapphire (5th), diamond (10th), ruby (15th), emerald (20th), silver (25th), pearl (30th), gold (50th). Reference them specifically.
2. Capture every life milestone a client mentions, engagement, graduation, promotion, retirement, even without an immediate sale. These are future triggers.
3. If you learn a client has experienced a loss or major life change, pause any related automated reminder immediately. Don't let a scheduled "happy anniversary" text go out on autopilot.

**Script to practice today:**
If needed: "I'm so sorry to hear about your loss. I'm thinking of you, and there's no need to respond, we'll be here whenever you're ready."

**Question to ask the team:**
Do we have a clear way to flag and pause a client's automated reminders the moment we learn about a life change? If not, what should that process look like?
