## MUST VERIFY
- **The 20-40% conversion rate / 80% non-conversion figure:** appears in sponsored trade content (National Jeweler, sponsored by Clientbook, a CRM vendor with a commercial interest in this framing). The direction (most in-store visits don't convert immediately) is broadly consistent with general retail conversion literature, but the precise range should be treated as an industry-reported estimate rather than an independently audited statistic.
- **Vendor-specific performance claims:** any figures describing a specific CRM platform's own reported results should be attributed explicitly to that vendor's own reporting and not generalized as typical results for all jewelry stores or all CRM tools.
