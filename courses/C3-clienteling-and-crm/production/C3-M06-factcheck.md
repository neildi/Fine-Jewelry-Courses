Status: Directionally accurate at a general level (e.g., U.S. TCPA and similar consumer-protection frameworks in other jurisdictions generally require consent for commercial text messaging). **2. "A phone number captured for a repair pickup is not the same as consent for marketing texts."**
Status: Accurate as a general consent-hygiene principle. Purpose-limited consent (operational/transactional) is commonly treated differently from marketing consent under most consumer-protection frameworks. No specific legal citation is made in the article; this is presented as a practical guideline, not legal advice.

Status: Presented and should remain framed as general best-practice guidance, not a rule to be applied universally regardless of client feedback. No external source is cited because this is an operational recommendation, not a factual/statistical claim.
