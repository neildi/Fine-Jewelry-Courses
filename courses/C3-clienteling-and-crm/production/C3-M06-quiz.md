# C3-M06 Quiz: Personalized Outreach Without Sounding Like Spam

**Instructions:** Answer all questions. This module's quiz emphasizes applying the Four-Part Test and matching channel/cadence to scenario, not memorizing definitions.

---

**1. A client received this text from her associate: "Hi Jennifer! As one of our valued clients, don't miss our end-of-season sale, 15% off through Sunday!" Which filter(s) of the Four-Part Test does this message fail?**
A) Specificity and reason to exist (business-driven, not client-driven)
B) Easy exit only
C) Value before ask only
D) It passes all four filters

**Correct answer: A**
Explanation: The message uses generic "valued clients" language (fails specificity) and the reason for the message is a store sale deadline, not anything true about Jennifer specifically (fails reason to exist).

---

**2. Which of the following is the BEST reason to send outreach, according to this module?**
A) It has been exactly 60 days since the client's last purchase, per the CRM cadence rule
B) New inventory just arrived that matches something the client specifically asked about
C) The store needs to hit a contact quota for the week
D) A promotional campaign is going out to the client's segment today

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: A genuine match to something the client specifically expressed interest in is a client-centered reason to exist. The others are business-driven timing disguised as personalization.

---

**3. A client has previously told an associate, "Please just email me, I really don't like getting texts." Two weeks later, the associate wants to notify her quickly about a new arrival and considers texting because it's faster. What should the associate do?**
A) Text her anyway since it's a quick, low-effort update
B) Call her instead, since a call is even more personal than a text
C) Respect her stated channel preference and email her
D) Wait until her next in-store visit to mention it

**Correct answer: C**
Explanation: Preference violations damage trust more than almost any content mistake. The client explicitly stated a channel preference, and it must be documented and honored regardless of convenience.

---

**4. Which scenario is the MOST appropriate use of a phone call rather than a text or email, per the module's channel guidance?**
A) Confirming a repair pickup time
B) Sending a routine restock notification to five clients
C) Following up sensitively after a client mentioned a personal loss
D) Sending a holiday greeting to the entire client book

**Correct answer: C**
Explanation: Phone calls signal real importance and are appropriate for sensitive or high-value moments. Restock notices to multiple clients and holiday greetings are lower-stakes and better suited to text/email; batch calling is impractical.

---

**5. An associate wants to text a client marketing content for the first time. What must be true before sending it, per the compliance guidance in this module?**
A) The associate has the client's phone number on file from a repair pickup
B) The client has given explicit consent to be contacted by text, documented in the CRM
C) The associate has texted the client's spouse before
D) The store's general marketing list includes the client's number

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Having a phone number for one purpose (e.g., a repair pickup) is not the same as consent for marketing texts. Explicit, documented consent is required before sending marketing content via text.

---

**6. Put the Anchor-Bridge-Offer-Exit framework in the correct order:**
A) Offer, Anchor, Exit, Bridge
B) Anchor, Bridge, Offer, Exit
C) Bridge, Offer, Anchor, Exit
D) Exit, Anchor, Bridge, Offer

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Anchor (specific true detail) → Bridge (why reaching out today) → Offer (the value/opportunity) → Exit (easy way to not respond).

---

**7. Which cadence approach is recommended for a warm client with no active project or reason to contact them?**
A) Weekly check-ins to stay top of mind
B) One meaningful touch roughly every 60-90 days, anchored to genuine reasons like life events
C) No contact until they reach out first
D) Daily texts during promotional periods

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The module recommends a general range of every 60-90 days for warm clients without an active reason, anchored primarily to life-event triggers rather than an arbitrary contact quota.

---

**8. A client responds "not right now" to an outreach message. Which response best reflects the module's guidance?**
A) Ask again in a few days in case she changes her mind
B) Stop all future contact with this client entirely
C) Acknowledge warmly, note you'll only reach out when something truly fits, and let it go without pressure
D) Escalate to a manager to close the sale

**Correct answer: C**
Explanation: An easy, pressure-free exit is one of the Four-Part Test filters. Respecting "not now" gracefully preserves trust and keeps the door open for future genuine outreach.

---
