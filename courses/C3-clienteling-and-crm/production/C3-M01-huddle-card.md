# Huddle Card: The Client Profile — What to Capture, What to Leave Out

**Headline:** A client you don't remember next year is a client you'll meet again somewhere else. Capture the seven fields that matter, and nothing more.

**3 key points:**
1. Capture these seven fields whenever you can: purchase date/occasion, partner details, ring sizes, birthday/marriage date, style preferences, purchase history, and language preference.
2. Ask naturally, at a real moment: during a sizing, a repair pickup, or right after a sale. "Can I take a few details so we can let you know about new pieces and remind you before your anniversary?"
3. Never write down an assumption. If a client didn't say it, it doesn't go in the file — no guesses about relationships, income, or reasons for buying.

**Script to practice today:**
"Can I take a few details so we can let you know if we have new pieces in your style, and send a reminder before your anniversary?"

**Question to ask the team:**
Pick one client file at random. Is everything in it something the client actually told us, or is anything an assumption we should remove?
