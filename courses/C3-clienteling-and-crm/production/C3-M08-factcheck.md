# C3-M08 Fact-Check: Building a Referral Engine Through Clienteling

## Verification Status: Verified (external research pass completed 2026-09-15) — General Best Practice Content, No Incentive Mechanics or Legal Claims Made

## Claims Reviewed

**1. "A referral is the highest-trust lead you will ever receive... referred leads convert at a far higher rate than cold leads."**
Status: VERIFIED, and if anything understated. Referral-conversion benchmark data shows referred leads converting at roughly 2x-10x cold leads depending on measurement and industry: B2B referral leads close around 25-26% versus 9-20% for cold pipeline, and lead-to-close data shows an even wider gap (3.63% vs 0.94%) in some analyses; other sources cite referred leads with 30% higher conversion than cold leads. Sources: "Referrals vs. Cold Leads: 3-4x Higher Conversion Rates" (salesgravy.com); "How Much B2B Sales Is on Referral" (syncgtm.com); "B2B Referral Marketing Stats 2026" (growsurf.com); "Referral Statistics" (worldmetrics.org, citing Neil Patel: 30% higher conversion). The article's qualitative phrasing ("tends to be far higher") is accurate and appropriately avoids overclaiming a single specific number, since actual figures vary widely by industry and measurement method — no correction needed.

**2. Three referral-asking moments (peak satisfaction, named mention, gift-giving occasion).**
Status: Not an externally sourced statistic — presented as practical, principle-based training guidance consistent with sales-psychology best practice (asking when goodwill is highest). No correction needed.

**3. No specific referral-incentive amounts, bonus structures, or discount percentages are recommended anywhere in the article, outline, quiz, or huddle card.**
Status: Confirmed intentional and unchanged. Any incentive/reward mechanics should go through separate compliance/legal review outside this training module.

**4. Capstone synthesis claims (that this module draws on Modules 1-7).**
