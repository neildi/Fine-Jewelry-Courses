## MUST VERIFY

- **Do not generalize the Altagamma "True Luxury" ultra-top-tier figures to a typical independent jewelry store's VIC segment.** These describe UHNWI-level global luxury spending and are included in the article only as broader calibration context, not as a claim about what a normal store's top clients spend.
