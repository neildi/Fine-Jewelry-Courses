## 1. Executive Summary & Verification

Course C3 Module 8 serves as the culminating capstone of the clienteling discipline. It equips advisors with the quantitative mechanics to audit their own personal book: Repeat Purchase Rate (RPR), Client Retention Rate (CRR), Client Acquisition Cost (CAC), and the Referral Coefficient.
