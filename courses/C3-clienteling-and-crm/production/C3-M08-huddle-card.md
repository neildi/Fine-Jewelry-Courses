# C3-M08 Huddle Card: Building a Referral Engine Through Clienteling

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Referrals are what good clienteling produces when you ask systematically instead of hoping by chance. Vague asks fail — specific, benefit-framed asks work.

## Three Moments to Ask
1. **Peak satisfaction** — post-purchase check-in once they've confirmed they love it (not at checkout).
2. **They name someone** — "my sister would love this" is your opening; don't let it pass.
3. **Gift-giving occasions** — clients shopping for others are already thinking about someone else's needs.

## Vague vs. Specific
- Vague (skip): "Know anyone looking for jewelry?"
- Specific (use): "If [person] ever wants to talk through options, I'd love to help them the way I've helped you — want me to send my card, or text them directly?"

## Frame It Around Them, Not You
Not: "Referrals really help my business."
Say: "I'd take great care of anyone you send my way — the same way I've tried to take care of you."

## Never Skip the Thank-You
The referral doesn't end when the new client walks in — it ends when the referrer feels genuinely thanked. Skipping this is the #1 way associates quietly kill their own referral pipeline.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Think of your happiest client from the last 30 days. What's the specific referral ask you could make to them this week?
