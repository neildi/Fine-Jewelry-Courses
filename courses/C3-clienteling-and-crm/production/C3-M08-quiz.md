# C3-M08 Quiz: Building a Referral Engine Through Clienteling

**Instructions:** Answer all questions. This module's quiz emphasizes correctly identifying referral moments, distinguishing specific asks from vague ones, and applying the capstone synthesis across the course.

---

**1. According to this module, why does simply saying "let me know if you know anyone looking for jewelry" usually fail as a referral ask?**
A) Clients find the topic of jewelry uncomfortable to discuss
B) It's vague, comes at the wrong moment, and doesn't have a visible benefit for the client
C) Most clients don't have any friends or family
D) It should only be asked by managers, not associates

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The module identifies three specific failure modes of generic referral asks: wrong moment, vague prompt, and no visible reason it benefits the client — not the topic or who is asking.

---

**2. Which of the following is the BEST moment to ask for a referral, per this module?**
A) Immediately at checkout, right after the client pays
B) During a warm post-purchase check-in when the client has confirmed they love the piece
C) The first time a client walks into the store
D) Only during scheduled promotional periods

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Peak satisfaction — such as a warm post-purchase check-in once the client has actually experienced and confirmed enjoying the piece — is one of the three key moments for asking, unlike point-of-sale which is too early.

---

**3. Which of these is the MORE effective, specific referral ask, per the module's guidance?**
A) "If you know anyone looking for jewelry, let me know!"
B) "If your sister ever wants to talk through options before she gets engaged, I'd love to help her — feel free to pass along my card."
C) "We'd really appreciate referrals if you have any."
D) "Do you know anyone who might want to buy something?"

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The specific version names a person, a context, and an easy next step, doing the mental work for the client instead of leaving them to generate an idea from an open-ended request.

---

**4. What is the recommended way to frame a referral ask, according to this module?**
A) Around how much referrals help the associate's sales numbers
B) Around the benefit to the client and the person they might refer
C) Around a mandatory company policy the client should be aware of
D) Around a comparison to competitor referral programs

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The module recommends reframing the ask from "help me" to "let me help someone you care about," centering the client's motivation to help a friend or family member rather than the associate's sales goals.

---

**5. An associate successfully receives a referral, and the new client makes a purchase. What should happen next, according to the module?**
A) Nothing further is needed — the new client's satisfaction is the goal
B) The associate should personally thank the person who made the referral
C) The referral should be reported to management only
D) The associate should immediately ask the new client for their own referral

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Closing the loop by personally thanking the referrer is described as essential — skipping it is one of the most common ways associates quietly kill their own future referral pipeline.

---

**6. A client mentions, "My brother's getting engaged next year." What should the associate do, per this module's guidance?**
A) Immediately ask the client for the brother's phone number to contact him directly without asking
B) Ignore it since it's too far in the future to matter
C) Note it in the CRM and revisit the specific opening at the appropriate time
D) Ask the client to bring the brother in that same week

**Correct answer: C**
Explanation: Named referral leads should be logged and followed up on at the right moment rather than acted on immediately with pressure, or forgotten because the timeline is further out.

---

**7. Which client is described as the best priority for a referral conversation, given limited time?**
A) A brand-new client who hasn't purchased yet
B) A top-tier VIC with a strong, positive recent relationship
C) A client who explicitly asked not to be contacted
D) A client who has never responded to any outreach

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The module recommends prioritizing referral conversations with the strongest-relationship, most-satisfied clients (often top-tier VICs from Module 4), since they tend to be the most valuable referral sources.

---

**8. This module is described as the course capstone. Which prior module's framework does it explicitly reuse to evaluate the quality of a referral thank-you or follow-up message?**
A) Module 3's CRM daily workflow steps
B) Module 6's Four-Part Test (specificity, reason to exist, value before ask, easy exit)
C) Module 2's calendar cadence chart
D) Module 1's client profile capture fields

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: While the capstone note synthesizes all seven prior modules, the module explicitly draws on Module 6's personalized-outreach principles (via the same underlying discipline of specific, genuine communication) for crafting referral asks and thank-yous, consistent with how Module 7 also reused the Four-Part Test.
