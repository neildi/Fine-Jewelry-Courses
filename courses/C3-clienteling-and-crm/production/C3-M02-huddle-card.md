# Huddle Card: The Clienteling Calendar

**Headline:** Silence loses clients. So does spam. The fix is the same six triggers, run on a schedule, every time.

**3 key points:**
1. Run the post-sale sequence on every sale: a 30-day check-in, a 90-day cleaning reminder, and a 365-day anniversary-of-purchase note.
2. Capture three dates (birthday, partner's birthday, anniversary) and message the gift-giver two weeks ahead, by text, referencing something specific from their file.
3. Cap it at 2-4 intentional touches per year per client. More than that, and unsubscribe rates climb. Quality beats volume every time.

**Script to practice today:**
"Your anniversary is coming up on [date]. If you want ideas, stop by or text us back, we have notes from your last visit."

**Question to ask the team:**
Pick one client who hasn't heard from us in 12+ months. What's a genuine reason (not a discount) we could reach out with this week?
