## 1. Executive Summary & Verification

Course C3 Module 2 transforms the vulnerable friction point of retail data collection into an act of white-glove luxury hospitality. Rather than treating client profiling as an administrative clipboard task, the module trains advisors to capture the Seven Core Fields through organic conversational touchpoints (ring sizing, repair intake, packaging, and high-resolution media sharing) while strictly respecting client boundaries and GDPR data-minimization mandates.

The audit verified three mandatory dimensions:
1. **Brand Compliance:** STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE for discount/mass-market retailers (`Kay Jewelers`, `Effy`, `Blue Nile`, `James Allen`, `Diamonds International`, `Zales`). Counter interactions benchmarked against high-jewelry maisons (**Cartier, Bulgari, Tacori, Hearts On Fire, Tiffany & Co., Stuller, Harry Winston, and Van Cleef & Arpels**).
2. **Attribution & White-Labeling:** 100% white-labeled under Jewelswell proprietary trademarks: ***The 7-Pillar Luxury Client Profiling Matrix™***, ***The Organic Discovery Timeline™***, and ***The Custodial Data Architecture™***. Zero third-party trainer citations.
3. **Pedagogical Polish & UI/UX:** Deployed the **Anti-Dropout Counter Simulation Dialogue Suite: 3 Master Scenarios** directly before Section 6. Verified all 6 embedded visual assets (including 2 custom SVG schematics and 4 GIA/historic museum archives).
