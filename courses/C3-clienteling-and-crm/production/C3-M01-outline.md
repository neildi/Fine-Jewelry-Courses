# C3 Module 1 Production Record

## Inputs
Module 1: The Client Profile: What to Capture, What to Leave Out
Persona: P1 The New Associate (secondary P2, P3)
KPI: Data-capture percentage; client retention rate; repeat-purchase rate
Terminal outcome: Build an accurate, useful client profile from a single conversation, capturing the details that drive future sales while leaving out anything judgmental, unnecessary, or collected without genuine permission

## Working title and alternates
1. The Client Profile: What to Capture, What to Leave Out (selected, matches C3 course README module title)
2. Alternate: The Stranger Who Bought a Ring Here Last Year
3. Alternate: Seven Fields, One Rule

## Scenario hook
A client buys an engagement ring in November; if nothing beyond the transaction is recorded, they are a stranger again by their anniversary eleven months later, per Gem Logic's own framing of this exact pattern.
