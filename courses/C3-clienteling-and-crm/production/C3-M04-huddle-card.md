# Huddle Card: Segmenting Your Book

**Headline:** A small slice of your book drives most of your future revenue. Do you know exactly who's in it, and are you still taking care of everyone else?

**3 key points:**
1. Rank clients using five signals together, not just total spend: revenue concentration, recency, purchase frequency, category depth, engagement, and relationship potential.
2. Decide deliberately how wide your top tier is (many brands now go beyond the traditional top 1-2%, up to 5-10%), based on your own book, not a guess.
3. Scale the same four things (accessibility, service, experiences, recognition) down through every tier. Don't reserve real attentiveness only for the very top.

**Script to practice today:**
Pull up one client who looks unremarkable by total spend but has been engaging well recently. Ask yourself: does their relationship potential deserve a higher tier than their number alone suggests?

**Question to ask the team:**
If you left the store tomorrow, would your top clients' history and preferences be findable by a colleague, or only in your head?
