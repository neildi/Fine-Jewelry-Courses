# C13-M01 Huddle Card: What Great Jewelry Sellers Have in Common

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** "Determined, persistent and persuasive, but not pushy" — that specific balance is what separates a great jewelry seller from a good general salesperson.

## The Jewelry-Specific Trait Cluster
- Personable, enthusiastic, genuine storytelling ability
- Real product knowledge that actually informs the customer
- Careful listener, creative problem solver
- Determined and persistent — but NEVER pushy
- Even-tempered, trustworthy, versatile

## The Cornerstone Trait for Luxury Specifically
Emotional intelligence — ahead of product knowledge, ahead of raw sales skill. Add: authentic passion, discretion, storytelling, poised confidence, adaptive communication.

## Serve, Don't Sell
Never size up a customer's spending power and adjust your effort. The person who looks unassuming might be your best client for the next twenty years.

## Drive AND Restraint, Not One or the Other
Too much drive with no restraint = pushy. Too much warmth with no drive = never closes. Great jewelry sellers have both.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Think of the best salesperson on our floor. What specifically do they do that balances persistence with genuine care for the customer?
