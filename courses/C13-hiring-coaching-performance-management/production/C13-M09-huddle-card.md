# C13-M09 Huddle Card: Recognition, Incentives and Commission Fairness

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** An unwritten split-sale rule isn't a policy — it's a future argument waiting to happen. Write it down before the dispute forces one.

## Recognition: Often and Specific
- "Great job" = noise
- "You turned that difficult return into a repeat customer by staying calm" = lands
- Weekly shout-outs and same-day recognition beat one big annual event

## Why Incentive Plans Fail
1. Vague or overly complex criteria
2. Different managers applying the rules differently
3. Nobody can actually see how their payout is calculated

## Gross Profit, Not Just Sales Volume
A commission tied to raw sales volume can quietly reward unsustainable discounting. A commission tied to gross profit rewards protecting the store's actual margin.

## Split-Sale Defaults (Jewelry-Industry Standard)
- **50/50** — a genuine turnover, second associate closes
- **75/25** — one associate did the groundwork (ordered, presented), a different one closes

## Don't Create Sharks
Pure individual commission can make people guard customers instead of sharing them. Pair it with a team bonus, proportionate to hours or sales contributed.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Do we have a written split-sale rule? If a dispute happened today, would everyone already know the answer?
