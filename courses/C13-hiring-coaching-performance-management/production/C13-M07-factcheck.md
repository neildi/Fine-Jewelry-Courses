# C13-M07 Fact-Check: Performance Reviews With Evidence

## Verification Status: The behavior-not-personality principle and the SBI model are both confirmed across an unusually large number of independent sources; the Nature/Botelho et al. rating-scale research is confirmed but its context limitation (online marketplace, not in-store retail review) is explicitly flagged rather than overstated

## Claims Reviewed

**1. The running evidence log (dated examples of strong work, misses, feedback given, goals progress, third-party comments) and its function in countering recency bias.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly and specifically from resources.rework.com's "Your First Performance Review as a Manager" article, which explicitly names this exact log structure and states it should be maintained "throughout the year" specifically to fight recency bias.

**2. The three-step "Evidence-Based Review Prep" discipline (evidence collection, bias-check against recency/halo/leniency bias, outcome framing).**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly and specifically from the same resources.rework.com article, which names this exact three-step sequence and its specific outcome-framing structure ("[person] did [behavior] in [context], which [effect]").

**3. The "behavior, not personality" writing principle.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across an unusually large number of independent sources: hr.wisc.edu's "Manager Writing Guide for Performance Review Comments," Culture Amp's "60 useful performance review phrases," Venngage's "200+ Performance Review Examples," hibob's "150+ performance review examples," AIHR's "192 Performance Review Phrases," PerformYard's "Negative Performance Review Examples," evalflow.com's "How to Write Performance Review Examples," Indeed's "45 Examples of Effective Performance Evaluation Phrases," LinkedIn's "How to Write Performance Review Comments," and status.net's "300 Examples for Performance Reviews." This is one of the most consistently and independently corroborated claims across this entire course's research to date.

**4. The SBI model (Situation, Behavior, Impact).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across multiple independent sources: hr.wisc.edu, Culture Amp, and AIHR's "192 Performance Review Phrases" article, all describing this identical three-part structure with matching definitions.

**5. This module's explicit connection of the SBI model to Module 5's observation-impact-alternative structure and Module 6's feedback structure.**
Status: ORIGINAL synthesis created for this module, connecting the confirmed general SBI framework to this course's own previously-established, independently-verified content. Not itself an externally sourced claim, though the structural parallel between these three frameworks (all being essentially the same fact-then-consequence discipline applied at different time horizons) is genuine and direct.

**6. The focused review structure (2-4 strengths, 1-2 growth areas, each anchored to a specific example, closing with a forward plan and follow-up commitment).**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced from Venngage's and status.net's articles, both independently recommending this same focused range and structure.

**7. The simpler-rating-scale bias-reduction research finding.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly from a Nature News & Views article (d41586-025-00304-y) discussing peer-reviewed research by Botelho et al. published in Nature, which found that switching from a five-star scale to a simpler binary scale eliminated racial bias in performance ratings in an online-marketplace context. This module explicitly and appropriately flags the context limitation: the original study examined online-marketplace ratings, not in-store retail performance reviews, and this module presents the underlying mechanism (finer granularity gives more room for bias to operate) as a reasonable, evidence-informed argument for simplicity rather than claiming the study's exact findings transfer directly to a retail review context.

**8. Worked scenario (the review that held up under scrutiny due to a full evidence log) and practice exercise.**
Status: ORIGINAL illustrative synthesis created for this module, not externally sourced cases.
