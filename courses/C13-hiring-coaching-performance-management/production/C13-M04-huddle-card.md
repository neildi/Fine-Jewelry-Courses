# C13-M04 Huddle Card: The 30-Day Onboarding Plan

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** A new hire's first 90 days shouldn't be improvised. Learn, then Contribute, then Own — in that order, with the manager stepping back a little at each stage.

## The Three Phases
- **Days 1-30: LEARN** — buddy assigned day one, weekly 1:1s, product training, shadowing
- **Days 31-60: CONTRIBUTE** — solo customer interactions, objection-handling practice
- **Days 61-90: OWN** — partial ramp-quota (NOT full quota yet), one process-improvement suggestion

## Buddies Are Not a Replacement for Compliance Training
A peer buddy helps with "how do I actually do this" and team integration. But security protocols and policy training still need to happen formally — don't let informal buddy-learning replace it.

## Check Every Onboarding Plan Against Four C's
- **Compliance** — rules and policies
- **Clarification** — the role, from our own role profile
- **Culture** — our values, our customer-service ethos
- **Connection** — real relationships with peers and managers

Skip Culture and Connection, and you get a technically trained hire who still doesn't feel like part of the team.

## One Page. Before Day One.
Not twenty pages. Not something they discover piecemeal. One page, shared before their first shift.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Look at our last new hire's actual first 30 days. Which of the four C's got the least attention?
