# C13-M03 Huddle Card: Structured Interviews and Auditions

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** A warm conversation isn't a hiring tool. The same questions, the same rubric, for every candidate — that's what actually predicts who'll succeed.

## Build Interview Questions Straight From the Role Profile
Every must-have competency gets one question. No must-have without a matching question.

## STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result
Listen hardest for Action and Result — candidates get vague exactly there when the story isn't as strong as it sounds.

## Module 1's Traits, as Real Questions
- Product passion → "Describe a piece of jewelry you personally love."
- Listening/restraint → "Tell me about a time a customer said they were 'just looking.'"
- Persistence without pushiness → "How did you follow up with someone who wanted to think it over?"

## The Role-Play Is a Real Audition
Watch for: does the candidate ask before pitching? Does the pitch actually reference what the "customer" said? Do they handle pushback with real empathy, not scripted lines?

## BARS: Write Down What Each Score Actually Looks Like
Don't just rate "communication, 1-5." Write out what a 1, a 3, and a 5 LOOK like, specifically. That's what keeps two interviewers from scoring the same answer completely differently.

## Calibrate Before You Interview Anyone Real
Score a few practice examples together first. If you and a colleague land more than a point apart, talk it through using the rubric language — not your gut.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Pick one must-have from our current role profile. What's the specific question, and what does a "5" answer actually sound like?
