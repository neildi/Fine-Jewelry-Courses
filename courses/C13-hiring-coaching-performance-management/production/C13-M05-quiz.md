# C13-M05 Quiz: Floor Coaching: Observe, Debrief, Practice

**Instructions:** Answer all questions.

---

**1. What is the core floor-coaching loop this module describes, and how quickly should the debrief happen?**
A) Observe, then wait a week before discussing anything
B) Observe, debrief (within roughly five minutes), practice (a specific commitment for the next interaction, followed up on later)
C) Only debrief; observation and practice are unnecessary
D) The loop has no meaningful time constraint

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Coaching value decays quickly once too much time passes between the observed behavior and the conversation about it.

---

**2. What is the observation-impact-alternative debrief structure, and why does the ordering matter?**
A) Always start with a judgment, then explain why it matters
B) State a specific, factual observation with no judgment attached, name the actual impact that behavior had, and only then offer an alternative — an associate can disagree with an interpretation, but a specific, accurately observed behavior is much harder to argue with
C) Only the alternative matters; observation and impact are optional
D) There is no meaningful order to an effective debrief

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This ordering keeps the conversation productive rather than defensive.

---

**3. Why does this module recommend asking an associate to self-assess before offering any observation?**
A) Self-assessment has no effect on how feedback is received
B) It surfaces whether the associate already has accurate self-awareness, and people generally accept feedback more readily when they've already started the diagnosis themselves
C) Self-assessment should always replace manager feedback entirely
D) Only new hires should ever be asked to self-assess; experienced associates should not

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This makes the eventual coaching land better than receiving feedback cold.

---

**4. What does the GROW model stand for, and what specific discipline applies to the Options step?**
A) Growth, Revenue, Ownership, Wins — with no specific discipline for any step
B) Goal, Reality, Options, Way Forward (or Will) — the manager should let the associate generate options first before adding any of their own, since self-generated options get better buy-in
C) GROW has no standard, agreed-upon meaning across coaching sources
D) Goal, Rules, Obstacles, Warnings

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This four-part structure is confirmed across an unusually large number of independent coaching sources.

---

**5. What is the five-step structured debrief sequence this module describes for coaching visits?**
A) Ignore, criticize, repeat, punish, forget
B) Self-assessment first, affirm one specific strength, name exactly one development focus, get a specific commitment, and follow up at the next observation
C) Only affirm strengths; never name any development focus
D) There is no recommended sequence; debriefs should be entirely improvised

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The final follow-up step is what separates genuine coaching from disconnected comments; a commitment never checked on quietly teaches an associate that it didn't really matter.

---

**6. Why does this module specify naming exactly one development focus, rather than several, during a debrief?**
A) Naming multiple development areas always produces faster improvement
B) An associate walking away with five things to fix rarely improves on any of them, so naming exactly one keeps the coaching focused and actionable
C) Development focuses should never be named at all during a debrief
D) The number of development focuses named has no effect on outcomes

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This specific discipline is independently corroborated across multiple structured-coaching sources.

---

**7. Why does this module argue that floor coaching should watch for the same trait cluster established in Module 1 and screened for in Module 3?**
A) Hiring criteria and ongoing coaching standards should always be completely different and unrelated
B) An associate hired specifically because they demonstrated genuine listening, authentic product enthusiasm, and appropriate persistence should be coached against that same standard on the floor, not a separately invented one
C) Trait clusters are only relevant during hiring and become irrelevant immediately after a candidate is hired
D) Coaching should focus exclusively on sales numbers, with no attention to underlying behavioral traits

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This maintains consistency between what the store screens for at hiring and what it actually reinforces on the floor.

---

**8. In the worked scenario, what specific alternative did the manager offer only after stating the observation, naming the impact, and asking for the associate's own take?**
A) The manager simply told the associate to talk less, with no further detail
B) Leading with a discovery question about the occasion first, then framing technical detail around what the customer actually said they cared about
C) The manager offered no alternative at all, ending the conversation after stating the observation
D) The manager suggested discontinuing all technical product explanations entirely

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The associate, who had already sensed something felt off but couldn't name it, committed to leading with one discovery question on the next customer interaction.
