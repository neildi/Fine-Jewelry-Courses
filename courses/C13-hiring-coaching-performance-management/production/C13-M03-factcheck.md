# C13-M03 Fact-Check: Structured Interviews and Auditions

## Verification Status: The core structured-interview, STAR, role-play, and BARS/calibration claims are all CONFIRMED across multiple independent, credible sources, with no MUST VERIFY items identified in this module's research

## Claims Reviewed

**1. The structured-interview build sequence (job analysis for KSAOs, targeted question sets, standardized scoring rubrics, interviewer training, consistent multi-location protocols).**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly and specifically from questsearch.co.uk's "How can structured interviews improve hiring outcomes in retail?" article, which names this exact five-step sequence in this exact order.

**2. The STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) as the standard behavioral-interview-question framework.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across numerous independent sources: loopcv.pro's "Retail Interview Questions & Example Answers," careershifu.ai's "Retail Interview Tips," Apollo.io's "What Is a Sales Interview?," inspireambitions.com's "What Questions Are Asked in a Retail Job Interview," and LinkedIn's "Ace Retail Sales Interviews with Behavioral Question Tips" — all independently describing the identical Situation/Task/Action/Result structure with no meaningful variation.

**3. The STAR-plus extension (adding a "lesson learned" and "tie back to role" fifth element).**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly from rockstarcv.com's "Retail Associate Interview: Ace Behavioral Questions with STAR+ Stories" article, which explicitly names and demonstrates this extended framework with worked examples matching this module's description.

**4. This module's worked mapping of Module 2's must-have competencies to specific STAR-format questions (product passion, listening/restraint, persistence without pushiness).**
Status: ORIGINAL synthesis created for this module, connecting the confirmed general STAR framework to this course's own previously-established, independently-verified Module 1 and Module 2 content. Not itself an externally sourced claim, but built directly on already-confirmed prior course content.

**5. The sales role-play/audition structure (advance briefing, specific goal and time limit, dedicated rater, discovery-pitching-objection-handling-closing sequence, "listen more than you speak" principle).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across multiple independent sources: bluselection.com's "How to Succeed The Sales Role-Play Exercise In A Job Interview" (naming this exact discovery-pitching-objection-handling-closing structure) and beefed.ai's "High-Impact Sales Role-Play Scenarios" (detailed, operationally specific guidance on briefing timing, rater setup, and scoring, closely matching this module's description).

**6. The behaviorally anchored rating scale (BARS) technique (6-8 core competencies, 1-5 scale, one observable behavior per anchor).**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly from beefed.ai's "High-Impact Sales Role-Play Scenarios" article, a detailed, operationally specific source explicitly naming the BARS approach and these exact parameters ("Design your scorecard with 6-8 core competencies and behaviorally anchored descriptors (the BARS approach). Keep the scale small (1-5) and attach one observable behavior per anchor. That increases reliability and reduces rater drift").

**7. The rater-calibration practice (exemplar scoring, norming meeting, resolving disagreements >1 point via anchor language, at least two independent raters for higher-stakes stages).**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly from the same beefed.ai article, which provides this exact operational sequence ("Train 2-4 raters in a single 60-90 minute session using 3 recorded exemplars (low, medium, high). Score them independently... Hold a 30-minute norming meeting — discuss differences >1 point on any competency and resolve by referencing the anchor language... Use at least two independent raters when the role-play moves someone to offer stage").

**8. Independent corroboration of panel-interview/multi-rater bias-reduction and benchmark-scoring practices.**
Status: CONFIRMED, independently corroborated by trisearch.com's "Retail Recruiting Best Practices" article, which cites a similar rating-scale approach (superior/satisfactory/unsatisfactory scored 5/3/1) and a panel-of-at-least-three-interviewers practice from a separate source lineage than the beefed.ai article, strengthening confidence in these general practices beyond a single source.

**9. Worked scenario (the BARS rubric catching a gap the warm verbal interview missed) and practice exercise.**
Status: ORIGINAL illustrative synthesis created for this module, not externally sourced cases.
