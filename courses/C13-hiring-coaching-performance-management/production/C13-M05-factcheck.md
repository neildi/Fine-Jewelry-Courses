# C13-M05 Fact-Check: Floor Coaching: Observe, Debrief, Practice

## Verification Status: The observation-impact-alternative structure, the GROW model (confirmed across an unusually large number of independent sources), and the five-step structured debrief sequence are all well-corroborated, with no MUST VERIFY items identified in this module's research

## Claims Reviewed

**1. The observation-impact-alternative three-part debrief structure, and the recommendation to apply it to role-play debriefs as well as real interactions.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly and specifically from HubSpot's "Sales coaching: Examples, models, techniques & best practices" article, which explicitly names this exact structure with matching example language ("Observation: What specifically happened?... Impact: What did that behavior lead to?...") and explicitly states "After role-play, debrief using the same framework as for real calls: observation, impact, and alternative."

**2. The "ask the rep to self-assess before weighing in" opening move.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly from the same HubSpot article ("Start by asking the rep how they think they're performing. Let them self-assess before weighing in.").

**3. The GROW model (Goal, Reality, Options, Way Forward/Will) as the standard developmental coaching-conversation framework.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across an unusually large number of independent sources: HubSpot, mtdsalestraining.com's "5 Sales Coaching Models," Salesloft's "The Guide to Sales Coaching," johnnygrow.com's "The #1 Sales Coaching Model," mysalescoach.com's "Sales Coaching Techniques," investra.io's "Building A Coaching Culture," socoselling.com's "7 Popular Coaching Models," and Klozers' "How to use the GROW Sales Coaching Model" — all independently describing the identical four-part structure, with only minor wording variation (Options vs. Obstacles/Options; Will vs. Way Forward). This level of independent convergence gives strong confidence in this specific framework.

**4. The "let the rep generate options before offering your own" and "one thing, agreed at the start" (for Goal) principles.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly from HubSpot's article ("Let them self-assess before weighing in... [Options] Let the rep generate these before offering any of your own") and mysalescoach.com's article ("Goal: What are we improving today? One thing, agreed between both people at the start... Options: What could you do differently? Let the rep generate these before offering any of your own").

**5. The five-step structured debrief sequence (self-assessment, affirm one specific strength, name one development focus, get a commitment, follow up at the next visit), including the five-minute-maximum debrief timing.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly and specifically from resources.rework.com's "Sales Capability and Coaching in FMCG" article, a detailed field-sales-coaching source explicitly naming this exact five-step sequence ("Post-call debrief. Immediately after the call, before the next outlet. Five minutes maximum. Step 1: Self-assessment first... Step 2: Affirm one specific strength... Step 3: Name one development focus... Step 4: Get a commitment... Step 5: Follow up at the next call"). This source describes the sequence in an FMCG field-sales context; this module adapts it to a jewelry retail floor-coaching context, which is a reasonable, low-risk translation given the structural similarity between field sales-call coaching and retail floor coaching.

**6. Independent corroboration of the "one specific strength, one suggestion for improvement" pairing.**
Status: CONFIRMED, independently corroborated by xenia.team's "Retail Sales Training" article, which describes a closely matching retail-specific practice ("Compliment something they did well, maybe their energy or ability to quickly find the right size for a customer. Then, offer one suggestion for improvement").

**7. This module's explicit tie connecting floor-coaching observation criteria back to Module 1's trait cluster and Module 3's interview screening.**
Status: ORIGINAL synthesis created for this module, connecting the confirmed general coaching frameworks above to this course's own previously-established, independently-verified content. Not itself an externally sourced claim, but built directly on already-confirmed prior course content.

**8. Worked scenario (the debrief that changed behavior around leading with discovery questions) and practice exercise.**
Status: ORIGINAL illustrative synthesis created for this module, not externally sourced cases.
