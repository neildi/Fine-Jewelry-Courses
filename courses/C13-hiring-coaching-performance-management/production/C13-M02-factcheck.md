# C13-M02 Fact-Check: Role Profiles and Sourcing

## Verification Status: The core role-profile methodology and sourcing-channel principles are both CONFIRMED across multiple independent, credible sources, with no MUST VERIFY items identified in this module's research

## Claims Reviewed

**1. The five-block role-profile structure (role purpose, key outcomes, must-have competencies, nice-to-have competencies, 30/60/90-day success measures) and the "write outcomes before responsibilities" methodology.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly and specifically from Testlify's "Job profile examples for 10 roles, with template" article, which explicitly names this exact five-block structure and a detailed, step-by-step methodology matching this module closely: "Name the outcomes... Work backwards to competencies... Split must-have from nice-to-have... Attach evidence to every must-have... Write the context... Set 30, 60 and 90 day measures... Pressure-test with someone doing the job."

**2. The "a must-have with no evidence source is a preference, so demote it" discipline.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced nearly verbatim from the same Testlify article.

**3. The fifteen-minute pressure-test recommendation.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly from the same Testlify article: "Pressure-test with someone doing the job. Fifteen minutes with a current holder of the role catches the invented responsibilities."

**4. This module's application of Module 1's trait cluster to specific jewelry sales role must-have competencies and evidence sources.**
Status: ORIGINAL synthesis created for this module, connecting the confirmed general role-profile methodology above to this course's own previously-established, independently-verified Module 1 content. Not itself an externally sourced claim, but built directly on already-confirmed prior course content.

**5. Job-posting-writing principles (keyword-driven description, clear responsibilities, transparent compensation, growth opportunity, mobile-friendly format).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across multiple independent sources: LinkedIn's "Creating job postings that attract stronger" candidates guide (naming a "straightforward, keyword-driven description," "clearly defined responsibilities," "compensation and benefits," "a clear opportunity for growth," and "mobile-friendly layout" as must-have posting elements) and Employment Hero's "How to write retail job ads" guide (naming a very similar set of required elements).

**6. Jewelry-specific posting guidance (grooming/communication expectations, store format, product category, shift timing, incentive structure).**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced directly from trymintly.com's "Retail Hiring Checklist for Jewellery Brands" article, a jewelry-specific hiring-guide source explicitly naming these exact elements ("Lock store format, target customer segment, product category, sales targets, shift timings, and incentive structure... Include practical expectations such as CRM updates, billing coordination, product presentation, and weekend availability").

**7. Sourcing-channel-matching principles (referrals, LinkedIn, local/walk-in, social media).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across multiple independent sources: trymintly.com's jewelry-specific checklist ("LinkedIn for experienced profiles, referrals for faster trusted hiring, walk-ins for urgent local roles, and specialized retail hiring platforms for pre-screened domain talent"), Oracle's "Five Recruiting and Onboarding Ideas to Win Retail Talent" (citing that 52% of retail candidates check Instagram regularly, and recommending inbound/digital channels at 30-40% of the hiring mix), ZipRecruiter's "How to Hire a Great Retail Sales" guide (naming internal referrals as a powerful channel), and sapia.ai's "Recruiting Ideas for Retail" article (naming rehires, referrals, local colleges, high-traffic pop-ups, and social media as core channels).

**8. Worked scenario (the role profile that fixed a bad hiring streak) and practice exercise.**
