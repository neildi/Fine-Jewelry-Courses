# C13-M07 Huddle Card: Performance Reviews With Evidence

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Behavior, not personality. Always. "Missed two deadlines" is fixable. "Disorganized" is just a label.

## Keep a Running Log, Starting Now
Don't write reviews from memory in the final week. Dated entries: what happened, when, what it led to. Feed it straight from your Module 5 debrief notes and Module 6 one-on-one notes.

## Three Steps Before You Write Anything
1. **Collect evidence** — the WHOLE period, not just the last month
2. **Bias-check** — would you rate this the same if last month had gone differently?
3. **Frame the outcome** — "did X, in context Y, which caused Z"

## SBI: Situation, Behavior, Impact
Same structure as our floor debriefs and one-on-ones — now written down formally.
- **Situation**: when and where, specifically
- **Behavior**: exactly what happened, no interpretation
- **Impact**: what it actually led to

## Keep It Focused
2-4 strengths. 1-2 growth areas. Each with a real, dated example. Close with a plan and a follow-up date.

## Simpler Scales, Less Bias
If you use numbers at all, keep the scale simple. Research shows finer scales give more room for unconscious bias to creep in.
