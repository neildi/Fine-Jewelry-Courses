# C13-M04 Quiz: The 30-Day Onboarding Plan

**Instructions:** Answer all questions.

---

**1. What are the three phases of the onboarding structure this module describes, and their approximate day ranges?**
A) Hire, fire, rehire
B) Learn (days 1-30), Contribute (days 31-60), Own (days 61-90)
C) Morning, afternoon, evening
D) There is no meaningful phase structure to onboarding

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The manager's role shifts across these phases, from close pairing and weekly check-ins toward stepping back and running a full performance check-in by day 90.

---

**2. What does this module recommend regarding a new hire's sales target during the days 61-90 "Own" phase?**
A) A new hire should be expected to hit full quota immediately in their first quarter
B) A partial, not full, ramp-quota target, since a new hire genuinely ramping in a high-consideration, high-ticket category like jewelry should not be expected to hit full productivity in their first quarter
C) No sales target of any kind should ever be set during onboarding
D) The target should be identical to an experienced associate's target from day one

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This partial-target principle reflects the genuine ramp curve specific to jewelry's high-ticket, high-consideration sales category.

---

**3. What distinct function does a peer buddy serve, separate from manager coaching?**
A) A buddy has no distinct function and duplicates the manager's role exactly
B) A new hire is often more comfortable asking a peer "how do I actually do this" than asking their manager, and the buddy relationship accelerates informal cultural integration a formal training schedule doesn't cover
C) Buddies should replace managers entirely in the onboarding process
D) Peer buddies are only useful for senior-level hires, never entry-level ones

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The buddy relationship specifically supports both practical skill-transfer and informal team integration.

---

**4. What jewelry-specific caveat does this module add to the peer buddy system?**
A) Jewelry retailers should never use buddy systems under any circumstances
B) Buddy/mentorship pairing must run alongside, not instead of, formal compliance and security-protocol training specific to a jewelry environment
C) Only the store owner, never a peer, should ever serve as a buddy
D) Buddy pairing should replace all formal training entirely

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: A new hire in a jewelry environment specifically needs to absorb security and loss-prevention protocols early and formally, not only informally from a buddy's example.

---

**5. What are the "four C's" this module identifies as necessary components of a complete onboarding plan?**
A) Cost, Cash, Credit, Commission
B) Compliance, Clarification, Culture, and Connection
C) Customers, Competitors, Categories, Channels
D) There is no consistent framework for what onboarding should cover

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: A plan that only covers task-level training while skipping culture and connection produces a technically competent hire who still doesn't feel like part of the team.

---

**6. What format and timing does this module recommend for the onboarding plan document itself?**
A) A twenty-page detailed manual, distributed after the first month
B) A single page, shared with the new hire before their first day, starting with basics (name, role, start date, manager, key people to meet, day-one tools/access checklist)
C) The plan should never be written down or shared with the new hire at all
D) A plan should only be shared verbally, with no written document

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Sharing the plan in advance lets a new hire arrive on day one already oriented to what the next ninety days will look like.

---

**7. In the worked scenario, what specific gap explained the new hire's disengagement in week three, despite strong technical onboarding?**
A) The new hire lacked basic product knowledge
B) The onboarding plan had been almost entirely compliance and clarification, with no deliberate culture or connection component, and no buddy had been formally assigned
C) The new hire was simply a poor fit for the role from the start
D) The store had no compliance training program at all

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Assigning a peer buddy and deliberately including the new hire in informal team routines reversed the disengagement within two weeks.

---

**8. Why does this module recommend that a manager step back during the days 61-90 "Own" phase, after close involvement in phase one?**
A) A manager should always be equally involved at the same intensity throughout all ninety days
B) The manager's role deliberately shifts from pairing and weekly check-ins toward stepping back, since day 90 is the point at which the new hire should be driving an initiative without a safety net and receiving a genuine, complete performance check-in
C) Managers should have no involvement whatsoever after the first week
D) There is no meaningful shift in the manager's role across the three phases

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This progression reflects the new hire's growing independence across the three phases, from learning to contributing to owning.
