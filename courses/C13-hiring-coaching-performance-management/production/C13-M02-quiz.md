# C13-M02 Quiz: Role Profiles and Sourcing

**Instructions:** Answer all questions.

---

**1. What are the five blocks of a well-structured role profile, in order?**
A) Salary, benefits, schedule, dress code, and start date
B) Role purpose, key outcomes, must-have competencies, nice-to-have competencies, and 30/60/90-day success measures
C) Job title, company logo, application deadline, and contact email
D) There is no consistent structure to an effective role profile

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Key outcomes are deliberately written before day-to-day responsibilities are listed, working backward from what has to be true twelve months in for the hire to be obviously worth it.

---

**2. What discipline does this module apply to "must-have" competencies specifically?**
A) Must-have competencies never need any supporting evidence
B) Every must-have competency needs a named piece of evidence that verifies it (a specific interview question, work sample, or reference-check question); a competency with no evidence source is actually a preference and should be demoted
C) All competencies should always be listed as must-have, regardless of importance
D) Must-have competencies should be kept secret from candidates

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This discipline directly connects to Module 3, where structured interview questions get built specifically to generate the evidence each must-have competency requires.

---

**3. How does this module recommend translating Module 1's trait cluster into a jewelry sales role profile's must-have competencies?**
A) Module 1's traits have no relevance to writing a role profile
B) Traits like genuine product passion, listening skill and restraint, and persistence without pushiness each become a specific must-have competency, paired with a specific evidenced interview question or role-play exercise
C) Only sales-closing ability should be listed as a competency
D) Role profiles should be written without any reference to specific desired traits

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This directly traces Module 1's research-backed trait cluster into concrete, evidence-attached hiring criteria.

---

**4. What is the fifteen-minute pressure-test this module recommends before posting a role?**
A) Timing how quickly a candidate can complete an application form
B) Spending fifteen minutes with someone currently doing the job to catch invented responsibilities that sound reasonable on paper but don't reflect how the job is really done
C) A fifteen-minute unpaid trial shift for every applicant
D) A fifteen-minute background check on every candidate

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This low-cost check prevents a misleading role profile from wasting time in the interview process later.

---

**5. What specific practical details does jewelry-specific hiring guidance recommend including in a job posting, beyond general retail posting principles?**
A) No jewelry-specific details are ever necessary in a job posting
B) Store format, product category, shift timing (including weekend availability), the store's incentive/commission structure, and grooming/communication expectations
C) Only the store's total annual revenue
D) A full list of every piece of inventory currently in stock

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: These are legitimate, real considerations in a luxury retail environment that shouldn't be left for a candidate to discover only after applying.

---

**6. Which sourcing channel is consistently identified as particularly strong for retail hiring specifically, due to an implicit vouching effect?**
A) Random cold-calling of strangers
B) Employee referrals, since a referred candidate arrives with an informal vouching from someone who already understands the job and culture
C) Print newspaper classified ads exclusively
D) Sourcing has no meaningful channel differences; all channels perform identically

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This is confirmed across multiple independent retail-recruiting sources.

---

**7. How should sourcing channels be matched to candidate type, according to this module?**
A) Every channel should be used identically for every type of role
B) LinkedIn works well for experienced, credentialed candidates; local walk-ins and community channels work well for urgent entry-level roles; social media (particularly Instagram) reaches passive candidates not actively browsing job boards
C) Only one single channel should ever be used, regardless of role type
D) Sourcing channel choice has no measurable effect on hiring outcomes

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Matching channel to candidate type, rather than posting everywhere identically, is a well-corroborated sourcing principle.

---

**8. In the worked scenario, what specific change to the role profile and job posting corresponded with more consistent client feedback for the next hire?**
A) The store simply raised the offered salary with no other changes
B) The rebuilt role profile added must-have competencies for listening skill and genuine product passion, each with evidence attached, shifting the posting's tone from "closer wanted" to "trusted advisor wanted"
C) The store stopped requiring any interview process at all
D) The store hired the very next applicant regardless of fit

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The store's previous postings had emphasized general-sales-drive language almost exclusively — exactly the traits Module 1 identified as necessary but insufficient on their own.
