# C13-M05 Huddle Card: Floor Coaching: Observe, Debrief, Practice

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Observation → Impact → Alternative. In that order. Every time.

## The Coaching Loop
**Observe** (scheduled, not just reactive) → **Debrief** (within 5 minutes) → **Practice** (one specific commitment) → follow up next time

## Debrief in This Exact Order
1. **Observation** — what specifically happened (factual, no judgment)
2. **Impact** — what that behavior actually led to
3. **Alternative** — offered only after asking what THEY noticed first

## Ask Before You Tell
"How do you think that went?" first. People accept coaching better when they've already started diagnosing it themselves.

## GROW for the Bigger Conversation
- **Goal** — one thing we're improving today
- **Reality** — what's actually happening (not what should be)
- **Options** — let THEM generate ideas first
- **Way Forward** — one specific commitment

## One Development Focus. Not Five.
Affirm one specific strength. Name ONE thing to work on. Get a commitment. Follow up next time — that follow-up is what makes it real coaching, not just a comment.

## Coach the Same Standard We Hired For
Listening before pitching. Genuine enthusiasm. Persistence that respects the customer's space. Same trait cluster from day one of the interview process.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Think about your last debrief with an associate. Did you lead with judgment, or with a specific, factual observation?
