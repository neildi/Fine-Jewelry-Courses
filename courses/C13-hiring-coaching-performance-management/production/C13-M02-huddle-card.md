# C13-M02 Huddle Card: Role Profiles and Sourcing

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** If a "must-have" on a job posting has no way to actually check for it in the interview, it's not a must-have — it's a wish.

## The Five-Block Role Profile
1. **Purpose** — one sentence, why this role exists
2. **Key outcomes** — 3-5 results, written BEFORE responsibilities
3. **Must-haves** — 4-6, each with a way to verify it
4. **Nice-to-haves** — 2-3, explicitly not screened on
5. **30/60/90-day success measures**

## Evidence or Demote
Every must-have needs a specific interview question, work sample, or reference check attached. No evidence source? It's a preference, not a must-have.

## Module 1's Traits, Made Concrete
- Product passion → "Describe a piece of jewelry you personally love" (listen for authentic detail)
- Listening/restraint → role-play: customer says "just looking"
- Persistence without pushiness → "How have you followed up with someone who wanted to think it over?"

## Pressure-Test in 15 Minutes
Before you post anything, walk the draft profile past a current top performer. They'll catch the invented responsibilities you didn't realize you wrote.

## Match Channel to Candidate
- Referrals → fastest, most trusted
- LinkedIn → experienced/credentialed hires
- Walk-ins/local → urgent entry-level
- Instagram/social → passive candidates not job-hunting
