# C13-M08 Huddle Card: Managing Underperformance

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Never write "bad attitude." Write what actually happened, when, and what it led to. That's the difference between documentation that helps and documentation that fails everyone.

## The Four Steps, In Order
1. **Verbal warning** — direct, documented conversation
2. **Written warning** — formal, if it persists
3. **PIP / final warning** — structured, time-bound, last chance
4. **Termination** — only if the pattern continues despite real support

## What a PIP Actually Needs
- Clear expectations
- The factual issue (pull straight from your evidence log)
- SMART goals
- A check-in schedule
- Stated consequences
- 60-90 days, typically

## The Documentation Test
Could a stranger with zero context read this and understand exactly what happened and what's expected? If not, it's too vague.

## Never Criticize Without Coaching + a Deadline
"This isn't working" helps no one. "Here's specifically what needs to change, here's how, and here's the date we check back" actually gives someone a real chance.

## Get the Signature
Not agreement — acknowledgment. It confirms the conversation happened and protects everyone.

## Important
This is operational guidance, not legal advice. Employment law varies by location — confirm specifics with HR or counsel before finalizing policy.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
If you had to document one real performance concern today, could you do it without a single vague, character-based phrase?
