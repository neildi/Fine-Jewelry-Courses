# C13-M10 Huddle Card: Building Bench Strength for Your Own Promotion

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Your best seller and your best future manager might not be the same person. Watch for who helps without being asked and makes everyone around them better.

## Three Signals of Real Leadership Potential
1. Asks "why," not just "what" — thinking ahead
2. Helps without being asked — notices and steps in
3. Makes others better — people naturally go to them for help

None of these require topping the sales leaderboard.

## Say It Out Loud
Found your top two or three? Tell them directly: "I see leadership ability in you. Here's what the path could look like." Potential nobody names often goes unrecognized by the person who has it.

## Not Everyone Is Ready at the Same Speed
- Ready now
- Ready within a year
- Ready within 2-3 years
- Longer-term potential

Match the development to the actual readiness.

## Delegate to Develop, Not Just to Offload
- Clear outcome (what result, specifically)
- The right person (who needs this growth, not just who's available)
- Explain why
- Define their actual authority
- Agree on check-ins
- Close the loop with real feedback

## Never Delegate
Performance reviews. Discipline. Anything requiring your own specific authority.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Who on our team shows these three signals but has never been told directly that we see leadership potential in them?
