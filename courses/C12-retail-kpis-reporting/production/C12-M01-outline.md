# C12-M01 Outline: The KPI Vocabulary of Jewelry Retail

## Module Metadata
- Course: C12 - Retail KPIs & Reporting for Jewelry Store Leaders
- Module: 1 of 8
- Persona: P7 (First-Time Store Leader), P8 (Multi-Store Leader), per official course architecture
- Working title: The KPI Vocabulary of Jewelry Retail
- Learning objective: Store leaders can define and correctly calculate conversion rate, ATV/UPT, sales per hour/sq ft, sell-through rate, inventory turn, GMROI, shrinkage rate, and customer retention rate, and can explain what distinct question each KPI answers.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: Directly opens the C12 terminal outcome "define and calculate conversion, ATV, UPT, sales per hour, sell-through, GMROI, shrink and retention" per this project's official course architecture, and establishes the shared vocabulary every subsequent C12 module depends on.

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames KPI fluency as the prerequisite for diagnosing problems and communicating credibly upward.
2. **Conversion Rate: The Percentage That Explains Everything Else** — Formula (transactions ÷ foot traffic × 100), tie to C11's daily/weekly operations.
3. **Average Ticket (ATV) and Units Per Transaction (UPT)** — Formulas and the distinct coaching implications of each.
4. **Sales Per Hour and Sales Per Square Foot** — Formulas and their tie to C11-M06 (scheduling) and C14 (multi-store comparison).
5. **Sell-Through Rate: What Percentage of Stock Actually Moved** — Formula (units sold ÷ beginning inventory × 100), worked numeric example.
6. **Inventory Turn: How Many Times Stock Cycles Through** — Formula (COGS ÷ average inventory), contrast with sell-through, jewelry-specific low-turn context.
7. **GMROI: Profitability Per Dollar of Inventory Invested** — Formula (gross margin $ ÷ average inventory cost), why it's not redundant with sell-through/turn.
8. **Shrinkage and Retention: The Two Numbers That Round Out the Set** — Definitions and explicit ties back to C11-M04/M07 and C3/C11-M03.
9. **Putting the Vocabulary to Work** — Synthesis paragraph previewing Modules 2, 4, and 7 of this course.
10. **Practice Exercise** — Hand-calculating conversion/ATV/UPT from raw data and reconciling against POS output.
11. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Production Notes
- All eight KPI formulas in this module (conversion rate, ATV/AOV, UPT, sales per hour, sales per square foot, sell-through rate, inventory turn, GMROI) are CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across multiple independent, credible retail-metrics sources fetched during this module's research: Shopify's "Retail Metrics: 16 Key Metrics for Your Store," Shopify's "Store Performance: How To Track and Improve," Toolio's "Fundamental Retail Math Formulas and Free Cheat Sheet," DaVinci Retail's "Retail inventory and KPIs," and Faire's "What a sell through rate tells you about inventory." Multiple sources independently state identical or functionally identical formulas for each metric, giving strong confidence in the accuracy of the definitions used in this module.
- This module directly fulfills the "Resource pairing" named in this project's official Course Architecture for C12 (Shopify Retail Metrics guide, Retail Ops Toolkit KPI dashboard and scorecards, Endear benchmarks) — the Shopify guide specifically was used as primary source material for this module.
- The worked sell-through numeric example (40 units, 10 sold, 25% weekly sell-through) is this module's own original illustrative calculation using the confirmed formula, not an externally sourced statistic.
- Shrinkage rate and customer retention rate definitions in this module are general, non-jewelry-specific retail definitions; the module explicitly ties the operational disciplines that produce good numbers on these two KPIs back to this project's own prior, independently-verified C11 and C3 content, rather than introducing new jewelry-specific shrinkage/retention benchmarks not yet verified.
- The practice exercise is an ORIGINAL synthesis designed for this module, not an externally sourced case.
