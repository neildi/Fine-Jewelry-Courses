# C12-M04 Huddle Card: Inventory KPIs: Sell-Through, Turn, GMROI

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Jewelry is supposed to turn slowly. The real danger isn't slow turn — it's aged inventory nobody's tracking against a schedule.

## Our Benchmarks Are Not General Retail's Benchmarks
- Inventory turn: 1-2x/year is normal (boutiques often lower, 0.7-1.5x)
- GMROI: often near 1.0 for mass-market jewelry — that's expected, not alarming
Comparing ourselves to apparel or grocery benchmarks will make us panic over nothing.

## Aging Tolerance Depends on Category
- Bridal/engagement: 12-18 months is NORMAL
- Fashion jewelry: ~6 months is the real window
- Classic fine jewelry: 12-24 months is fine
- Seasonal pieces: must clear before the season ends, or they're basically dead for a year

## The Markdown Ladder (Removes the Guesswork)
1. **10-15% off** at 12-18 months — a quiet nudge
2. **20-30% off** at 18-24 months — get serious
3. **40%+ off** after 24 months — it's dead stock, recover the capital

## Before You Reach for a Discount
- Put a **bounty** on a newly-aging piece — pay a bonus to whoever sells it
- **Bundle** it with a strong seller
- Ask the **vendor** about consignment or return options
