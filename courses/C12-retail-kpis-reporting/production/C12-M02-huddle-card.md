# C12-M02 Huddle Card: Conversion and Traffic: Measuring What You Can't See

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** A conversion rate is only as good as the traffic count underneath it — and jewelry stores should NOT compare themselves to grocery-store benchmarks.

## Five Ways to Count Traffic (Accuracy Ranges)
- Manual clicker: 85-95%
- Basic infrared sensor: 75-85%
- Thermal sensor: ~85%
- Stereo-video / AI video: 95-99%
- Wi-Fi/mobile tracking: only 60-80% (phones hide their signal now)

## Free Baseline: Google Popular Times
No budget for hardware? Google Maps "Popular Times" gives a free relative busyness read — not exact, but useful for spotting patterns.

## Our Conversion Rate Is Not a Grocery Store's Conversion Rate
Jewelry/luxury retail typically runs 8-15% conversion. Grocery runs 20-40%. That's not us underperforming — it's a completely different purchase pattern (browsing, research, multi-visit decisions).

## Count Staff Out
If employees or vendors walk through the same door sensor repeatedly, they inflate our traffic number and make our conversion rate look artificially low. Track staff entries separately if you can.

## Trend Beats Benchmark
Our own rolling multi-week average matters more than any industry number. A steady 12% is fine. A slide from 15% to 12% over three weeks is the real signal.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
What method are we currently using to count foot traffic, and are we accidentally counting ourselves?
