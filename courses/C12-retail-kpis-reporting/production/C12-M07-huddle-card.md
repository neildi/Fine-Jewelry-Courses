# C12-M07 Huddle Card: Diagnosing a Miss

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Before anyone guesses why we missed plan, check traffic and conversion first. The answer tells you which of four completely different conversations to have.

## Sales = Traffic × Conversion × ATV
Check traffic and conversion against last year FIRST. That answer decides everything else.

## Four Branches
1. **Traffic down, conversion fine** → problem is OUTSIDE the store (marketing, location, or window/storefront)
2. **Traffic fine, conversion down** → problem is INSIDE the store (layout, engagement, checkout, peak-hour staffing)
3. **Both down** → fix traffic, but do NOT cut hours — that makes conversion worse and speeds up the decline
4. **Neither down, sales still missed** → it's not about people buying, it's about WHAT they're buying (pricing, mix, add-ons)

## The Trap Everyone Falls Into
When traffic drops, the instinct is to cut hours to save money. That's exactly backwards — fewer staff during a traffic dip tanks conversion too, and now you have TWO problems instead of one.

## Before Blaming the Floor
Rule out: were we out of stock on what people actually wanted? Did a promotion flop? Did returns spike and quietly erase a good week?

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Pull up our numbers from our worst day this week. Traffic down, conversion down, or both? Which branch are we actually in?
