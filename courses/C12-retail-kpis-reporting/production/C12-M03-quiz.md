# C12-M03 Quiz: Average Ticket, UPT and Mix

**Instructions:** Answer all questions.

---

**1. What is the key conceptual distinction between footfall/conversion and ATV/UPT?**
A) They all measure exactly the same thing
B) Footfall and conversion describe how many visitors become buyers, while ATV and UPT describe what happens once someone is already a buyer — they move on different levers
C) ATV and UPT only apply to online stores
D) Footfall is more important than ATV and UPT in every situation

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: A store can grow total sales meaningfully through ATV/UPT improvement even with flat traffic and flat conversion, since these are independent growth levers.

---

**2. In the worked two-associate example, why did Employee 2 generate $4,000 more in revenue than Employee 1 across the same 100 transactions?**
A) Employee 2 closed more sales overall
B) Employee 2 had a higher UPT (6 items vs. 3) and higher average sale value ($90 vs. $50) per transaction, even though both completed the same number of transactions
C) Employee 2 worked more hours than Employee 1
D) The difference was due to random chance with no identifiable cause

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This illustrates that basket-expansion skill is separately coachable from the initial closing skill.

---

**3. According to two independently converging benchmark sources, what is a typical UPT range for jewelry retail?**
A) 5 to 10 units per transaction
B) Roughly 1.2 to 1.6 units per transaction
C) UPT is not a meaningful metric for jewelry stores
D) 20 to 30 units per transaction

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This reflects that most jewelry transactions center on a single primary piece with, at most, one modest add-on — a very different pattern from grocery or apparel retail.

---

**4. What are the four specific techniques this module identifies for growing ATV and UPT?**
A) Discounting, clearance sales, loss-leader pricing, and price matching
B) Point-of-sale add-ons, cross-selling, upselling, and complementary-item merchandising
C) Reducing store hours, cutting staff, and raising prices across the board
D) There are no specific techniques; ATV and UPT cannot be intentionally influenced

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Cross-selling and upselling are associated with meaningfully larger reported effects than simple point-of-sale add-on offers alone, according to this module's flagged source.

---

**5. Why does this module flag the specific cross-selling/upselling percentage-effect claims (25-50% ATV increase, 10-20% average sale increase) as a MUST VERIFY item?**
A) These figures are certainly accurate and require no further verification
B) They come from a business-planning content site rather than a primary trade research source and could not be cross-verified against a second independent source during this module's research
C) These figures apply only to online retail, not in-store jewelry sales
D) Cross-selling and upselling have no measurable effect on ATV

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: These figures should be treated as directional and illustrative rather than as guaranteed, precisely reproducible outcomes for any individual store.

---

**6. What is product mix, and how can it affect ATV independent of any change in selling behavior?**
A) Product mix has no effect on ATV whatsoever
B) Product mix is the proportion of sales coming from different price tiers or categories; a shift toward higher-priced categories (like more bridal, less fashion jewelry) can raise ATV even if every sales conversation was executed identically to before
C) Product mix only affects UPT, never ATV
D) Product mix is a synonym for foot traffic

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This distinction matters directly for correctly diagnosing whether an ATV change reflects real coaching progress or simply a seasonal or inventory-driven mix shift.

---

**7. In the worked scenario, why did the manager conclude the cross-selling training hadn't yet moved actual behavior, despite ATV rising 18 percent?**
A) ATV rising 18% was itself proof the training worked
B) A closer look revealed the real driver was a seasonal shift toward higher-priced bridal transactions (mix change), while UPT — the number less affected by simple mix shifts — stayed essentially flat over the same period
C) The manager had no way to investigate the cause of the ATV increase
D) The training was cancelled before it could have any effect

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: UPT counts items rather than dollars, making it a useful cross-check against ATV changes that might just reflect a mix shift rather than a genuine behavioral change.

---

**8. What is the recommended practical sequence when either ATV or UPT moves meaningfully?**
A) Immediately credit or blame the sales team without further investigation
B) Check product mix first to see if it explains the change on its own; only after ruling out or accounting for mix should the change be attributed to specific technique adoption or lack thereof
C) Ignore any change in ATV or UPT since these numbers are not actionable
D) Always assume the change is due to random statistical noise

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This sequence prevents a manager from crediting a coaching win that didn't actually happen, or missing a real coaching win that a mix shift happened to mask.
