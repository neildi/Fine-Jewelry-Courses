# C12-M01 Huddle Card: The KPI Vocabulary of Jewelry Retail

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** You can't fix a number you can't define. These eight formulas are the shared language for every conversation about how we're doing.

## The Eight KPIs, Quick Reference
1. **Conversion rate** = transactions ÷ foot traffic × 100
2. **ATV** = (gross sales − discounts) ÷ number of orders
3. **UPT** = units sold ÷ number of transactions
4. **Sales per hour** = total sales ÷ total labor hours
5. **Sell-through rate** = units sold ÷ beginning inventory × 100
6. **Inventory turn** = COGS ÷ average inventory
7. **GMROI** = gross margin $ ÷ average inventory cost
8. **Shrinkage rate** and **retention rate** — outputs of our own daily discipline, not abstract numbers

## ATV vs. UPT: Different Problems, Different Fixes
Low ATV, high UPT? Customers are buying lots of smaller pieces — work on trading up. High ATV, low UPT? Fewer bigger sales — work on add-ons.

## Sell-Through vs. Turn: Not the Same Thing
Sell-through tells you about THIS batch, right now. Turn tells you how the WHOLE inventory cycles over time. Jewelry naturally turns slower than apparel — that's normal, not a problem.

## Shrinkage and Retention Aren't Abstract
They're the direct result of our verification protocol, our pipeline visibility, and our client-book follow-up habits. Good numbers here come from good daily habits, not luck.
