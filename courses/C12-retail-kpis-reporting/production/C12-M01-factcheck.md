# C12-M01 Fact-Check: The KPI Vocabulary of Jewelry Retail

## Verification Status: All eight KPI formulas are CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across multiple independent, credible general-retail-metrics sources fetched during this module's research; none are jewelry-industry-specific, and jewelry-specific benchmark values were deliberately not asserted

## Claims Reviewed

**1. Conversion rate formula: transactions ÷ foot traffic (or visits) × 100.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across Shopify's "Store Performance: How To Track and Improve" ("Divide the number of transactions by foot traffic (or visits) for the period, then multiply by 100") and Toolio's "Fundamental Retail Math Formulas" ("Conversion Rate = Number of Transactions ÷ Foot Traffic × 100"). Two independent sources state an identical formula.

**2. ATV/AOV formula: (gross sales − discounts) ÷ number of orders.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced from Shopify's "25 Key Ecommerce Metrics to Track" ("Average order value = (Gross sales − Discounts) / Number of orders"). This is a general retail/ecommerce formula, not jewelry-specific, but the underlying calculation logic applies directly to in-store jewelry transactions as well.

**3. UPT (units per transaction) definition: units sold per transaction, revealing basket-size behavior.**
Status: CONFIRMED, sourced from Shopify's "Store Performance" article, which explicitly defines UPT as "how many items your customer is purchasing per order." This module's specific formula (units sold ÷ number of transactions) is a standard, unambiguous restatement of this definition.

**4. Sales per hour and sales per square foot as staffing- and footprint-normalized productivity metrics.**
Status: CONFIRMED as a general retail metrics category, referenced across Shopify's retail-metrics articles (e.g., "sales per employee" and "sales per square foot" both named as efficiency/productivity metrics). This module's specific formulas (total sales ÷ labor hours; total sales ÷ square footage) are standard, unambiguous applications of this metric category.

**5. Sell-through rate formula: units sold ÷ beginning-of-period units on hand × 100.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across three independent sources: DaVinci Retail's "Retail inventory and KPIs" ("Sell-Thru rate = (Units sold / Beginning inventory) x 100"), Faire's "What a sell through rate tells you about inventory" ("Sell-through rate = (units sold divided by initial inventory) x 100"), and Toolio's cheat sheet ("ST% = Units Sold ÷ BOP Units On Hand × 100"). Three independent sources state an identical formula, giving strong confidence.

**6. Inventory turnover (turn) formula: COGS ÷ average inventory.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across DaVinci Retail ("Inventory Turnover = Cost of goods sold / Average Inventory") and Toolio's cheat sheet ("Turn = COGS ÷ Average Inventory (Cost $ or Units)"). Two independent sources state an identical formula.

**7. GMROI formula: gross margin dollars ÷ average inventory cost.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across Shopify's "Retail Metrics" article ("Gross profit / Average inventory cost") and Toolio's cheat sheet ("GMROI = Gross Margin $ ÷ Average Inventory at Cost"). Two independent sources state an identical formula.

**8. Shrinkage rate and customer retention rate as general retail KPI concepts.**
Status: CONFIRMED as standard, widely recognized retail KPI concepts (shrinkage and retention rate are both explicitly named in Shopify's core-KPI lists). This module deliberately did not assert specific jewelry-industry benchmark values for either metric, since this project's own prior research (used in C11) ties the operational disciplines behind these two numbers to specific, already-verified C11 and C3 content rather than to a new external jewelry-specific benchmark.

**9. Worked sell-through numeric example (40 units, 10 sold, 25% weekly sell-through) and practice exercise.**
Status: ORIGINAL illustrative calculation and exercise created for this module using the confirmed formula above; not an externally sourced statistic.
