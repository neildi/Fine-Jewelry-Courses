# C12-M03 Huddle Card: Average Ticket, UPT and Mix

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Getting someone in the door is one skill. Getting them to buy more once they're already buying is a completely different, separately trainable skill.

## ATV and UPT Describe the Buyer, Not the Visitor
- **ATV** = total sales ÷ number of transactions
- **UPT** = total items sold ÷ number of transactions
Traffic and conversion get more buyers. ATV and UPT get bigger baskets. Different levers, different coaching.

## Our Jewelry Benchmark: UPT Around 1.2-1.6
Most jewelry sales = one primary piece + maybe one add-on. That's normal for this category — don't compare to grocery or apparel.

## Four Ways to Grow the Basket
1. **POS add-ons** — cleaning kit, polishing cloth, travel case at the register
2. **Cross-selling** — a matching, complementary piece
3. **Upselling** — a better/larger version of what they're already considering
4. **Merchandising placement** — put complementary pieces next to each other

## Before You Celebrate a Rising ATV — Check the Mix
Did we actually sell better, or did we just sell more bridal this month (which is naturally pricier)? Check UPT too — it's less fooled by mix shifts than ATV is.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Pick one technique from the list above that we're not doing consistently. Who's going to practice it on their next three transactions today?
