# C12-M08 Huddle Card: Reporting Up: The One-Page Store Review

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** A report that names the driver and asks for something specific gets approved. A report that just states a number and hopes gets deferred.

## Context → Insight → Action
- **Context**: what happened (the actual number)
- **Insight**: why it happened (the real driver, not a guess)
- **Action**: what we're doing next, who owns it, by when

## Four Things Every Leadership Summary Needs
1. The result
2. The comparison (vs. plan, vs. last year)
3. The driver (name it specifically)
4. The decision (one action, one owner, one date)

Never end on a red or green number with nothing attached to it.

## The 30-Second Script
"Overall, we're performing [above/at/below] plan. Sales are [up/down] X%, driven by [specific driver]. The biggest opportunity is X, the biggest risk is Y. Our focus moving forward is [action]."
Practice this until it's automatic.

## A One-Pager Is Not a Dashboard Dump
Only include what answers a question the district manager actually has. More numbers isn't more useful.

## Individual Targets Should Trace Back to the Store Number
Not "the store target divided by headcount." Account for hours, role, and let associates see exactly how their number connects to the store's number.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
If your DM called right now and asked "how's the week going," could you deliver the 30-second script without stumbling?
