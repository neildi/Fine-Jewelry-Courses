# C12-M01 Quiz: The KPI Vocabulary of Jewelry Retail

**Instructions:** Answer all questions.

---

**1. What is the formula for conversion rate?**
A) Total sales divided by total inventory value
B) Number of transactions divided by foot traffic (or visits), multiplied by 100
C) Number of employees divided by store square footage
D) Total sales divided by number of employees

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Conversion rate is the number most directly shaped by staffing, floor coverage, and product knowledge.

---

**2. What distinct question does UPT (units per transaction) answer that ATV does not?**
A) UPT and ATV measure exactly the same thing
B) UPT measures how many items a customer buys per visit, revealing whether growth is coming from bigger individual pieces or more items per visit — a different pattern than ATV alone shows
C) UPT only applies to online sales, never in-store transactions
D) UPT measures employee headcount, not customer purchasing behavior

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: ATV and UPT decompose total sales into two distinct drivers that respond to different coaching interventions.

---

**3. What is the formula for sell-through rate?**
A) Total revenue divided by total profit
B) Units sold divided by beginning-of-period units on hand, multiplied by 100
C) Number of employees divided by number of customers
D) Total inventory value divided by store square footage

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: A collection that arrived with 40 units and sold 10 in its first week has a 25 percent weekly sell-through rate.

---

**4. What is the formula for inventory turnover (turn), and how does it differ conceptually from sell-through rate?**
A) Turn and sell-through are identical formulas measuring the same thing
B) Turn is cost of goods sold divided by average inventory (at cost); it measures the ongoing cycling rate of the whole inventory investment over a longer horizon, while sell-through looks at a specific batch or period
C) Turn only applies to jewelry stores, not other retail categories
D) Turn measures foot traffic, not inventory movement

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: A jewelry store, given its high per-unit value and deliberately slower velocity, will run a lower turn number than a typical apparel retailer, and that's expected rather than a red flag.

---

**5. What is the formula for GMROI, and what question does it answer that sell-through and turn cannot answer alone?**
A) GMROI is the same as conversion rate
B) GMROI is gross margin dollars divided by average inventory cost; it answers how profitably a store's inventory dollars are working, since a category with strong sell-through but thin margins can still produce a disappointing GMROI
C) GMROI measures only foot traffic, not profitability
D) GMROI is irrelevant to inventory decisions

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This is why GMROI belongs on the same dashboard as sell-through and turn rather than being treated as redundant with either.

**8. Why does this module emphasize that a manager should track all eight KPIs together rather than just one or two?**
A) Tracking multiple KPIs is unnecessary busywork with no practical benefit
B) Each KPI answers a different question, and tracking only one or two gives an incomplete picture — for example, a manager tracking only total sales could be fooled by higher foot traffic masking declining floor conversion skill
