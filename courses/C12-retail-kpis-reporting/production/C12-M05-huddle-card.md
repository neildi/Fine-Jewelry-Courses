# C12-M05 Huddle Card: Retention and Client-Book KPIs

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** 9 out of 10 luxury customers never come back to buy again. That's not inevitable — it's a gap we can close with almost no budget.

## The Uncomfortable Number
Luxury retail repeat purchase rate: ~9.9% (vs. 20-26% for apparel). Top clienteling brands hit 40%+. The gap is execution, not customer loyalty.

## Why Automated Texts and Emails Don't Fix This
A personalized email that's obviously automated feels WORSE than a plain generic one. It reveals the machine. Real luxury customers want a real person.

## The 30-Day Window Is Everything
Most brands go silent right after a big sale — exactly when the customer is most excited. A $2-5 handwritten note gets a 4-9% response rate. Email gets 0.12%. Do the math on what one retained $25,000+ customer is worth.

## Every Client Needs an Owner
A client nobody's assigned to is a client nobody contacts. Assign every name in the book to a specific associate.

## Our Weekly Numbers
- Contacts per associate per day: target 5-10
- Response rate: floor of 1 in 10
- Appointments booked and kept

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Pick 5 clients from your book who haven't heard from us in a year. Who's sending the first message today?
