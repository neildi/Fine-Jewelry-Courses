# C6 Bridal & Engagement Mastery

**Track:** Specialist
**Personas:** P4 The Bridal Specialist (primary); P1 The New Associate (secondary)
**10 articles, 8–10 learner hours**
**Research anchor:** Reference R4 (Section 4, "Bridal / Engagement-Specific Selling" free-resource table), Section 2 row 4 (Bridal/Engagement Specialist job postings and KPIs), Section 5 Tier 2; Prompt Kit Part A C6.

## Terminal outcomes

The learner can:
- Run a bridal appointment from research-stage inquiry to purchase
- Guide a couple through shape, setting and metal choices without overwhelming them
- Present natural vs. lab-grown diamonds without bias
- Attach wedding bands and set up future upgrades and referrals (the second sale)
- Manage two-decision-maker dynamics (the couple, and the couple-plus-family)
- Convert online researchers into in-store appointments

**Assessment:** Appointment role-play scored against a rubric, plus a personal bridal conversion log (see M10 capstone).
**KPI link:** Bridal conversion, band attach rate, referral volume.
**Resource pairing:** JCK bridal articles, GIA 4Cs engagement-ring content, NDC Diamond Learning Center "Train the Trainer" guides.

### Note on the module map

The Prompt Kit's Part A C6 list names ten modules. This course keeps that arc but reorganizes the back half so the course lands on a real "second sale" skill and a working capstone:

- Prompt Kit M6 ("Band Attachment and the Upgrade Path") and M9 ("Post-Purchase: Proposal Follow-Up and Referral Asks") are merged into **M09 "The Second Sale"** — the wedding band, the anniversary upgrade, and the referral are all the same skill: converting a first sale into a relationship.
- Prompt Kit M10 ("Bridal Metrics: Tracking Your Own Conversion") becomes the assessment spine of **M10 "Capstone: Running a Full Bridal Appointment"** — a full role-play plus the personal bridal conversion log, instead of a metrics-only article.
- One gap is filled: **M06 "The Try-On"** — the physical skill of presenting a loose diamond, the hand, and reading the couple's reaction at the emotional peak of the appointment. This sits between budget (M05) and couple dynamics (M07) where it belongs in a live appointment.
