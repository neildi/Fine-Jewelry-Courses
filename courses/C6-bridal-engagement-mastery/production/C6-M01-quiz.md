# C6-M01 Quiz: The Modern Engagement Ring Buyer

**Instructions:** Answer all questions. This module's quiz emphasizes correctly applying trend knowledge with appropriate caution about source variation, not memorizing exact percentages.

---

**1. What do independent 2026 industry sources agree on regarding lab-grown diamonds, per this module?**
A) Lab-grown diamonds are declining in market share
B) Lab-grown diamonds crossed the 50% threshold of new US engagement ring sales in 2025-2026, a genuine tipping point
C) Lab-grown diamonds are exclusively for budget buyers under $1,000
D) No credible sources track lab-grown market share

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Multiple independent sources agree lab-grown diamonds crossed the 50% threshold of new engagement ring sales in 2025-2026, up from roughly 15-18% just four to five years earlier — this is described as a genuine tipping point.

**2. Why does this module present two different specific percentages for oval vs. round shape share instead of picking one?**

**3. What is a "hidden halo" setting, per this module?**
A) A halo with no diamonds at all
B) A ring of small accent diamonds placed just beneath the center stone, visible from the side rather than from above
C) A halo made entirely of colored gemstones
D) A discontinued setting style with no current relevance

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: A hidden halo gives side-view sparkle without the bulkier look of a traditional full halo, and is identified across sources as one of the fastest-rising setting details.

**4. What does "toi et moi" mean, and what does it describe?**

C) A traditional pavé-covered band
D) A metal alloy used in platinum settings

Explanation: Toi et moi (French for "you and me") describes a two-stone setting symbolizing two people joining, identified as a breakout design trend of the current decade.
