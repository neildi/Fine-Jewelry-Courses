# Huddle Card: The Try-On

**One headline:** The try-on is the emotional peak of the appointment — clean the stone, fix the light, say one sentence, and then get out of the way of the silence.

**3 key points**
1. Before the hand: clean the stone (in front of them), get the light right, and have the scope/loupe ready.
2. On the hand: one honest observation, then silence. The ring sells itself in your silence faster than in your sentences.
3. Read the reaction, not the specs: the partner watching the wearer's face is the actual decision happening — be invisible and let it land.

**1 script to practice today**

"Watch how the center catches the light when you turn it. … [pause] … You keep looking at it — that's usually the tell."

**1 question to ask the team**

"In our last few try-ons, did we talk through the moment or let the silence work? What's the one sentence we'll say before we go quiet?"
