# M02 Production Record — Booking and Preparing the Appointment

**Course:** C6 Bridal & Engagement Mastery
**Module:** 2
**Persona:** P4 The Bridal Specialist (primary); P1 (secondary)
**KPI link:** Appointment conversion; closing ratio; average sale
**Terminal outcome:** Convert a bridal inquiry into a scheduled appointment, run a brief pre-appointment discovery, and prepare the case, room, and team so the appointment starts on purpose.

## Working title and alternates
1. Booking and Preparing the Appointment (selected — matches Prompt Kit Part A)
2. From "Quick Question" to a Scheduled, Prepared Bridal Appointment
3. The Appointment Is the Product: Booking and Staging the Bridal Visit

## Scenario hook
A proposer's 4:55 pm price call is answered as a transaction instead of booked as an appointment, and the sale walks out the door two weeks later to a store that ran the appointment model.
