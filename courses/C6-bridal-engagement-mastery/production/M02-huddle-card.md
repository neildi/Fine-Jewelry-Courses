# Huddle Card: Booking and Preparing the Appointment

**One headline:** A bridal inquiry is an appointment waiting to be booked — answer the question, then bridge to a specific time, and never let a prepared buyer walk in cold.

**3 key points**
1. Answer the price question honestly first; dodging it feels like a game. Then bridge: "I can show you X next to two alternatives — Thursday at 3 or Saturday at 11?"
2. Know five things before they arrive: who's coming, occasion/timeline, budget range, what they know, what they've seen.
3. Thirty minutes before: stage the selection (not the whole case), set the room, and brief the greeter so the buyer is welcomed by name.

**1 script to practice today**

"Will it be just you, or are you bringing anyone? … Great. I'll have a quiet corner ready and a few rings staged. Thursday at 3 or Saturday at 11 — which works better?"

**1 question to ask the team**

"How many of our bridal inquiries are we answering as transactions instead of booking as appointments — and what's the one bridge we'll all use from today?"
