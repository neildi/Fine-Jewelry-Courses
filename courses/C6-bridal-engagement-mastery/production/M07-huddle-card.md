# Huddle Card: Couple Dynamics

**One headline:** A bridal appointment has two silent conversations — the one out loud and the one between the couple. Read both, and make sure both voices are heard.

**3 key points**
1. Bring the quiet partner in with a direct, low-stakes question; never address everything to the talker.
2. On a disagreement, don't referee — isolate what each person actually values and find the third option that holds both.
3. With a family advisor, address the fear with calm facts, then re-center: "which feels right to you two?"

**1 script to practice today**

"He keeps coming back to the clean classic line; you keep gravitating to the warm metal. What if we do the classic solitaire in rose gold — does that feel like both of you?"

**1 question to ask the team**

"In our last couple appointment, did both people get heard? What's the one question we'll each use to bring the quieter partner in?"
