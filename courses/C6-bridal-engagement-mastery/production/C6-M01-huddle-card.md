# C6-M01 Huddle Card: The Modern Engagement Ring Buyer

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Trend direction is reliable ("oval is rising," "lab-grown crossed 50%"). Exact percentages vary by source — don't repeat a specific number with more confidence than you actually have.

## What Everyone Agrees On
- Lab-grown diamonds crossed 50% of new engagement ring sales in 2025-2026 — a real tipping point.
- Oval is rising fast, whether or not it's technically overtaken round yet.
- Yellow gold is back in a big way after ~20 years of white gold/platinum dominance.

## Know These by Name
- **Hidden halo** — sparkle from the side, not a bulky halo from above.
- **Toi et moi** — two stones side by side, "you and me."
- **Bezel** — metal rim around the stone, clean modern profile, no prongs.
- **East-west** — elongated stones set sideways across the finger.

## The Genuinely Disputed One
Average carat size: is it shrinking (quiet luxury) or growing (lab-grown made big stones cheap)? Credible sources disagree. Don't assume you know which way a specific client will go.

## The Real Skill
Ask what "different" or "modern" or "classic" actually MEANS to this specific couple before naming a trend. Trend vocabulary is a menu, not a script.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Has a client cited a ring statistic you weren't sure about recently? How did you handle it — and how would you handle it now?
