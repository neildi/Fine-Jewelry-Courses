# C6-M04 Fact-Check: Natural, Lab-Grown, and Simulants

## Verification Status: Mixed — GIA-primary-sourced production methods are high-confidence; price/resale statistics show significant cross-source variance, disclosed transparently

## Claims Reviewed

**1. Natural diamond formation (mantle, ~1 billion+ years, volcanic transport).**
Status: Well-established, uncontested geological consensus, consistent with general gemological knowledge.

**2. HPHT and CVD production method descriptions (pressures, temperatures, reactor mechanics).**
Status: HIGH CONFIDENCE — sourced directly and specifically from GIA's own "Summer 2024 Gems & Gemology Laboratory-Grown Diamonds" update, a primary, authoritative, non-commercial source. This is a stronger sourcing basis than most other claims in this course, since it comes directly from the trade's dominant research institute rather than a retailer or secondary publication.

**3. Moissanite as silicon carbide, distinct from diamond and CZ; technical comparison table (hardness, refractive index, composition).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across multiple independent sources (Wikipedia's Diamond Simulant page, Arete Diamond, Jewins, La Joya Jewelry, IGS, Velur). All sources report matching or near-matching hardness (moissanite 9.25, CZ 8-8.5, diamond 10) and refractive index figures, and consistently describe moissanite as heat-resistant/resizable while CZ is not.

**4. Price gap between natural and lab-grown diamonds (60-80% savings range, widening at larger carats).**
Status: DIRECTIONALLY CONSISTENT but WIDE VARIANCE across sources. Cited figures ranged from "40-70% less" to "60-70% savings" to a specific "78.1%" spread at 1ct G/VS2, to "60-80% less" and "70-80% savings" at larger carats from different sources. This module presents the range transparently rather than picking one figure, consistent with the course's established approach to variable market statistics.

**5. Resale value estimates (lab-grown 0-40%, natural 25-60%, depending on source).**
Status: SIGNIFICANT VARIANCE, explicitly disclosed. Sources ranged from "$0-180 (0-10% equity)" for lab-grown up to "10-40%" in another; natural ranged from "25-50%" to "45-55%" to "40-60%." One source (CaratCandor) explicitly states: "No verified industry-wide resale percentages exist as of 2026, so any specific figure should be treated as an estimate rather than a guarantee" — this exact caveat is quoted in the module and should be preserved, as it's a genuinely useful, honest framing for client conversations.

**6. Laser inscription on lab-grown diamond girdles.**
Status: Consistent with well-established, standard industry practice for major labs (GIA and others) identifying lab-grown stones with a permanent inscription — this is standard, uncontested gemological/trade practice.

**7. Worked scenario and practice exercise.**
Status: ORIGINAL illustrative synthesis created for this module, not drawn from any external source.
