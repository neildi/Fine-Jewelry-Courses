# C6-M06 Huddle Card: Ring Sizing, Fit, and Comfort

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** When between two sizes, round UP. A loose ring is a minor fix; a ring that won't clear the knuckle is a failed sale.

## The Measurement Protocol
- End of day (fingers are largest)
- Normal temperature (cold shrinks, heat swells — half a size either way)
- No recent salt or alcohol
- Measure 3-4 times
- Knuckle bigger than base? Size between the two

## The Hard Limit Everyone Agrees On
**Full eternity bands cannot be resized.** No plain metal section to cut = must be remade, not resized. Say this UPFRONT if a client with size uncertainty is considering one.

## Resizing Windows by Construction (from Module 5)
- Plain shank → ~2 sizes either way (more for wide bands)
- Partial pavé/eternity → 0.5-1 size, careful re-securing
- Full eternity → can't resize, must remake
- Tension/titanium/tungsten → can't resize at all

## Surprise Proposals: What Actually Works
Trace an existing ring, soap/clay impression, discreet sleep-measurement, or wear-and-mark. None are perfect — always build in a post-proposal resize plan.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Next time a client mentions size uncertainty on an eternity-style ring, practice raising the resizing limitation proactively, before they ask.
