# C6-M08 Outline: The Proposal Logistics Conversation: Secrecy, Timing, and Same-Day Pickup

## Module Metadata
- Course: C6 - Bridal & Engagement Mastery
- Module: 8 of 14
- Persona: P2
- Working title: The Proposal Logistics Conversation: Secrecy, Timing, and Same-Day Pickup
- Learning objective: Associates can proactively recommend discreet packaging/pickup options based on a client's living situation, explain layaway mechanics and its all-or-nothing usability constraint, set realistic rush-order expectations by product type, and appropriately refer clients to proposal-planning partnerships.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Manage the logistics of a surprise proposal (secrecy, holds, same-day pickup, referral partnerships) professionally."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames proposal logistics as extending beyond the sale itself into secrecy and timing management.
2. **Discreet Packaging and Pickup** — Unbranded packaging, in-store pickup vs. shipping trade-offs, common concealment locations, the dry-run recommendation.
3. **Holds and Layaway: Managing Timing Without Losing the Ring** — Deposit percentages, term lengths, holding conditions, cancellation terms, and the critical "can't propose until paid in full" constraint.
4. **Rush Orders and Same-Day Pickup: What's Actually Realistic** — Same-day only for in-stock/unsized/unengraved; sizing/engraving adds 1-2 weeks; custom rush minimum 2-4 weeks regardless of budget.
5. **Proposal-Planning Partnerships: Extending What the Store Can Offer** — What proposal planners actually do; the case for building local referral relationships.
6. **A Worked Scenario: The Ring Pickup Under Time Pressure** — Illustrative synthesis applying pickup-vs-delivery and dry-run guidance to a cohabiting-partner scenario.
7. **Practice Exercise** — Three-scenario logistics roleplay (cohabiting partner, five-day custom deadline, undated layaway).
8. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Production Notes
- Discreet packaging/pickup and concealment-location guidance is corroborated across Brilliant Earth's official proposal guide and Bellisa Jewellery's rush-order page (which explicitly markets discreet, unbranded packaging as a proposal-specific feature) and JewelTrendPro's body-concealment guide. The dry-run recommendation is sourced specifically from JewelTrendPro.
- Layaway mechanics (deposit %, term length, cancellation terms) show MODERATE VARIANCE across nine retailer-specific sources (Brilliant Earth, WJewel, Since1910, Pampillonia, King of Jewelry, Mullen Jewelers, Estate Diamond Jewelry, and JewelTrendPro's two layaway guides) — deposit ranges from 10% to 30%, terms from 60 days to 12 months, cancellation policies ranging from full refund to 10-20% restocking fee to store-credit-only. This module presents ranges and explicitly recommends checking store-specific written terms, consistent with this genuine variance.
- The "cannot propose with the physical ring until layaway is paid in full" constraint is CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across every layaway source reviewed — this is presented as the single most important logistics point for a client with a proposal-timing goal.
- Rush-order realism (same-day for in-stock/unsized only; 1-2 weeks with sizing/engraving; 2-4 weeks minimum for custom) is corroborated across Quality Diamonds (24-48hr for in-stock), Bellisa Jewellery (2-week rush custom), Moissy Fine Jewelry (4-week rush vs. 6-8 week standard), and Mitubaci's same-day-pickup discussion of ready-made vs. custom-engraved pieces.
- Proposal-planner service descriptions are corroborated across The Proposal Planners (UK), Surprise Proposals (Australia), Serendipity Events by Tina (Dallas), and My Proposal Co. (Australia), all describing a highly consistent service model (consultation, vendor coordination, run-sheet, day-of on-call support).
- The worked scenario and practice exercise are ORIGINAL illustrative syntheses, not externally sourced cases.
