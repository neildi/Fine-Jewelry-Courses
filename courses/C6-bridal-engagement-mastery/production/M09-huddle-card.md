# Huddle Card: The Second Sale

**One headline:** The ring is the first sale — the band, the upgrade, and the referral are the second, and they are won by following up, showing the set, and giving the couple the words.

**3 key points**
1. Begin the band at the try-on: show it with the ring, at multiple price points, and name the future, not the pressure.
2. Follow up where the industry doesn't — proposal check-in, free check-and-clean, and the milestone cadence (C3's territory).
3. Make referrals easy: ask after the proposal, and give them the exact words and a named associate.

**1 script to practice today**

"When you're ready for bands, we'll match the metal and width to sit flush with this ring — and down the road, if you ever want to trade it up, your original purchase value stays in the house."

**1 question to ask the team**

"For every engagement ring we sold this month, did the couple get a proposal follow-up? If not, which second sale did we give away?"
