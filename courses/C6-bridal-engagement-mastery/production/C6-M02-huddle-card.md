# C6-M02 Huddle Card: Diamond Shape vs. Cut

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** "What cut is this?" often means "what shape is this?" — clarify both. Shape is the outline (round, oval, emerald). Cut is the craftsmanship quality. Every shape can be cut well or badly.

## The Number That Changes Budget Conversations
An Excellent-cut 0.90ct diamond often looks LARGER and brighter than a Good-cut 1.10ct diamond — and usually costs less. Cut quality beats carat weight almost every time.

## The Three Things Cut Optimizes
- **Brilliance** — overall brightness
- **Fire** — flashes of color
- **Scintillation** — sparkle when it moves

## One Word for Elongated Shapes
**Bowtie** — a dark shadow across the center of ovals, pears, and marquises. Nearly all have some; the question is whether it's subtle or distracting. With oval trending up (Module 1), you'll see this more.

## The Visual Pitch, Not the Verbal One
Don't just tell a client to go smaller for better cut — SHOW them side by side. The visual comparison is what actually moves the decision.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Next time a client asks "what cut is this," notice whether they mean shape or quality — and practice clarifying both in one sentence.
