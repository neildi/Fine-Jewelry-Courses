# C6-M04 Outline: Natural, Lab-Grown, and Simulants: Advising Without Bias

## Module Metadata
- Course: C6 - Bridal & Engagement Mastery
- Module: 4 of 14
- Persona: P1
- Working title: Natural, Lab-Grown, and Simulants: Advising Without Bias
- Learning objective: Associates can explain what natural diamonds, lab-grown diamonds (HPHT/CVD), moissanite, and CZ each actually are, present accurate price/resale comparisons with appropriate humility about source variance, correctly apply origin-disclosure requirements including the laser-inscription detail, and use discovery questions rather than a predetermined pitch to advise clients.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Advise on natural, lab-grown, and simulant options without steering the client toward a predetermined answer."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames this as one of the most common bridal conversations, with bias (personal or margin-driven) as the primary risk.
2. **What Each Option Actually Is** — Natural formation; lab-grown HPHT/CVD methods (sourced from GIA's own research); moissanite as a genuine mineral, not inferior diamond; CZ definition.
3. **The Technical Comparison** — Composition/hardness/refractive-index/heat-resistance/grading-tradition table across all four options.
4. **Price: The Gap Is Real, Even Though the Exact Number Varies by Source** — Multi-source price-gap synthesis with explicit "direction reliable, magnitude varies" framing, consistent with Module 1's approach.
5. **Resale Value: A Genuinely Disputed Number, But a Consistent Direction** — Explicit multi-source range disclosure (10-60% across sources) with the "no verified industry-wide percentages exist" caveat directly quoted from one source.
6. **Disclosure Is Not Optional** — Direct tie to C5's FTC disclosure module; the laser-inscription detail as a client-reassuring documentation point.
7. **Advising Without Bias: Questions, Not a Pitch** — Discovery-question framework for letting client values/plans guide the recommendation.
8. **A Worked Scenario: The Client Who Asks "Which Would You Get?"** — Illustrative synthesis modeling a balanced, non-imposing response.
9. **Practice Exercise** — Self-audit of tone/enthusiasm consistency across price tiers.
10. **Key Takeaways** — Six-point recap.

## Production Notes
- HPHT and CVD production method descriptions are sourced directly from GIA's own "Summer 2024 Gems & Gemology" laboratory-grown diamonds update (a primary, authoritative source, not a retailer-affiliated one) — this gives Module 4 a stronger primary-source foundation than several prior modules.
- Moissanite/CZ technical comparison data (hardness, refractive index, composition) is CONSISTENT across multiple sources (Wikipedia's diamond simulant page, Arete Diamond, Jewins, La Joya Jewelry, IGS), giving high confidence to the core technical comparison table.
- Price and resale-value statistics show SIGNIFICANT VARIANCE across sources reviewed (price-gap estimates from 60% to 80%; resale estimates for lab-grown from 0-10% to 40%, and for natural from 25% to 60%). This module deliberately presents this variance transparently rather than citing a single confident figure, consistent with the course's established approach to variable market statistics. One source (CaratCandor) is directly quoted stating "no verified industry-wide resale percentages exist as of 2026."
- The worked scenario and practice exercise are ORIGINAL illustrative syntheses, not externally sourced cases.
