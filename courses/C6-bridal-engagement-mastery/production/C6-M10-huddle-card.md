# C6-M10 Huddle Card: Appraisal, Insurance, and Aftercare

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** "Worth" has two different answers. Insurance appraisal (retail replacement value) is almost always HIGHER than resale (fair market value). Both are correct — explain which is which before a client gets confused later.

## The Insurance Talk Track
"Your homeowners policy alone probably only covers about $1,000-$2,500 in jewelry — not nearly enough for this ring. You've got two better options: add a rider to that policy (raises the limit, but reports to your claims history), or get a standalone jewelry policy (broader coverage, doesn't touch your homeowners record). Both run about 0.5-2% of the ring's value per year. Either is a real upgrade from doing nothing."

## The Maintenance Cadence
- Daily: soft cloth wipe
- Weekly/monthly: warm soapy water + soft brush
- EVERY 6 MONTHS: professional inspection + prong check (usually free here!)
- Every 1-2 years: rhodium replating (white gold)
- Every 2-3 years: reappraisal for insurance
- Every 5-10 years: prong retipping (normal, not a failure)

## Why This Matters
Prong wear is invisible until a stone is already at risk. The 6-month check is the cheapest insurance policy in the store — literally.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice raising insurance BEFORE the client asks, at checkout, in one or two sentences — not as an upsell pitch, but as basic protection information.
