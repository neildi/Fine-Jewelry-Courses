# C6-M06 Quiz: Ring Sizing, Fit, and Comfort

**Instructions:** Answer all questions. This module's quiz emphasizes correctly applying measurement protocol and resizing limits by setting type, not memorizing exact percentages.

---

**1. Why does temperature significantly affect ring sizing accuracy?**
A) Temperature has no real effect on finger size
B) Fingers can measure roughly half a size smaller when cold and half a size larger when hot
C) Temperature only affects metal, never the finger itself
D) Cold always makes fingers larger

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Cold causes fingers to shrink slightly and heat causes them to swell, producing a measurable difference of roughly half a size in either direction.

---

**2. When should sizing be measured for the most accurate reading, per this module?**
A) First thing in the morning
B) At the end of the day, when fingers are at their largest, avoiding extreme temperatures and recent salt/alcohol intake
C) Immediately after drinking alcohol
D) Time of day makes no difference

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Fingers are consistently smaller in the morning and largest by evening, so end-of-day measurement under normal conditions is the standard recommendation across nearly every source.

---

**3. If a client's knuckle is noticeably larger than the base of their finger, what does this module recommend?**
A) Always size to the knuckle measurement
B) Measure both the knuckle and the base, and choose a size in between
C) Always size to the base-of-finger measurement only
D) This mismatch has no bearing on sizing decisions

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Choosing a size between the knuckle and base measurements balances the need for the ring to pass over the knuckle while still fitting snugly at the base.

---

**4. If a client is between two sizes after repeated measurement, what does this module recommend?**
A) Always round down to the smaller size
B) Round up to the larger size, since a loose ring is a manageable annoyance while a ring that won't clear the knuckle is a failed fit
C) Flip a coin to decide
D) Always choose whichever size is cheaper

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: The near-universal recommendation is to round up when uncertain, since a slightly loose ring is far less problematic than one that can't pass over the knuckle.

---

**5. What is the safe resizing window generally cited for a plain-shank ring (solitaire or simple band) in gold or platinum?**
A) Unlimited in any direction
B) Roughly two sizes in either direction, with wider bands sometimes allowing more
C) Plain shanks can never be resized at all
D) Only one-quarter size in either direction

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Plain shanks are the most flexible construction for resizing, with roughly a two-size safe window commonly cited, and wider bands (3mm+) sometimes allowing more due to having more metal to work with.

---

**6. What is the resizing limitation for a full eternity band (stones set continuously all the way around), per this module?**
A) Full eternity bands resize exactly like plain shanks, with no real limitation
B) There is generally no plain metal section left to cut, so full eternity bands typically cannot be resized through traditional methods and must be remade instead
C) Full eternity bands can always be resized by any amount with no consequence
D) This construction type has no bearing on resizing feasibility

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: This is the hard limit unanimously described across sources reviewed — without a plain metal section, a traditional resize isn't possible, and the ring generally must be remade rather than resized.

---

**7. Why should resizing limitations be raised proactively at the point of sale for a client considering a full-eternity design who expresses size uncertainty?**
A) It has no real relevance to the purchase decision
B) It respects the client's ability to make an informed decision before they're locked into a design that can't be adjusted later if their size changes
C) Associates should never mention resizing limitations to avoid losing a sale
D) Full eternity bands never actually need resizing

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: Proactively disclosing this limitation lets the client factor it into their decision now, rather than discovering it only years later when they actually need a resize.

---

**8. Which of the following is an established technique for estimating ring size for a surprise proposal, per this module?**
A) Simply guessing based on general population averages
B) Tracing an existing ring's inner circumference, taking a soap or clay impression of a ring, or discreetly measuring while the recipient sleeps
C) There are no reliable techniques; sizing must always be skipped for surprises
D) Only asking the recipient directly, which defeats the surprise

**Correct answer: B**
Explanation: These are established, practical techniques used across multiple sourced guides for estimating size without alerting the recipient, though none are as reliable as an in-person measurement.
