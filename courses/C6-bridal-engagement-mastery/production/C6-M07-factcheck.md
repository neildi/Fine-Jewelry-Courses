# C6-M07 Fact-Check: Custom Design and CAD Workflow

## Verification Status: Core workflow sequence strongly cross-corroborated; total timeline and revision-round figures reflect genuine complexity-driven variance

## Claims Reviewed

**1. Five-stage workflow sequence (consultation/brief, CAD design, revisions, sign-off/production, inspection/delivery).**
Status: STRONGLY CONSISTENT across nine independent sources (Jae's Jewelers, Regal Studio, Tillander, BR Diamonds — two articles, M Diamond, Farsi Jewelers, Loose Grown Diamond, Ballard Gem). All describe the same core sequence, though some sources combine or split individual sub-steps slightly differently (e.g., some separate stone sourcing as its own explicit stage).

**2. Per-stage timing (consultation 30 min-2 hrs; CAD design 3 days-2 weeks; revisions 3-7 days per round; production/fabrication 2-6 weeks; final inspection 2-7 days).**
Status: Broadly consistent across sources with some numeric variance — e.g., Jae's Jewelers cites 1-3 weeks for design/rendering while Farsi Jewelers cites 3-5 days for the CAD wireframe specifically; BR Diamonds cites 1-2 weeks for design/CAD review. This module presents ranges rather than single figures to reflect this genuine variance.

**3. Total timeline (4-12 weeks overall; 4-6 weeks cited most frequently as typical).**
Status: MODERATE VARIANCE across sources reflecting genuine differences tied to design complexity. Regal Studio and BR Diamonds cite 4-6 weeks as typical for custom engagement rings; Tillander cites 4-12 weeks; M Diamond frames it as 4-8 weeks combined with workshop time; Jae's Jewelers cites 6-10 weeks total. This module presents the 4-12 week range with 4-6 weeks flagged as the most frequently cited typical figure, which accurately reflects the spread across sources.

**4. Fabrication/production as the most time-intensive single phase.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT — every source that breaks the timeline into stages identifies casting/setting/fabrication as the longest individual phase, generally 2-6 weeks depending on complexity.

**5. CAD sign-off locks the file's specifications; further changes require rework/added time/cost.**
Status: Sourced specifically from Gurudiam's "How CAD Approval Works Before Casting" guide, which frames this explicitly and in detail (prong height, band profile, metal weight, engraving placement all becoming fixed at sign-off). This is a single-source claim but consistent with general, standard CAD/manufacturing logic described more generally (client approval required before production) across other sources (Loose Grown Diamond, BR Diamonds).

**6. Most custom orders resolve within one to two revision rounds.**
Status: Sourced from Gurudiam's CAD approval guide (single specific source for this exact figure), though broadly consistent with other sources' general framing of "rounds of feedback and refinement" (Regal Studio) without contradicting the one-to-two-round figure.

**7. Eight-point CAD review checklist (stone specs, proportions, prong placement, metal weight, seating depth, shank/gallery clearance, engraving space, then face-up aesthetics).**
Status: Sourced directly from Gurudiam's "CAD File Revisions Explained" guide, presented in the same order recommended in that source. This is a single-source, but detailed and CAD-technical, checklist; cross-consistent in principle with Jewelry CAD Dream's more general guidance to inspect a model from top, side, perspective, and underside views before finalizing.

**8. Worked scenario and practice exercise.**
Status: ORIGINAL illustrative synthesis created for this module, not drawn from any external source.
