# C6-M05 Outline: Metals and Settings: Durability, Style, and the Yellow Gold Resurgence

## Module Metadata
- Course: C6 - Bridal & Engagement Mastery
- Module: 5 of 14
- Persona: P1
- Working title: Metals and Settings: Durability, Style, and the Yellow Gold Resurgence
- Learning objective: Associates can explain karat/hardness trade-offs, platinum's distinct wear mechanism, white gold's replating maintenance requirement, nickel-allergy-safe metal options, and the security/sparkle/maintenance trade-offs of prong, bezel, pavé, channel, and halo settings, applying all of this through a lifestyle-based recommendation framework.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Guide metal and setting choices based on durability, lifestyle, and current style direction."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Connects Module 1's yellow gold and bezel/hidden-halo trend findings to the technical foundation needed to recommend confidently.
2. **Gold Karats: The Durability Trade-Off Most Buyers Don't Expect** — 14k/18k/22k composition and Vickers hardness comparison; higher karat = richer color, softer metal.
3. **Platinum: Different Failure Mode, Not "No" Failure Mode** — Displacement/patina wear mechanism vs. gold's scratch-and-loss mechanism; permanent white color, no replating.
4. **White Gold: The Maintenance Commitment Clients Often Don't Anticipate** — Rhodium plating mechanics; 1-3 year replating cycle; expectation-setting at point of sale.
5. **Rose Gold and the Nickel Allergy Question** — Rose gold's copper-driven hardness/color; nickel allergy prevalence data; safe-metal list for sensitive clients.
6. **Setting Types: What Each One Actually Trades Off** — Prong (brilliance vs. exposed-tip risk, inspection cadence), bezel (durability/lifestyle fit), pavé/channel (band sparkle, individual stone protection), halo (shared-prong vs. bezel-halo construction).
7. **A Practical Recommendation Framework by Lifestyle** — Four lifestyle profiles mapped to metal/setting combinations.
8. **A Worked Scenario: The Nurse Buying Her Own Engagement Ring** — Illustrative synthesis using a naturally-offered lifestyle detail to make a specific recommendation.
9. **Practice Exercise** — Three-profile metal/setting recommendation exercise.
10. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Production Notes
- Karat/hardness figures (14k ~150 HV, 18k ~125 HV, platinum ~130 HV, rose gold ~150 HV) are sourced from Up-Stat's "Engagement Ring Metals" comparison. These are presented as approximate, comparative figures (Vickers hardness varies by specific alloy formulation), not laboratory-precise universal constants.
- Setting-type descriptions and durability/security trade-offs are CONSISTENT across six independent sources (The Diamond Price, A Star Diamonds x2, Sabyl's, Tanishq, GemPundit, Diamonds.pro, Mapplegems, Angara) — this is the most heavily cross-corroborated module in the course to date on setting mechanics specifically.
- Prong inspection cadence recommendations vary slightly by source (6 months vs. 12 months vs. "annually"); this module presents the range (6-12 months) rather than a single number, consistent with the course's handling of variable practical-guidance figures.
- Nickel allergy prevalence statistics (17% women, 3% men) are sourced from Mayo Clinic Minute reporting on NIH data — a credible medical source, though the module treats this as general population data, not a claim about the store's specific client base.
- Safe-metal list for nickel-sensitive clients is corroborated across Mayo Clinic, DermNet, and Gale.care sources.
- The worked scenario and practice exercise are ORIGINAL illustrative syntheses, not externally sourced cases.
