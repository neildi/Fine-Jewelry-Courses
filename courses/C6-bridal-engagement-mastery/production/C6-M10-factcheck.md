# C6-M10 Fact-Check: Appraisal, Insurance, and Aftercare

## Verification Status: Strong cross-source agreement across all major claims; no significant unresolved variance identified

## Claims Reviewed

**1. Retail replacement value (RRV) as the standard/highest insurance valuation type; fair market value as a distinct, typically lower resale figure.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across four independent sources (Jewelers.org — official trade association guidance, La Jolla Gem Appraisal, Gemology Resources, Antwerp Diamond Store), all describing RRV in nearly identical terms as the standard for insurance purposes and explicitly distinguishing it from fair market value as a lower, resale-oriented figure.

**2. Appraisal cost ($50-200) and turnaround (up to one week).**
Status: Sourced from CNBC's consumer guide to insuring an engagement ring, cross-consistent with general appraisal guidance from Jewelers Mutual (which does not provide a specific dollar figure but corroborates the general appraisal-first process).

**3. Standard homeowners/renters insurance jewelry sub-limit ($1,000-2,500) and theft-only coverage limitation.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across five independent sources (Jewelers Mutual, Hippo, InsuranceNewsNet citing BriteCo's October 2025 survey data, CaratYes, CaratYes's insurer-comparison guide) — all cite matching or closely overlapping sub-limit figures.

**4. Scheduled rider mechanics (raises coverage to full appraised value; may exclude mysterious disappearance/accidental damage depending on carrier; reports to CLUE).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across CaratYes (explicitly naming CLUE impact), Insurance.com, SuperJeweler, and Hippo, all describing the same rider mechanics and the carrier-dependent exclusion caveat.

**5. Standalone jewelry policy mechanics (all-risk/open-perils coverage; does not affect homeowners CLUE record; cost roughly 0.5-1.5% of appraised value annually).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across CaratYes (providing the most granular side-by-side rate table: 0.5-1.5% for BriteCo specifically), SuperJeweler, and Jewelers Mutual's general guidance (as a named standalone insurer example), all agreeing on the all-risk/no-CLUE-impact structure.

**6. Maintenance cadence (daily wipe, weekly/monthly soap-and-brush, six-month professional cleaning/inspection, 1-2yr rhodium replating, 5-10yr prong retipping, 2-3yr reappraisal).**
Status: STRONGLY CROSS-CORROBORATED across three independent, detailed sources (Joalys, CaratYes's maintenance-schedule guide, JewelryCarats), all converging on six months as the professional consensus specifically for rings worn daily, with matching or closely overlapping figures for the other cadence items.

**7. The "cheapest repair vs. most expensive failure" framing for prong wear.**
Status: Sourced directly from Joalys ("the cheapest repair in jewelry and the most expensive failure"), echoed in closely similar language and logic by CaratYes's maintenance guide.

**8. Six-month cleaning/inspection frequently offered free of charge by jewelers for in-store purchases.**
Status: Sourced from JewelryCarats' cost comparison table (listing "Free – $30" for basic cleaning and inspection every six months) and consistent with standard, widely-known jewelry-retail practice of complimentary lifetime cleaning/inspection for purchased pieces.
