# C6-M08 Fact-Check: The Proposal Logistics Conversation

## Verification Status: Discretion/concealment guidance and rush-order tiers well corroborated; layaway terms show substantial real-world variance by retailer

## Claims Reviewed

**1. Discreet/unbranded packaging and in-store pickup recommendations.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT — Brilliant Earth's official proposal guide explicitly recommends in-store pickup, an alternative address, or careful delivery-timing when a security camera or home-presence is a risk factor; Bellisa Jewellery explicitly markets discreet, unbranded packaging with no jewelry indication as a rush-order feature specifically for secret proposals.

**2. Concealment location examples (hollowed-out book, coat pocket, sock drawer, mini safe, bag/pocket for the day-of).**
Status: Sourced directly from Brilliant Earth's proposal guide, which provides an extensive, specific list of pre-proposal and day-of hiding locations; JewelTrendPro's body-concealment guide independently corroborates specific on-body methods (waistband, sock, bra clasp, coat lining) with matching practical detail.

**3. The 48-hour dry-run recommendation.**
Status: Sourced specifically from JewelTrendPro's body-concealment guide, which explicitly recommends a dry run at least 48 hours before the proposal, walking, sitting, and bending in the planned outfit. This is a single-source-specific figure but consistent with general common-sense risk management described in less specific terms elsewhere.

**4. Layaway deposit percentages, term lengths, and cancellation terms.**
Status: SUBSTANTIAL VARIANCE across nine sources. Deposits range from 10% (WJewel's low end) to 30% (King of Jewelry, Mullen Jewelers); terms range from 60-90 days (WJewel, JewelTrendPro) to 6-12 months (Since1910, Brilliant Earth); cancellation policies range from full refund (Brilliant Earth, JewelTrendPro's layby guide) to 10-20% restocking fees (King of Jewelry, WJewel) to non-refundable deposits with store-credit-only options (Pampillonia). This module presents these as ranges and explicitly recommends confirming a specific store's written terms rather than presenting a single universal figure, which accurately reflects this variance.

**5. The "cannot propose with the ring until paid in full" constraint.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT AND UNANIMOUS across every layaway source reviewed (Brilliant Earth, WJewel, Since1910, Pampillonia, CaratYes, JewelTrendPro x2, King of Jewelry, Mullen Jewelers, Estate Diamond Jewelry) — this is the single most reliably corroborated claim in this module.

**6. Custom/special-order pieces frequently ineligible for layaway.**
Status: Sourced specifically from Pampillonia's FAQ, which explicitly states custom or special-order pieces are not eligible for their layaway program; consistent with the general logic that layaway holds an existing, sellable piece out of inventory, which doesn't directly apply to a not-yet-manufactured custom piece.

**7. Rush-order realism tiers (same-day for in-stock/unsized/unengraved; 1-2 weeks with sizing/engraving; 2-4 weeks minimum for custom).**
Status: Corroborated across Quality Diamonds (24-48hr for in-stock/loose diamonds specifically), Bellisa Jewellery (2-week rush production for handcrafted pieces), Moissy Fine Jewelry (4-week rush vs. 6-8 week standard turnaround), Mitubaci (same-day for fixed-design in-stock pieces with no engraving; ~2 weeks for rushed custom engraving). These figures vary by exact retailer and product type but consistently support the general tiered pattern presented in this module.

**8. Proposal planner service model (consultation, vendor coordination, run-sheet, day-of on-call support).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across four independent proposal-planning services reviewed (The Proposal Planners UK, Surprise Proposals Australia, Serendipity Events by Tina, My Proposal Co.), all describing a highly similar service structure and sequence.

**9. Worked scenario and practice exercise.**
Status: ORIGINAL illustrative synthesis created for this module, not drawn from any external source.
