# Huddle Card: Shape, Setting, Metal

**One headline:** Never dump the whole case — run the three-decision sequence (shape → setting → metal), one binary at a time, so a couple makes small, easy choices instead of freezing.

**3 key points**
1. Start shape with one binary: brilliant (sparkly) versus step cut (architectural). Then narrow by silhouette and one practical note per shape.
2. Present settings as answers to two real questions — how high does it sit, and how protected is the stone? Connect each to lifestyle (bezel = works with your hands; halo = more presence on a budget).
3. Say the maintenance facts out loud before the decision: platinum patinas, white gold is re-plated, tension is hard to resize.

**1 script to practice today**

"Let's start with one question: do you picture sparkly and classic, or clean and architectural? … Great — within that, the oval gives length to the finger and the cushion reads soft and antique. Which silhouette feels more like her?"

**1 question to ask the team**

"What's the one setting or metal trade-off our clients most often aren't told until after the sale — and how do we move it in front of the decision?"
