# Huddle Card: Natural vs. Lab-Grown

**One headline:** Lab-grown and natural are the same stone with different origins — present both accurately, state the three honest differences, and let the couple decide for their own reasons.

**3 key points**
1. Same material, same properties: lab-grown is a real diamond grown in a lab (HPHT or CVD), not a fake and not a simulant like moissanite.
2. Three honest differences: price (lab-grown typically less), disclosure (always say what it is — natural means natural), and value over time (different markets; never quote a resale number you can't stand behind).
3. Stay neutral. When they ask what you'd choose, hand it back: "which direction feels more like you?"

**1 script to practice today**

"Lab-grown and natural diamonds are the same material and test the same to the eye. The difference is origin — grown in a lab over weeks, or formed in the earth over millions of years. Which is right for you is a values and budget question, not a quality question."

**1 question to ask the team**

"Do we ever say 'real diamond' when we mean 'natural diamond'? What's our one agreed sentence so the FTC terms are right every time?"
