# C6-M08 Huddle Card: Proposal Logistics

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** If the client lives with the person they're proposing to, always recommend in-store pickup over delivery. It's the single easiest way to eliminate discovery risk.

## Discretion Checklist
- Unbranded packaging, no logo, no visible contents
- In-store pickup when home isn't reliably private
- If shipping: schedule for a window when the recipient is out, or ship to a trusted friend
- Recommend a DRY RUN in the actual outfit, 48+ hours ahead

## Layaway in 30 Seconds
- Deposit: usually 15-25% (varies 10-30% by store — know YOUR store's number)
- Term: usually 60-120 days (some up to 12 months)
- THE KEY POINT: client can't propose with the ring until it's paid in full — structure the term to clear well before their date, not right up against it
- Always put it in writing

## Rush Reality Check
- Same-day: in-stock, no sizing, no engraving only
- Sizing/engraving: 1-2 weeks even rushed
- Custom: 2-4 weeks minimum, no matter the budget
Be honest about which bucket a client's request actually falls into.

## Referral Opportunity
Know 1-2 local proposal planners/photographers you trust. It's a genuine value-add and often flows business back to us.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice asking "where do you live, and will they be around this week?" as an early, natural question in a proposal-timing conversation.
