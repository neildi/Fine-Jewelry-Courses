# C6-M11 Huddle Card: Selling Across Demographics and Life Stages

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** There's no single "bridal client" script. Match your questions to what the client actually tells you, not to a default assumption.

## Self-Purchasers
Real, growing segment — not a rare exception. Don't ask "what would he think?" Just help them find something they love.

## Same-Sex Couples
Co-decision is often the norm, not the exception. Ask about symbolism and shared values, not assumed roles. Give both partners equal airtime.

## Second Marriages
- Budget conversations often run higher
- Gently steer away from designs that closely echo a "typical first engagement" look if it seems relevant
- Respond with the SAME warmth and enthusiasm as any first engagement — no hesitation, no unspoken judgment

## Cultural Traditions
Don't guess, don't assume none applies. Ask: "Is there a specific piece or style that's traditional for your family?" Examples to be aware of: South Asian gold sets, Chinese si dian jin, Middle Eastern mahr/shabka, Mediterranean gold coins.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Think of one phrase you say by habit that assumes a specific client dynamic (partner choosing, partner paying, one specific gender). Practice a neutral alternative.
