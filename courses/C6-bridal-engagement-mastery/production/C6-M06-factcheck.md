# C6-M06 Fact-Check: Ring Sizing, Fit, and Comfort

## Verification Status: Measurement protocol strongly cross-corroborated; resizing-limit figures show moderate variance with unanimous agreement on the eternity-band hard limit

## Claims Reviewed

**1. Temperature effect on finger size (~half a size smaller when cold, larger when hot).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across multiple sources (Glamira, Caratyes, Labdiams, David Yurman), all describing the same directional effect with matching approximate magnitude.

**2. End-of-day measurement recommendation; avoiding salt/alcohol before measuring.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across essentially every source reviewed (David Yurman, Glamira, It'sHot, Labdiams) — this is among the most uniformly corroborated practical guidance in the course.

**3. Knuckle-vs-base measurement mismatch guidance (measure both, choose size in between).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across David Yurman, Glamira, and It'sHot, using nearly identical worked examples (e.g., knuckle 8/base 6 → choose 7).

**4. Round-up rule when between two sizes.**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across Glamira and RingChart UK specifically stating this rule explicitly; consistent with the general logic (a loose ring is more manageable than one that won't pass the knuckle) presented across other sources.

**5. Plain-shank resizing safe window (~2 sizes, more for wide bands).**
Status: MODERATE VARIANCE across sources. JewelTrendPro cites an "industry standard" of ±2 sizes; CaratYes cites ±2 for gold/platinum; LILY DIA cites 1-2 sizes "comfortably" but up to 3-4 for a moderate-thickness band and 4+ for wide bands; Diavlia and Dorah Jewelry describe similar 2-3 size ranges. This module presents ±2 as the most commonly cited baseline while noting wider bands can extend this range, consistent with the range disclosed across sources.

**6. Partial pavé/partial eternity resizing limit (0.5-1 size).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across CaratYes, Omac Canada, LILY DIA, and The Carat Cut, all citing figures in the 0.5-1 size range for partial pavé/eternity constructions.

**7. Full eternity bands cannot be traditionally resized; must be remade.**
Status: UNANIMOUS ACROSS ALL SEVEN SOURCES REVIEWED (JewelTrendPro, Diavlia, CaratYes, Omac Canada, LILY DIA, The Carat Cut, RingChart UK) — this is the single most confidently corroborated specific claim in this module, with no source presenting any exception or contrary view.

**8. Tension settings, titanium, and tungsten cannot be resized through traditional methods.**
Status: Sourced from LILY DIA and RingChart UK, consistent with well-established, standard jewelry-trade knowledge about these specific materials/constructions.

**9. Surprise-proposal sizing estimation techniques (tracing, soap/clay impression, sleep-measurement, wear-and-mark).**
Status: CONFIRMED CONSISTENT between Blue Nile's official published guide and TDFJ's guide, which describe matching techniques with overlapping specific details.
