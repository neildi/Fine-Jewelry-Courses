# C6-M11 Outline: Selling Bridal Across Demographics and Life Stages

## Module Metadata
- Course: C6 - Bridal & Engagement Mastery
- Module: 11 of 14
- Persona: P2
- Working title: Selling Bridal Across Demographics and Life Stages
- Learning objective: Associates can adapt consultation language and approach for self-purchasing clients, same-sex couples, second-marriage clients, and clients with specific cultural bridal traditions, avoiding default assumptions rooted in a single traditional bridal script.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Adapt a bridal sales approach across client demographics, life stages, and sales channels... while holding a consistent ethical standard." (demographic/life-stage portion; channel portion covered in Modules 13-14)

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames "the bridal client" as a range of distinct profiles, not a single script.
2. **Self-Purchasers: A Growing, Not Niche, Segment** — De Beers/Rapaport self-purchase growth data, BriteCo/PriceScope general jewelry self-purchase trend; practical language adjustments.
3. **Same-Sex Couples: Co-Decision Is Often the Default, Not the Exception** — Co-shopping/co-decision as common; open-question approach; visible-welcome signals.
4. **Second Marriages: A Different Emotional Calculus, Not a Different Product** — Higher typical budget, avoiding visual similarity to a previous ring, joint decision-making, retiring the previous ring (etiquette awareness only).
5. **Multicultural Bridal Traditions: Know the Landscape, Don't Assume the Specifics** — South Asian streedhan, Chinese si dian jin, Middle Eastern mahr/shabka, Mediterranean coin/stefana traditions; ask-don't-assume principle.
6. **A Worked Scenario: The Self-Purchasing Client** — Illustrative synthesis of a straightforward, non-surprised response to a self-purchasing client.
7. **Practice Exercise** — Four-scenario consultation-language adaptation roleplay.
8. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Ties to Other Modules
- Directly begins fulfilling the C6 terminal outcome on adapting bridal sales approach across demographics and life stages (channel adaptation continues in Modules 13-14).

## Production Notes
- Self-purchase growth statistics (7% to 14% of engagement rings financed solely by the bride, 2013-2017; $4,400 vs. $3,300 average spend) are sourced from Rapaport's reporting on De Beers' Diamond Insight Report — a credible, named, dated primary industry source. This data is from 2017 and is presented as a historical trend data point establishing directional growth, not necessarily reflecting exact current percentages.
- The 80% figure for general fine-jewelry self-purchase preference is sourced from PriceScope's reporting on a 2025 BriteCo-backed report — a specialized jewelry insurer with direct industry data access, treated as a credible single-source figure for the general (not bridal-specific) self-purchase trend.
- Content regarding same-sex couple engagement ring dynamics draws on Greenwich St. Jewelers' LGBTQ+ guide and Autostraddle's first-person guidance; several additional web results (JewelTrendPro's LGBT-focused articles) contained highly specific statistics and named-quote attributions (e.g., a claimed "Williams Institute" study figure, a named "GIA-Certified Jewelry Historian" quote) that could not be independently verified against a primary source and were EXCLUDED from this module's content as a precaution — this module presents only the qualitative, better-corroborated guidance (co-decision-making as common, open-question consultation approach) rather than unverifiable specific statistics.
- Second-marriage etiquette guidance (higher typical budget, avoiding visual similarity to a previous ring, joint decision-making, retiring previous rings) is CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across Estate Diamond Jewelry, The Knot, Alberthern, and Legacy Diamonds and Gemstones, all independently converging on the same core guidance points.
- Multicultural bridal jewelry tradition descriptions (South Asian streedhan/si dian jin/mahr-shabka/stefana) are corroborated across GIA's own 4Cs blog, Alromaizan, Menē, and Arthesdam's Singapore-specific guide — all four independently describe matching or closely overlapping tradition names and descriptions.
- The worked scenario and practice exercise are ORIGINAL illustrative syntheses, not externally sourced cases.
