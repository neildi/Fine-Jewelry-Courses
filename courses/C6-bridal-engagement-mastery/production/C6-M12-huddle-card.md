# C6-M12 Huddle Card: Ethical Standards in High-Ticket Bridal Selling

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** "12 months no interest" and "0% APR" are NOT always the same thing. Know which one you're actually offering, and say so plainly.

## FTC Disclosure — The Rule in One Line
Whenever you say "diamond" about a lab-grown stone, say "laboratory-grown/created" RIGHT BEFORE it, just as clearly and just as loud. Every time. Every carat weight, including melee. Never say "real," "natural," or "genuine" about a lab-grown stone.

## Deferred Interest — The Danger in One Line
Interest accrues silently from day one. Miss the deadline by $1 or one day, and the client owes ALL of it, retroactively, usually at 20-30%+ APR. Say this out loud to every client considering a "no interest" promo.

## The Real Numbers
About 1 in 5 deferred-interest balances get hit with retroactive charges. Many of those people end up paying over 150% of the original balance. This is common, not rare.

## High-Pressure Tactics to Never Use
- False urgency ("this piece won't be here tomorrow" when that's not true)
- Discouraging a second opinion or more time to think
- Steering toward financing that benefits the store more than the client
- Ignoring a client who's clearly said they're not ready

## Why This Is Also Just Good Business
An informed client who doesn't buy today comes back. A pressured client who buys today tells their friends not to.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice saying the deferred-interest disclosure out loud, in one clear sentence, before your next financing conversation.
