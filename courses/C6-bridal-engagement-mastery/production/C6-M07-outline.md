# C6-M07 Outline: The Custom Design and CAD Workflow for Bridal

## Module Metadata
- Course: C6 - Bridal & Engagement Mastery
- Module: 7 of 14
- Persona: P2
- Working title: The Custom Design and CAD Workflow for Bridal
- Learning objective: Associates can walk a client through the five-stage custom bridal design workflow, correctly review a CAD file in technical-then-aesthetic order before presenting it for approval, explain that sign-off locks the file's specifications, and set honest timeline expectations (4-12 weeks, 4-6 typical) especially against a fixed target date.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Run a custom design/CAD consultation from discovery through production tracking for a bridal client."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames custom bridal orders as a multi-stage, weeks-long process requiring active expectation management, not a single closing conversation.
2. **The Five-Stage Workflow** — Consultation/brief, CAD design, revision rounds, sign-off/production, final inspection/delivery, with typical timing per stage.
3. **The Sign-Off Detail Worth Getting Right** — CAD approval locks specifications; the eight-point technical review order before aesthetic review.
4. **Setting Timeline Expectations Honestly** — 4-12 week range, 4-6 typical; fabrication as the most time-intensive phase; ties to Module 6 sizing and upcoming Module 8 proposal logistics.
5. **A Worked Scenario: Managing the Anxious Custom-Design Client** — Illustrative synthesis of structured, specific reassurance against a fixed deadline.
6. **Practice Exercise** — CAD-review-checklist roleplay plus explaining the sign-off lock in plain language.
7. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Production Notes
- The five-stage workflow (consultation, CAD design, revisions, sign-off/production, inspection/delivery) and its approximate per-stage timing are STRONGLY CONSISTENT across nine independent sources (Jae's Jewelers, Regal Studio, Tillander, BR Diamonds x2, M Diamond, Farsi Jewelers, Loose Grown Diamond, Ballard Gem), all describing the same core sequence with matching stage names and comparable timing ranges.
- The total timeline range (4-12 weeks, with 4-6 weeks cited most frequently as typical) reflects genuine variance across sources depending on design complexity — this module presents it as a range rather than a single figure, consistent with how the sourced guides themselves frame it.
- The "one to two revision rounds is typical for most orders" figure and the CAD-sign-off-locks-the-file concept are both sourced specifically from Gurudiam's CAD-approval and CAD-revision guides, which are the most detailed and CAD-technical sources reviewed for this module.
- The eight-point CAD review checklist (stone specs, proportions, prong placement, metal weight, seating depth, shank/gallery clearance, engraving space, then face-up aesthetics) is sourced directly from Gurudiam's CAD file revision checklist, presented in the same technical-then-aesthetic order recommended in the source.
- The worked scenario and practice exercise are ORIGINAL illustrative syntheses, not externally sourced cases.
