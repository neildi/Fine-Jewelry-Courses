# M04 Quiz — Natural vs. Lab-Grown

10 questions testing the terminal outcome: presenting both without bias. Answer key at the end.

---

**1. What do lab-grown and natural diamonds share?**
a) Nothing meaningful.
b) The same material and the same optical and physical properties; they differ in origin.
c) The same price.
d) The same resale value.

**2. What are the two main lab-growth methods?**
a) Verneuil and flux.
b) HPHT and CVD.
c) HTHP and VDC.
d) Fusion and diffusion.

**3. A couple asks "will a lab-grown diamond hold its value?" What is the honest answer?**
a) "It holds 80% of what you paid."
b) "Lab-grown holds no value at all."
c) "The two sit in different markets; I won't quote a resale number I can't stand behind — for either."
d) "Natural diamonds always appreciate."

**4. A buyer asks "which do you recommend?" What is the disciplined response?**
a) Tell them what you personally bought.
b) Push whichever has the higher margin.
c) Hand the choice back: "which of these two directions feels more like you?"
d) Recommend natural, always.

**5. Why does the article warn against disparaging lab-grown diamonds?**
a) Lab-grown is objectively superior.
b) Disparaging a real product the couple is considering costs you trust — and therefore the sale and the referral.
c) It is illegal to mention lab-grown.
d) Lab-grown buyers never buy natural.

**6. What is the floor discipline on disclosure?**
a) Disclose only when asked.
b) Always say what the stone is, the same way, every time — natural means natural, lab-grown means lab-grown.
c) Disclosure is optional for lab-grown.
d) Only GIA reports matter.

**7. An advisor insists "lab-grown is fake." What is your move?**
a) Agree to keep the peace.
b) Argue with the advisor.
c) Give both parties the same accurate facts, then hand the choice back to the buyer.
d) End the appointment.

**8. What is the difference between lab-grown diamond and moissanite?**
a) Nothing — they are the same.
b) Moissanite is a different material entirely; a lab-grown diamond is a real diamond.
c) Moissanite is just a brand of lab-grown diamond.
d) Lab-grown is a simulant of moissanite.

**9. Why is the natural-vs-lab-grown moment the highest-stakes trust point in the appointment?**
a) It's the most expensive product.
b) It's where the buyer has researched most, heard conflicting claims, and is most sensitive to being steered.
c) It's required by the FTC.
d) It decides the metal choice.

**10. Which framing is accurate and unbiased?**
a) "Lab-grown is a fake diamond."
b) "Natural is the only real choice."
c) "Same material, different origin — a values and budget question, not a quality question."
d) "Only a GIA report makes a diamond real."

---

## Answer key

1. **b** — Same carbon material, same properties; origin is the difference.
2. **b** — High-pressure, high-temperature and chemical vapor deposition.
3. **c** — Directional honesty beats an invented number, for either stone.
4. **c** — Hand the choice back; your neutrality is the product.
5. **b** — Disparaging a real, disclosed product costs trust, which costs the sale and referral.
6. **b** — Say what the stone is, consistently; the FTC governs the representations.
7. **c** — Accurate facts for both parties, decision stays with the buyer.
8. **b** — Moissanite is a different material; lab-grown diamond is a real diamond.
9. **b** — Most researched, most conflicting claims, most sensitivity to steering.
10. **c** — Same material, different origin; values and budget, not quality.
