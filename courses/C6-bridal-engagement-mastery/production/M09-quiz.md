# M09 Quiz — The Second Sale

10 questions testing the terminal outcome: turning the ring sale into band, upgrade, and referral. Answer key at the end.

---

**1. What are the three forms of the second sale?**
a) The band, the upgrade, and the referral.
b) The discount, the warranty, and the receipt.
c) The appraisal, the cleaning, and the box.
d) The email, the call, and the text.

**2. Why is the second sale really "the first sale, tended"?**
a) It's a separate product line.
b) The band, upgrade, and referral all come from the same relationship you built at the first sale.
c) It's a marketing term.
d) It has no connection to the first sale.

**3. When should you begin the band conversation?**
a) The week before the wedding.
b) At the engagement-ring try-on, when they ask to see it with a band.
c) After the honeymoon.
d) Never — bands sell themselves.

**4. What do the two JCK-reported facts together tell you?**
a) Bridal is shrinking.
b) Bands are drifting to other stores, and most stores aren't following up to stop it.
c) Follow-up doesn't matter.
d) Bands aren't profitable.

**5. What is the upgrade sentence, and why isn't it a pitch?**
a) "Buy the bigger stone now."
b) It plants the trade-up path ("your original purchase value stays in the house") as a fact, once, without selling today.
c) A discount offer.
d) A warranty upsell.

**6. Why should you log the upgrade conversation?**
a) To track commissions.
b) So the milestone call can happen when the couple is actually ready to trade up.
c) Logs aren't needed.
d) Only for tax reasons.

**7. What are the two halves of making referrals easy?**
a) Timing (after the proposal) and a script (named associate + exact words).
b) Price and discount.
c) Ads and signs.
d) Email and phone.

**8. Why does a well-handled recovery refer harder than an easy sale?**
a) It doesn't.
b) A couple treated well through a rough moment tells the story with more energy.
c) Recoveries are illegal.
d) Easy sales never refer.

**9. What is the free-check-and-clean offer really buying you?**
a) Nothing.
b) A reason to come back that isn't a sale — and a second chance for the band conversation.
c) A bigger receipt.
d) Time.

**10. What is the proposal check-in, and why is it not a sales message?**
a) A discount reminder.
b) "How did it go? We'd love to hear the story" — the relationship's next line, and the natural referral moment.
c) A warranty call.
d) A survey.

---

## Answer key

1. **a** — Band, upgrade, referral.
2. **b** — All three flow from the relationship built at the first sale.
3. **b** — At the try-on, when the couple asks to see it with a band.
4. **b** — Bands drift and follow-up is rare: the second sale is being lost industry-wide.
5. **b** — A fact stated once, no sell.
6. **b** — The log makes the milestone call when they're ready.
7. **a** — After the proposal, with exact words and a named associate.
8. **b** — A cared-for recovery tells a stronger story.
9. **b** — A non-sales reason to return and reopen the band conversation.
10. **b** — The relationship's next line, which makes the referral natural.
