# C6-M09 Outline: Wedding Bands and Stacking: Pairing, Matching Metals, and the Second Sale

## Module Metadata
- Course: C6 - Bridal & Engagement Mastery
- Module: 9 of 14
- Persona: P1
- Working title: Wedding Bands and Stacking: Pairing, Matching Metals, and the Second Sale
- Learning objective: Associates can recommend contoured vs. straight wedding bands based on the engagement ring's setting style, explain metal-matching and spacer-band logic for wear/corrosion reasons, apply core stacking principles, and reverse-engineer a wedding-band shopping timeline from the wedding date (not the proposal date), while proactively planting the seed for this second sale at the original engagement-ring purchase.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Sell wedding bands and stacking pieces as a distinct 'second sale,' not an afterthought to the engagement ring."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames wedding bands as a distinct, under-captured second sale tied to the wedding-band attach-rate KPI.
2. **Pairing a Band with the Engagement Ring: Contour, Not Just Color** — Contoured/curved bands vs. straight bands for halo/three-stone/vintage settings; metal-matching for wear and galvanic-corrosion reasons; spacer bands for intentional metal mixing.
3. **Stacking: What Actually Works** — Width variation, two-metal-tone limit, spacer bands, anchoring the stack around the engagement ring.
4. **The Wedding-Band Timing Window: A Different Deadline Than the Engagement Ring** — 3-4 months (in-stock), ~6 months (customization/engraving), 8 months-1 year (fully custom/contour-matched); ties to Module 7's custom-timeline logic.
5. **A Worked Scenario: The Six-Month Check-In** — Illustrative synthesis connecting Module 5's setting knowledge, this module's timing guidance, and a specific recommendation.
6. **Practice Exercise** — Contour-vs-straight and timeline-recommendation roleplay across three engagement ring styles.
7. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Production Notes
- Contour/curved band vs. straight band guidance for halo, three-stone, and vintage-style rings is CONFIRMED CONSISTENT across Queensmith, Stienhardt, and multiple JewelTrendPro guides, all describing the same contour-matching logic to avoid a visible gap.
- Metal-matching rationale (wear from hardness differences; possible galvanic corrosion risk) is corroborated across JewelTrendPro's timing guide and Queensmith's stacking guide (metal density/wear-down point) and the JewelTrendPro stacking guide's thermal-expansion/buffer-band recommendation.
- Stacking principles (width variation, two-metal-tone limit, spacer bands, anchor-then-build approach) are STRONGLY CROSS-CORROBORATED across Queensmith, Stienhardt, and two separate JewelTrendPro stacking guides, all independently recommending highly similar practices with matching specific numeric guidance (e.g., two-metal-tone limit, spacer band use).
- Wedding-band timing windows show MODERATE VARIANCE across eleven independent sources (SoFi, Borsheims, JewelTrendPro x4, Scott's Custom Jewelers, Lucellia, WedSites, PriceScope, Alibaba Wedding) — in-stock figures range from a few weeks to 3-4 months; custom/contour-matched figures range from 8 months to 20 weeks (~5 months) to a full year depending on source and specific customization type. This module presents the widely-agreed general bands (3-4 months / ~6 months / 8 months-1 year) as the most representative synthesis of this range.
- The worked scenario and practice exercise are ORIGINAL illustrative syntheses, not externally sourced cases.
