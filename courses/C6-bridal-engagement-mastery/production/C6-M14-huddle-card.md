# C6-M14 Huddle Card: Luxury Hotel and Private Appointment Selling (Capstone)

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** The suite, the privacy, the champagne — none of it replaces the consultation. It supports it. Every skill from this entire course still applies.

## The Three Formats
- Hotel-suite trunk shows (jeweler rents the suite, invites core clients)
- Formal hotel-brand partnerships (hotel books a specialist for guest in-suite viewings)
- By-appointment private showrooms/ateliers (often NDA-protected)

## Discretion IS the Product Here
- Unmarked transport/packaging
- NDAs when the client's profile calls for it
- Need-to-know information sharing — full stop
- Coordinate quietly with hotel front desk/concierge in advance

## Before Pieces Leave the Store
- Confirm insurance actually covers off-site transit/display (don't assume)
- Keep a chain-of-custody log — which pieces, when, with whom
- Pick venues that are genuinely discreet, not just expensive

## The Capstone Test
Can you run a FULL consultation in this setting using:
- Trend context (M1) + honest stone advising (M4) + setting/durability fit (M5) + accurate sizing (M6) + realistic custom timeline (M7) + clear insurance/appraisal talk (M10) + zero-exception ethics (M12)?
If yes, you've internalized this whole course.
