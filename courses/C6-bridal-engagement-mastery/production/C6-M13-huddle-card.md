# C6-M13 Huddle Card: Selling Bridal in Cruise and Port Retail

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** NEVER use the ship's departure clock as a sales tool. Ever. If a client needs more time than they have, give them a written quote and let them decide later.

## The Reality Check
- Commission runs through the shopping consultant, the store, AND often the cruise line itself. Own that, don't hide it.
- "Duty-free" ≠ "discounted." It just means no import duty was charged. The base price can still be inflated.

## Customs Facts to Get Right
- Standard U.S. exemption: $800/person
- U.S. Virgin Islands specifically: $1,600/person (double!)
- Tell clients to keep receipts and declare accurately — bad info here becomes THEIR legal problem later.

## What NOT to Do (documented scam patterns)
- Pressuring a rushed decision before the ship leaves
- Comparing to an inflated "was" price to fake a discount
- Burying restrictive terms in receipt fine print
- Discouraging photos, comparison shopping, or independent appraisal

## The Differentiator
This channel has a real trust problem (ask GIA, AGS, The Points Guy). The associate who is radically transparent — accurate pricing basis, honest customs info, welcomes verification — is the one clients recommend to their cruise-forum friends.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice offering a "hold the price, take your time" option out loud, before a client even asks for one.
