# M10 Quiz — Capstone

10 questions testing the capstone: the full appointment, the role-play, and the log. Answer key at the end.

---

**1. What is the capstone's job in one sentence?**
a) A new skill not covered earlier.
b) The proof that the nine modules run as one continuous performance with no visible seams.
c) A written exam.
d) A product-knowledge test.

**2. What is the C6 assessment made of?**
a) A quiz only.
b) A role-play and a bridal conversion log.
c) A sales target.
d) A written thesis.

**3. What are the three KPIs the course moves?**
a) Traffic, ads, and payroll.
b) Bridal conversion, band attach rate, and referral volume.
c) Carat, color, and clarity.
d) Units, margin, and hours.

**4. Which stage of the walkthrough comes first?**
a) The try-on.
b) Book and prepare (M02) — the appointment starts before the couple arrives.
c) The second sale.
d) The budget conversation.

**5. What are the three things that fail the role-play?**
a) Talking through the try-on, taking a side in the disagreement, and promising an untrue timeline/resizing.
b) Smiling, listening, and following up.
c) Discounting, greeting, and cleaning.
d) Closing, qualifying, and presenting.

**6. What should you do when the role-play observer goes quiet?**
a) Fill the silence with specs.
b) Let the silence work — it's the same discipline as the try-on.
c) End the role-play.
d) Ask them to leave.

**7. What are the five fields of the conversion log?**
a) Name, date, price, discount, receipt.
b) Date/client, outcome, the moment, what worked, what to change.
c) Carat, color, clarity, cut, certificate.
d) Ads, traffic, units, margin, hours.

**8. Why are honest "lost" entries worth more than a clean column of "sold"?**
a) They aren't.
b) The losses are the only part that teaches — the "what to change" column is where the score improves.
c) Lost entries are easier.
d) It looks better to management.

**9. What are the three resources the spec pairs with this course, and what does each supply?**
a) JCK (market/conversation), GIA (stones), NDC (diamond story and ethics).
b) Google, YouTube, TikTok.
c) Price, margin, markup.
d) GIA, AGS, IGI only.

**10. What does "hide the seams" mean in the capstone?**
a) Hide the price.
b) Hold the sequence's spirit so the transitions disappear — the skills, not the script.
c) Skip the budget ask.
d) Shorten the appointment.

---

## Answer key

1. **b** — The capstone proves the nine modules are one performance.
2. **b** — Role-play + bridal conversion log.
3. **b** — Conversion, band attach, referral volume.
4. **b** — Book and prepare comes first.
5. **a** — The three failures are talking through the try-on, refereeing the couple, and untrue promises.
6. **b** — Same silence discipline as the try-on.
7. **b** — Date/client, outcome, the moment, what worked, what to change.
8. **b** — Losses teach; the change column improves the score.
9. **a** — JCK, GIA, NDC.
10. **b** — The spirit of the sequence, not the script — transitions become invisible.
