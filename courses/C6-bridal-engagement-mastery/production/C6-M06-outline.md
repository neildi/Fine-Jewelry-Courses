# C6-M06 Outline: Ring Sizing, Fit, and Comfort: Getting It Right the First Time

## Module Metadata
- Course: C6 - Bridal & Engagement Mastery
- Module: 6 of 14
- Persona: P1
- Working title: Ring Sizing, Fit, and Comfort: Getting It Right the First Time
- Learning objective: Associates can apply accurate measurement protocol accounting for temperature/time-of-day variation, explain resizing limits by setting construction (directly extending Module 5), and confidently guide a surprise-proposal client through sizing-estimation techniques and a post-proposal resize plan.
- Terminal outcome tie-in: "Correctly size a ring, explain resizing limits by metal and setting type, and handle surprise-proposal sizing constraints."

## Section Map

1. **Why This Module Matters** — Frames sizing as a logistics challenge distinct from product selection, especially acute for surprise proposals.
2. **Measuring Accurately: What Actually Affects the Number** — Temperature, time-of-day, salt/alcohol, knuckle-vs-base mismatch; the "round up when between sizes" rule.
3. **Resizing Limits: Tied Directly to Setting Type** — Plain shank (±2, more for wide bands) vs. partial pavé/eternity (0.5-1 size) vs. full eternity (cannot resize, must remake) vs. tension/titanium/tungsten (cannot resize at all); direct extension of Module 5.
4. **A Worked Comparison: Why This Conversation Belongs at the Point of Sale** — Proactive disclosure framing for eternity-style designs when a client expresses size uncertainty.
5. **Handling Surprise-Proposal Sizing** — Four established estimation techniques (tracing, soap/clay impression, sleep-measurement, wearing-and-marking).
6. **A Worked Scenario: The Nervous Proposer** — Illustrative synthesis combining estimation technique guidance with the plain-shank resizing flexibility from section 3.
7. **Practice Exercise** — Roleplay of the surprise-proposal sizing conversation across different ring constructions.
8. **Key Takeaways** — Five-point recap.

## Production Notes
- Measurement-accuracy guidance (temperature effects, time-of-day, salt/alcohol, knuckle-vs-base, round-up rule) is STRONGLY CROSS-CORROBORATED across at least eight independent sources (David Yurman, Glamira, It'sHot, Labdiams, RingChart UK, and others) with highly consistent specific details (half-size swing from temperature, end-of-day recommendation, 3-4 repeat measurements) — this is among the most reliably corroborated practical-guidance content in the course.
- Resizing-limit figures show MODERATE VARIANCE across sources but consistent qualitative categories: plain-shank safe windows range from ±2 sizes (most common figure) to ±3-4 for wide bands depending on source; partial pavé/eternity figures consistently land at 0.5-1 size; full eternity bands are UNANIMOUSLY described across all seven sources reviewed as generally non-resizable, requiring remaking instead. This unanimous agreement on the eternity-band hard limit is the most confidently-sourced specific claim in this module.
- Surprise-proposal sizing techniques (tracing, soap/clay impression, sleep-measurement, wear-and-mark) are corroborated across Blue Nile's official guide and TDFJ's guide, both describing matching techniques.
- The worked scenario and practice exercise are ORIGINAL illustrative syntheses, not externally sourced cases.
