# Huddle Card: Sizing, Timelines, Custom and Resizing Realities

**One headline:** A ring that must fit a specific date needs the size right the first time — size it properly, state the timeline honestly, and say the resizing limits before the sale, not after.

**3 key points**
1. Size the correct finger at a stable time, account for band width, and — for surprises — borrow-and-size with a post-proposal resize note.
2. Quote honest timelines with margin: custom takes weeks, and "tell me the date — I'll tell you honestly what gets there" beats the optimistic answer.
3. Say it up front on stone-covered bands and tension settings: "this can't be easily resized later, so let's get the size right now."

**1 script to practice today**

"Tell me the date you need it by, and I'll tell you honestly what gets there — in-stock sized by Thursday, or the custom build with a real margin."

**1 question to ask the team**

"What's the most common sizing or timeline surprise we've had to explain after the sale — and what's the one sentence that would have prevented it?"
