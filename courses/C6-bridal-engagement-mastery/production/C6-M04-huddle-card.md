# C6-M04 Huddle Card: Natural, Lab-Grown, and Simulants

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Present natural, lab-grown, and moissanite with the SAME energy. A drop in enthusiasm for the lower-price option is bias, even if you never say a discouraging word.

## What Each Option Actually Is
- **Natural** — formed in the Earth over a billion+ years.
- **Lab-grown** — chemically/physically/optically IDENTICAL to natural, made via HPHT or CVD in weeks instead of geological time.
- **Moissanite** — a real, different mineral (silicon carbide), not an inferior diamond. Heat-resistant, resizable.
- **CZ** — zirconium dioxide, inexpensive, can crack/cloud under resizing heat. Best for occasional wear, not decades of daily use.

## Numbers to Use Carefully
Price gap and resale value BOTH vary a lot by source. Say "roughly 60-80% less" and "natural generally holds more resale value" rather than quoting one precise percentage you can't fully back up.

## The Reassuring Detail
Lab-grown diamonds get a permanent laser inscription on the girdle. That's not a flaw — it's proof of origin that travels with the stone forever, even without the certificate.

## Ask, Don't Pitch
"Does the mined origin matter to you specifically, or is getting more size/quality for the budget the priority?" Let their answer decide, not your preference.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Practice presenting all three options for the same ring design. Did your tone stay consistent across all three?
