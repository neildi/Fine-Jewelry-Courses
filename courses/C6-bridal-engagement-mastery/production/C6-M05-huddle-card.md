# C6-M05 Huddle Card: Metals and Settings

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Higher karat gold ≠ more durable. 14k is actually the hardest common option. Richer color always trades off against softness.

## Metal Cheat Sheet
- **14k gold** — hardest, most durable daily-wear option.
- **18k gold** — richer color, a bit softer. Standard for high-end pieces.
- **Platinum** — displaces instead of scratching away; permanent white color, NEVER needs replating.
- **White gold** — rhodium-plated over a yellow-toned alloy; needs replating every 1-3 years. SAY THIS AT THE SALE, not after.
- **Nickel-safe options** — titanium, palladium, platinum, sterling silver, 18k+ yellow gold.

## Setting Cheat Sheet
- **Prong** — max brilliance, but exposed tips wear invisibly. Check every 6-12 months.
- **Bezel** — best security, no exposed tips. Best for hands-on lifestyles (nurses, chefs, tradespeople).
- **Pavé/channel** — band sparkle; channel protects individual stones slightly better.
- **Halo** — bigger look; shared-prong halos show less metal, bezel halos protect better.

## The Skill
Listen for lifestyle details clients offer naturally ("I work with my hands," "I always catch rings on things") and match the setting to THAT, not just what looks best on the case.

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Have you set expectations about white gold replating at the point of sale recently? How did the client react?
