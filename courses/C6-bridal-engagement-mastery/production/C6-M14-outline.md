- Course: C6 - Bridal & Engagement Mastery
- Module: 14 of 14 (Capstone)
- Persona: P3
- Working title: Selling Bridal in Luxury Hotel and Private Appointment Settings (Capstone)

## Production Notes
- Hotel-suite trunk show format and specific practical details (suite rental commonly $2,000+/night at a five-star property; core-client invitation model; venue-selection criteria emphasizing discretion and an attentive front desk) are sourced from Interweave's detailed practitioner guide, "Net Profits: Selling Jewelry at a Private Hotel Showing" — a specifically jewelry-trade-focused source describing this exact practice.
- The formal hotel-brand private-viewing partnership model (e.g., a luxury hotel partnering with a jewelry specialist for in-suite guest viewings, with advance-notice curation) is sourced from JCK's reporting on the Ritz-Carlton Central Park/Sotheby's partnership — a specific, named, real-world example of this exact format.
- By-appointment private showroom/atelier characteristics (NDA-protected service, appointment-only consultations, direct-access/no-gatekeeper positioning) are corroborated across Nathan Jewellers' "Atelier Privé" concierge page and multiple luxury-concierge service providers (AC Private, Private Sales Club, By Appointment), all independently describing a highly similar service model for high-net-worth and high-profile clients.
- Discretion practices (NDAs before consultation, need-to-know information control, unmarked/discreet logistics) are corroborated across multiple luxury-service-adjacent sources describing this same discretion protocol in analogous high-end service contexts (Royal Moving & Storage's celebrity-moving pages, Discreet Pest and Wildlife Services' UHNW page, Air Fresh Marketing's luxury brand activation guidance) — these are NOT jewelry-specific sources, but are used here only to corroborate the general, cross-industry discretion protocol pattern (NDA-first, need-to-know, vetted/background-checked staff) that also appears in the jewelry-specific atelier sources (Nathan Jewellers, AC Private).
- Off-site insurance and chain-of-custody considerations are sourced from ABP Insurance Agency's jewelry-store-insurance guide, which specifically addresses off-premises/off-site show coverage requirements and variability by policy.
- The whole-course synthesis in section 5 is this module's own original integration, explicitly cross-referencing prior modules' established content rather than introducing new externally-sourced claims.
- The worked scenario and practice exercise are ORIGINAL illustrative syntheses, not externally sourced cases.
