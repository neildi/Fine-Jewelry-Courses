# Huddle Card: Capstone

**One headline:** Mastery is nine modules run as one performance — discovery, guidance, honesty, silence, and follow-up — so the couple feels they decided, tells the story, and comes back.

**3 key points**
1. The appointment runs book-and-prep → discover → shape/setting/metal → lab-grown → budget → try-on → couple → sizing/timeline → second sale, with no visible seams.
2. The assessment is a role-play (scored on the behaviors, not a script) plus a bridal conversion log (outcome, the moment, what worked, what to change).
3. Keep the three references close — JCK for the market, GIA for the stones, NDC for the diamond story — and let honest "lost" entries teach.

**1 script to practice today**

"Here's the plan — I'll learn what you're looking for, show you shapes and settings, and we'll settle on a budget that works. Does that sound good?"

**1 question to ask the team**

"Which transition in our appointments feels most scripted — budget-into-try-on is the usual one — and how do we practice it until it disappears?"
