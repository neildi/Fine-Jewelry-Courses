# C6-M07 Huddle Card: Custom Design and CAD Workflow

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** CAD sign-off LOCKS the file. Once a client approves, prong height, band profile, metal weight, and engraving become fixed — further changes mean added cost and time, not a quick fix.

## The Five Stages
1. Consultation & design brief
2. CAD design & rendering
3. Revision rounds (usually 1-2)
4. Sign-off → production (casting, setting, finishing)
5. Final inspection & delivery

## The CAD Review Order (Before Presenting for Approval)
Check in THIS order, not face-up first:
1. Stone size/shape vs. actual order
2. Proportions vs. ring size
3. Prong placement/count vs. stone shape
4. Metal weight vs. quote
5. Seating depth (cross-section view)
6. Shank taper/gallery clearance (underside view)
7. Engraving space
8. THEN the face-up aesthetic view

## Timeline Talk Track
"Most custom rings take 4 to 6 weeks, sometimes up to 12 for complex designs. Let's build in time for at least one round of revisions, especially if you have a specific date in mind."

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Next time you present a CAD file, try walking the client through the technical checklist out loud before asking "how does it look?" Notice if it changes what they catch.
