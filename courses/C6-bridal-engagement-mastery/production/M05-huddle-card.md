# Huddle Card: Budget Conversations That Keep the Romance

**One headline:** Ask about budget early, in your normal voice, and treat every number with dignity — the budget is a tool for building the best ring, not a secret to be whispered about.

**3 key points**
1. Ask early and frame it as helpful: "have you landed on a range? It helps me show you the best options in that range."
2. Run the trade-off triage with two money-saving moves: a slightly warmer stone disappears in yellow/rose gold, and eye-clean beats paying for a flawless grade on paper.
3. If the dream ring is out of range, say it kindly and offer the two honest paths: a different stone now, or the upgrade plan (that's the second sale — Module 9).

**1 script to practice today**

"Before we get into the fun part — have you two landed on a range you want to stay within? It just helps me show you the best options in that range instead of guessing."

**1 question to ask the team**

"What's the most awkward budget moment we've each had on the floor, and what's the one phrasing we'll all use so it stops being awkward?"
