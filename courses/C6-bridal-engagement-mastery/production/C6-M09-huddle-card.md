# C6-M09 Huddle Card: Wedding Bands and Stacking

## 5-Minute Pre-Shift Discussion

**The one idea to remember:** Plant the seed at the ENGAGEMENT RING sale. Mention "come back about 6 months before the wedding for bands" — don't wait for the client to think of it themselves.

## Contour vs. Straight — The Quick Rule
- Halo, three-stone, vintage/side-detail rings → recommend a CONTOURED band (avoids the gap)
- Simple solitaire → either works, client's choice

## Metal Matching
- Match metal type for bands worn on the same finger (wear + possible corrosion)
- Mixing metals on purpose? Use a spacer band, don't let them touch directly

## Stacking Cheat Sheet
- Vary widths (don't stack identical bands)
- Max 2 metal tones per hand
- Spacer band between different hardness/metal types
- Anchor the stack around the engagement ring first

## Timeline by Customization
- In-stock, simple: 3-4 months out
- Engraving/light customization: ~6 months out
- Fully custom/contour-matched: 8 months to a year out (same logic as Module 7's CAD timeline)

## Today's Discussion Prompt
Next engagement ring sale, practice mentioning the wedding-band timeline before the client asks — even just one sentence.
