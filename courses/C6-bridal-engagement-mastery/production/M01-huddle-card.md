# Huddle Card: The Modern Bridal Buyer's Journey

**One headline:** The bridal buyer has already researched before they meet you — your job is to find where they are in that journey and meet them there, not re-teach the 4Cs.

**3 key points**
1. Bridal is ~35% (median) of a jeweler's business, and the buyer now shops in a loop: research online, visit, re-research, then return to buy.
2. Three journeys walk in: the solo researcher (wants education + a next step), the couple together (wants to pressure-test what they've agreed), and the family-in-tow (has an advisor with opinions).
3. When they bring a wrong fact, correct the fact and keep their dignity: "that was the rule for X — here's where it bends for Y."

**1 script to practice today**

Associate: "Welcome in — are you starting fresh today, or have you been looking at rings online already? … You've done the hard part. Let me just untangle a couple of things you've probably seen conflicting advice on."

**1 question to ask the team**

"What's the most common misconception our bridal clients walk in with, and what's our one-sentence, dignity-preserving correction for it?"
