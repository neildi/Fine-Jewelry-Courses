# Approved Media Sources — Verification Record

**Scope:** repo-wide standing record, maintained under Part C of the Prompt Kit.

This complements the per-module `production/*-media-manifest.md` files rather than replacing them. Manifests record what a given article uses; this file records **which sources are approved at all, on what licence terms, and which are barred** — so the same licensing question is not re-litigated across the remaining articles in the 130-article catalog.

An item may only enter a `<media>` manifest after its source appears here with a completed C5 check.

### Scope caveat — needs a human decision before first re-host

The terms restrict use to **"the promotion of Natural diamonds."** Our platform is commercial sales training, not consumer promotion. Two readings are available:

- **Permissive reading:** training retail associates to sell natural diamonds accurately is promotion of natural diamonds, and NDC explicitly targets these assets at the trade and encourages in-store use.
- **Strict reading:** a paid training product is a different use than a retailer's own consumer-facing channels, and "may not be sold, sublicensed, transferred" could be read to cover embedding assets in a course that is sold.

The terms also prohibit copying "without the written consent of the Category Marketing Team."

**Decision recorded:** until a human obtains written confirmation from NDC, this project uses NDC assets as **link-out only**, never `licensed-download`. Link-out requires no licence grant and is unambiguously safe. NDC provides a contact address for special requests: info@diamondlearningcenter.org.

**Action for the reviewer:** email NDC's Category Marketing Team describing the training-platform use and requesting written consent for `licensed-download`. If granted, file the email per C3 and this record can be upgraded.

## GAP RESOLUTION LOG — C1 M01/M02 media requests, closed 2026-09-06

Two placeholders that were blocking publication of C1 M01 and M02 were resolved with Tier 2 GIA sources found on a second search pass. No commissioned photography or retailer login was needed. Retained here because the reasoning generalises: when a Tier 1 asset library does not carry the visual you need, GIA's public blog is often the fastest legitimate substitute.

### C1-M01-I1 — clarity grade comparison at 10x — RESOLVED

**Source:** "VVS Diamond versus VS Diamond: What's the Difference in Diamond Clarity?" — https://4cs.gia.edu/en-us/blog/vvs-diamond-versus-vs-diamond/ (GIA, published 2023-08-08, verified 2026-09-06)

Better than the original request. The article contains:

- Two 1.00 ct, G colour, Excellent cut round brilliants photographed side by side — VVS1 and VS2 — with the reader invited to spot the difference. Courtesy Blue Nile.
- Individual grade-setting photographs for VVS1 (internal graining), VVS2 (feather), VS1 (feather under table) and VS2 (feather near table), each with the grade-setting inclusion marked in a red box. Photos credited to GIA and Shane F. McClure/GIA.
- The teaching point the original request could not have delivered: the VVS2 and VS1 examples show *the same type of inclusion* producing different grades because of **location** — hidden by a prong versus under the table.
- The five grading factors (size, number, location, relief, nature), now quoted into M01 as a table.
- Photographs of a pinpoint with crystal, and of clouds. Photos: John I. Koivula/GIA.

**Use type:** link-out. GIA photographs carry per-image credits; we do not re-host.

**Note:** the pair is VVS1/VS2 rather than the VS1/SI1 originally specified. This is an improvement for teaching purposes — GIA supplies the price context (a 1.00 ct D VVS1 can be 40% more than a VS1 of the same weight and colour) and the stones are matched on every other variable. M01 was updated to teach from the grades GIA actually photographed rather than restating the original request.

### C1-M02-I1 — girdle laser inscription — RESOLVED

**Primary source:** "How to Protect Your Diamond Engagement Ring", section 4 — https://4cs.gia.edu/en-us/blog/how-to-protect-your-diamond-engagement-ring/ (GIA, published 2017-09-26, verified 2026-09-06)

**Supporting source:** "Diamond Engagement Ring Terms" — https://4cs.gia.edu/en-us/blog/guide-diamond-engagement-ring-terms/ (GIA, published 2015-10-07, verified 2026-09-06)

Facts newly available to the articles, all from GIA:

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Inscription applied **automatically** to all diamonds with a GIA Diamond Dossier or Diamond Focus report | Protect Your Diamond §4 |
| **Optional, low-cost additional service** for diamonds with full GIA Diamond Grading Reports | Protect Your Diamond §4 |
| Viewable under 10x magnification with a jeweller's loupe | Protect Your Diamond §4; Engagement Ring Terms |
| Inscribed number can be entered into GIA Report Check | Protect Your Diamond §4 |
| May help police identify a stolen and recovered diamond | Protect Your Diamond §4 |
| Laser inscriptions **do not affect the clarity grade** | Engagement Ring Terms |
| Can be a report number, logo, message, date or symbol; Tiffany & Co. cited as a brand that inscribes | Engagement Ring Terms |
| GIA calls "certificate" a **misnomer** applied to its grading reports; a GIA report is "GIA's opinion about the quality of the diamond represented in the report" | Engagement Ring Terms |

That last row materially strengthened M02's "what not to say" table — the guidance is now GIA's own characterisation rather than our inference.

**Use type:** link-out.

**Residual gap (minor, non-blocking):** neither page provides a high-resolution macro photograph of an inscription that we may re-host. The articles now teach the inscription in words with a link-out, which satisfies the system prompt. If a re-hostable macro is ever wanted for visual polish, GIA's Retailer Support Program remains the route — but it is no longer blocking publication.
